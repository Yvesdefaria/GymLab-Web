# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Spanish-speaking lifters, beginner to intermediate, training 3–5 times a week in commercial gyms. They currently track with notes apps, paper, or memory. Primary job: know whether their training is working, set by set and week by week, and decide what to train before entering the gym.

## Product Purpose

GymLab is a strength-training app that plans routines, logs each set while training, and shows progress by exercise, muscle group, and period. It replaces guesswork and scattered notes with one record the lifter can act on. Success for the marketing site: launch interest captured by email before the app stores go live.

## Positioning

A complete Spanish-first training toolkit that works offline and without an account wall: 873 photographed exercises, 9 calculators, 68 routine templates, and progress graphs in one app.

## Operating Context

The site is the pre-launch marketing landing plus legal pages for `gymlab.dev`. Visitors arrive from direct links and word of mouth; there is no waitlist backend, so the only conversion is an email to `gymlab@gymlab.dev`. The app is Android-first with iOS planned; store badges are status signals. All site content is neutral/professional Spanish; code and docs are English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed product facts: 873 exercises with photos filterable by muscle, equipment, and category; 9 calculators (BMI, calories/macros, 1RM, body fat, water, measurements); 68 routine templates plus a routine builder; live session logging with an integrated rest timer; progress graphs by exercise, muscle group, and period; progress photos; daily steps, Android widgets, and device sync; achievements and streaks. No account is required to train; history stays on the device and is not uploaded to third-party servers. Pricing is undecided. Launch date is undecided. Legal copy states the app is not a medical device.

## Brand Commitments

Name: GymLab. The brand system is fixed by the repository: dark gold palette (`#d9b384` accent, `#121214` background), Oswald for display and Barlow for body, self-hosted WOFF2, no palette switcher or day/night toggle on the web. Copy language: neutral/professional Spanish (no voseo or regionalisms). The real app screenshots are the product proof.

## Product Principles

- Show the training record doing its job; proof over adjectives.
- Neutral Spanish, plain and concrete, never hype.
- Local-first is a supporting guarantee, not the pitch.
- Pre-launch honesty: no price, date, testimonial, or rating claims.
- Design for the real scene: a gym with poor signal, a phone in hand, sets to log between efforts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Site content is Spanish and must remain semantic and keyboard-operable. The repository's landing checks include an accessibility pass. No product-specific accommodation requirement was established beyond standard WCAG expectations for a marketing site.
