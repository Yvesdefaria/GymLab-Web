## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The brachialis is not a third biceps head.** Page 5 says "for some, the brachialis counts as the
   third head". It is a separate muscle under the biceps; a neutral grip shifts work to it. House line
   settled in batch 26.
2. **Training does not change breast shape.** Page 7 says women can "improve the shape and tone of the
   breasts" with chest training. Chest training builds the pectorals under the breast; it does not
   lift or reshape the breast itself. Also drop "a developed chest is the sign of a big strong man"
   as the opening frame.
3. **Two chest hubs cannot exist.** `rutinas-pecho` (page 7) is the hub of the chest block; page 10
   is the beginner set of routines and must link up to it instead of repeating the catalogue. Neither
   repeats `anatomia-del-pecho`, `consejos-ganar-musculo-pectoral` or the dumbbell/barbell comparison.
4. **Report athlete programmes as reported.** Page 1 is Lee Haney's programme and page 8 is Arnold's
   dips routine. Keep the numbers; attribute the routine to the athlete, never present it as
   physiology and never invent a quote.
5. **No "destroy the biceps" language.** Page 5's headings ("Destrozad los bíceps", "para hacerlos
   crecer") and its competition framing are out; the content is a set of three techniques plus a
   routine.
6. **Keep the numbers, drop the promises.** Every page carries sets and reps; keep them. Drop any
   promise of centimetres or weeks.
