## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The seated curl stops at full knee flexion, not 90°.** Page 1 makes 90° the terminal range and calls
   it a complete movement in the same paragraph. Full flexion is roughly 130-140°; 90° is shin-vertical,
   a half rep, and the last 40° are the arc the short head of the biceps femoris works through. Keep the
   1-2 s pause and a 2-3 s eccentric.
2. **Not all three hamstrings cross the hip.** Page 1 calls them all biarticular. The short head of the
   biceps femoris crosses the knee only, which is exactly why the seated position is worth a page: hip
   flexion slackens the two biarticular heads and biases the short head. The library already says this
   correctly in `anatomia-femorales`.
3. **The gastrocnemius always assists knee flexion.** Page 1 makes it conditional on dorsiflexion. It is
   the reason the curl never isolates the hamstrings completely; dorsiflexion only increases its share.
4. **The abductor machine is not useless.** Page 2 says activation is "very small" and calls it one of the
   most useless machines. The verdict is inverted: seated abduction is one of the higher gluteus medius
   efforts in a gym. The two honest caveats are that it trains abduction only, and that momentum or a
   forward lean hands the work to the TFL. Sartorius is a weak abductor; the abductors are gluteus
   medius, minimus, TFL and the upper gluteus maximus.
5. **The stretch is not the stop signal in an RDL.** Pages 3 and 6 tell the reader to keep descending
   until the stretch is felt. The stop is the pelvis beginning to tuck and the lumbar spine beginning to
   round. Chasing a stretch is how the back rounds under load, which page 3 warns about two paragraphs
   later. Publish the stop rule, then ration range by it.
6. **The stiff-leg deadlift keeps the bar close too.** Page 6 defines the stiff-leg version by the bar
   being away from the thighs, then spends the page insisting the bar stays 3-4 cm from the legs. Bar
   distance is the fault in both lifts. The real difference is knee flexion and how much of the movement
   comes from the hip versus the spine; both defer to `peso-muerto-piernas-rigidas-barra`.
7. **One RDL dose, reconciled.** The source says 8-12, the hub says 3-4 x 6-10 and `entrenar-femorales`
   says 4 x 12/10/8/6. Publish 3-4 x 6-10 with 2-3 min rest and 1-2 reps in reserve as the strength
   default, and 3 x 8-12 as the size variant when the hinge is already solid. Page 3 uses 3 x 8-12,
   because the dumbbell grip caps the load before the hamstrings do.
8. **The dumbbell lying curl is not a home option.** Page 4 sells it as one, then admits the bell falls
   to the ankles and that a partner is needed to load it. Publish it as the curl for when there is no
   machine, and let the body of the page explain why the machine wins: loading is capped by balance and
   by a bell pinched between the soles, so progression stops long before the hamstring does.
9. **The overhead squat is a screen, not a waist builder.** Page 5 sells it as exceptional strength for
   the waist and low back. The limiter is shoulder flexion, ankle and thoracic mobility. Publish it as a
   one-rep-per-side screen and a warm-up or shoulder-block drill. Drop the invented 5 x 10-per-arm
   routine and the fake "routine" bullet list.
10. **The chest dip needs a forward lean and a depth rule.** Page 7 says the torso should be "as upright
    as possible" and then recommends a forward lean. The chest version needs about 30° of lean. Nothing
    in the archive gives depth: stop when the shoulder reaches elbow height or at the first anterior
    pinch. Page 7 cross-links `fondos-pecho-triceps` (Arnold routine) and `fondos` (triceps) and owns
    only the chest-specific setup and progression.
11. **There is no inner and outer pectoral.** Page 8 says a close grip builds the inner chest and a wide
    grip the outer. Two heads, clavicular and sternocostal; grip width changes the elbow moment arm and
    the range, not the fibres. Page 9 repeats it for the fly. Both die.
12. **The bar does not travel only vertically.** Page 8 states it and the library contradicts it in
    `mejorar-fuerza-press-de-banca`. The bar rises on a slight diagonal toward the shoulder joint.
13. **The incline ceiling is about 30°.** Page 10 gives 30-45°; 45° is a shoulder press in the scapular
    plane. Publish 30°, 40° at most, consistent with `ejercicios-pecho` and
    `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho`.
14. **The fly is defined by the line of pull, not the bench.** Page 9 titles itself incline and describes
    a flat bench in the setup. The incline version needs low pulleys pulling up and back, the one
    direction a dumbbell fly cannot provide. Drop the crossed wrists.
15. **Every row states its dose.** Where the archive gives none, publish one recommendation labelled as
    the page's. Defaults: 3 x 8-12 for compounds, 2-3 x 12-15 for isolation, 60-90 s rest, add load when
    the last set is clean.
16. **No page invents a diagnosis.** Page 2's spine-harm claim and page 1's stability-and-prevention
    promise die. A machine has a setup and a range; it does not need a scare.

## Per page

### 1. Seated leg curl

- Setup: knee joint aligned with the machine axis, pad above the ankles, thigh pad snug, back against the
  pad. Full range to about 130-140°, 1-2 s pause, 2-3 s eccentric. 3 x 10-15, 60-90 s.
- Explain why hip flexion biases the short head, and that the gastrocnemius always assists. Correct the
  "all biarticular" and "90° is complete" lines. Drop the stability promise and the bodybuilding.com
  credit. Keep the toe-direction observation as an observation, not a diagnosis.
- Link `anatomia-femorales`, `femoral-maquina-tumbado-2`, `femoral-maquina-de-pie-2`,
  `flexion-femoral-acostado-con-mancuerna`, `peso-muerto-rumano`, `entrenar-femorales`, `rutinas-pierna`.

### 2. Hip abduction machine

- The counter-page. Seated abduction is a legitimate gluteus medius lift; lead with that, then the two
  caveats: it trains abduction only, and momentum or a forward lean shifts work to the TFL. Upright
  torso, 1 s pause at end range, 3 x 12-20, 60 s. Progression by load and by tempo.
- Correct the muscle list and drop "very small activation", the spine-harm claim and "most useless
  machine". No scare, no hype.
- Link `anatomia-de-la-cadera-gluteos`, `beneficios-hip-thrust`,
  `como-calentar-para-tu-entrenamiento-de-gluteos`, `rutinas-pierna`, `volumen-de-entrenamiento`,
  `principio-adaptacion-sobrecarga-progresiva`.

### 3. Dumbbell Romanian deadlift

- The version you can learn alone. Subject: hand path, grip ceiling and the stop rule. Dumbbells close
  to the legs over midfoot, soft knees, hips back, stop when the pelvis begins to tuck. 3 x 8-12,
  90-120 s; straps only when grip is the limiter.
- Fix the stretch cue, keep the moment-arm point, drop the first-person testimonial.
- Link `peso-muerto-rumano`, `peso-muerto-piernas-rigidas-mancuernas`,
  `peso-muerto-piernas-rigidas-barra`, `entrenar-femorales`, `anatomia-femorales`,
  `anatomia-de-la-cadera-gluteos`, `buenos-dias-barra-de-pie`.

### 4. Dumbbell lying leg curl

- Publish it as the curl for when there is no machine, and explain why the machine wins: balance and a
  bell pinched between the soles cap the load. 1 s squeeze, calves vertical as the top, lower until the
  calves approach parallel, pelvis down. 3 x 10-15 per leg; progression by ankle weight or band, not by
  a heavier bell.
- Drop the home-option promise and the claim that it removes lumbar and glute stress: the hip extensors
  hold the pelvis and the erectors are what fail when the bell is heavy.
- Link `femoral-maquina-sentado-2`, `femoral-maquina-tumbado-2`, `femoral-maquina-de-pie-2`,
  `anatomia-femorales`, `entrenar-femorales`, `rutinas-pierna`.

### 5. Overhead dumbbell squat

- A one-rep-per-side screen for ankle and thoracic restriction, then a warm-up or shoulder-block drill.
  Feet slightly wider than the shoulders, arms locked overhead, ribs down, descend only as far as the
  position holds. 2-3 x 5-8 per side light, or one rep per side as the screen.
- Drop the waist-strength promise, the 5 x 10-per-arm routine and the fake routine list. Keep the
  shoulder-flexion and thoracic-extension explanation. It is not a hamstring or glute lift: do not link
  it into that cluster.
- Link `ejercicios-pierna`, `sentadilla-sin-lesiones`, `sentadilla-mirada-al-frente`, `rutinas-hombros`,
  `rutina-para-trabajar-tus-hombros-de-forma-completa`, `anatomia-del-cuadriceps`.

### 6. Romanian deadlift

- Own the depth rule: stop when the pelvis tucks and the spine starts to round, then ration range by
  that signal instead of by a height. 3-4 x 6-10, 2-3 min, 1-2 reps in reserve as the strength default;
  3 x 8-12 as the size variant. Bar on the thighs and shins, shoulders over the bar, lats set.
- Fix the stiff-leg contradiction, keep the cervical cue and the Smith-machine note, drop the
  "bar away from the thighs" definition and the 8-12-only dose.
- Link `peso-muerto-rumano-mancuernas`, `peso-muerto-piernas-rigidas-barra`,
  `peso-muerto-piernas-rigidas-mancuernas`, `peso-muerto-sumo`, `buenos-dias-barra-de-pie`,
  `entrenar-femorales`, `rutina-de-femorales`, `anatomia-femorales`,
  `anatomia-de-la-cadera-gluteos`, `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`.

### 7. Chest dips

- The chest-specific setup: about 30° of forward lean, elbows out to roughly 45°, legs slightly forward.
  Depth rule: stop when the shoulder reaches elbow height or at the first anterior pinch. 1 s pause at
  the top, controlled descent, 3 x 8-12 bodyweight, belt or vest when 12 are clean, 90 s. Assisted
  machine as the regression; step-assisted negatives only after the depth rule.
- Resolve the "as upright as possible" contradiction. This page owns setup, depth and progression; the
  Arnold routine stays in `fondos-pecho-triceps` and the triceps version in `fondos`.
- Link `fondos-pecho-triceps`, `fondos`, `fondos-entre-bancos`, `fondos-maquina-triceps`,
  `ejercicios-pecho`, `press-banca-barra`, `anatomia-del-pecho`,
  `mejorar-fuerza-press-de-banca`.

### 8. Barbell bench press

- The flagship page the library lacks. Separate three jobs: strength (45° elbows, bar to the lower
  chest, leg drive, 4-5 x 3-5, 3 min), size (controlled eccentric, 3-4 x 6-10, 2 min) and shoulder
  safety (elbow angle, depth, no flaring to 90°). Scapular retraction, spotter or safeties, feet planted.
- Kill the inner/outer pectoral claim and the 10 % grip study. Correct the vertical-bar line to a slight
  diagonal toward the shoulder. Keep the thumbless-grip warning: it is the best-argued section in the
  source. 800-1,100 words.
- Link `mejorar-fuerza-press-de-banca`, `press-de-banca-mas-efectivo-con-mancuernas-o-con-barra`,
  `press-banca-agarre-cerrado`, `rutina-de-pecho-alternativa-al-press-de-banca`, `ejercicios-pecho`,
  `anatomia-del-pecho`, `rutinas-pecho`, `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`, `press-inclinado-multipower`,
  `aperturas-inclinadas-polea`, `fondos-pecho`.

### 9. Incline cable fly

- The thesis: the bench is not the variable, the line of pull is. Low pulleys on an incline bench pull
  up and back, the direction a dumbbell fly cannot provide. Setup with the bench at 30°, a small forward
  step for constant tension, elbows slightly bent and fixed, hands meet without crossing, 1 s
  contraction, 3 x 12-15, 60-90 s.
- Fix the flat-bench setup, drop the inner/outer promise and the crossed wrists.
- Link `maquinas-pesos-libres-al-entrenar-el-pecho`, `ejercicios-pecho`, `ejercicios-en-polea-ventajas`,
  `anatomia-del-pecho`, `consejos-ganar-musculo-pectoral`, `press-inclinado-multipower`,
  `press-banca-barra`, `rutinas-pecho`.

### 10. Smith machine incline press

- What the guided bar buys and what it costs: solo training near failure and a fixed arc the shoulder
  does not want. Bench at 30°, bar over the upper chest, feet planted, safeties set, wrists stacked.
  3-4 x 8-12, 90-120 s; 2-3 s eccentric and the last set taken to the safeties.
- Correct 30-45° to the 30° ceiling. Drop the clickbait title, "one of the best options" and the
  bodybuilding.com credit. Replace the archive's 136 words with real setup, errors, dose and programming.
- Link `ejercicios-pecho`, `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho`,
  `multipower-para-superar-puntos-de-estancamiento`, `rutina-con-multipower`, `press-banca-barra`,
  `press-banca-agarre-cerrado-en-multipower`, `rutinas-pecho`,
