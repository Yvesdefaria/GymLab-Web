## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The machine fixes the humerus; it does not isolate the biceps.** Page 1 claims "100 % isolation"
   and no other muscle. The brachialis and brachioradialis cross the elbow and always assist. The
   machine removes shoulder substitution and makes the load repeatable, which is strictness, not
   exclusivity.
2. **The cable keeps a constant line, not constant tension.** Page 2 says tension is constant through
   every phase. The moment arm still shrinks as the forearm approaches vertical; the cable is flatter
   than a dumbbell, not constant. State the real edge: the direction of resistance does not change
   while the hand rotates, which is why the one-arm version exists.
3. **Alternating is the teaching curl.** Page 3 claims the free arm guarantees the deltoids are not
   involved and profits from a shorter range. Pinning the elbow changes the humerus, not the elbow's
   range; the free arm resets and exposes torso swing. Nothing else.
4. **The concentration curl does not target the long head.** Pages 4 and 6 invert the house line. The
   concentration curl is a supinated curl with the humerus fixed; the neutral "hammer" variant is a
   different exercise, not a long-head trick. Shoulder angle decides the head; grip chooses biceps
   versus brachialis share.
5. **The incline curl stretches the long head at its origin.** Page 5 explains the benefit as "more
   contraction" because the insertion is stretched, then tells the reader to start with bent elbows,
   which deletes the exercise. The mechanism is the shoulder extension at the origin and training at
   long muscle length; keep the bottom stretched and lower the load.
6. **The biceps supinates the forearm, not the wrist.** Page 5 calls it a "strong wrist supinator".
   Fix the term.
7. **The hammer curl is the thickness and elbow-friendly curl.** Page 6 calls it a long-head
   exercise. It is the neutral-grip option that shifts work to the brachialis and brachioradialis and
   keeps working when the palm-up position irritates the elbow.
8. **The preacher hammer is a rare combination, priced honestly.** Page 7 says the pad fixes the
   range; it fixes the humerus. Neutral grip plus a fixed humerus leaves the brachialis with an
   anchored elbow, and the load will be lower than either parent.
9. **The seated curl is not a hip or knee exercise.** Page 8 sells it as ideal for hip and knee
   problems; a standing curl does not load those joints either. The real difference is that sitting
   removes leg drive and torso swing, so it measures what you actually curl.
10. **Pre-exhaustion is inverted in page 9.** Doing the preacher after curls is ordering by load, not
    pre-exhaustion. The page is the hub of the preacher family; fix the term and cut the padding.
11. **The Zottman's value is the pronated eccentric.** Page 10 gives no execution and recommends the
    Scott version as "more isolation", which is the least forgiving place for the exercise. Publish
    the rotation point (top), the supinated concentric and the pronated eccentric, and treat it as a
    brachioradialis exercise wearing a biceps costume.
12. **No page shortens the range as a feature.** Pages 7 and 9 tell the reader to stop short of elbow
    extension. `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom` already says isolation movements are trained through
    the full range. Control the bottom; do not cut it.
13. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: 3 x 8-12 for free-weight curls (90 s), 3 x 10-12 for
    hammer and cable (90 s), 3 x 12-15 for the preacher machine (60-90 s), 3 x 8-10 for the Zottman.
    The archive gives none on most pages; every dose is this batch's own recommendation and says so
    once.

## Per page

### 1. Preacher curl machine

- Seat height so the armpits sit on the pad, upper arms on the pad, grip the handles, curl to just
  short of vertical, control the descent. 3 x 12-15, 60-90 s, as the last movement of the session.
- Thesis: the machine is the measurable preacher. Same shoulder position as the dumbbell Scott, with a
  pin instead of plates, useful when the free-weight version irritates the elbow or when the load must
  be repeatable. Kill "100 % isolation" and the rehab line.
- Link `curl-predicador-mancuernas-a-dos-manos`, `curl-predicador-mancuernas-agarre-martillo`,
  `curl-scott-barraz`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rutinas-biceps`, `biceps-grandes`.

### 2. Standing cable curl

- Low pulley, supinated handle, one step back so the stack lifts, elbows pinned, curl to the top,
  control the return. 3 x 10-12, 90 s. One-arm version: stand far enough that the arm starts fully
  extended, rotate the wrist as the hand rises.
- Fix the "constant tension" overstatement and the image-as-heading. Thesis: the line of pull does not
  change while the hand rotates, which no dumbbell can do.
- Link `curl-polea-alta-tumbado-banco`, `ejercicios-en-polea-ventajas`, `curl-barra`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rutinas-biceps`, `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`.

### 3. Alternating dumbbell curl

- Standing, palms neutral at the bottom, supinate as the hand rises, alternate arms, elbows still.
  3 x 10-12, 90 s. The teaching curl: the free arm resets, torso swing becomes visible.
- Kill the deltoid-exclusion claim and the shorter-range rationale. Own supination as the biceps'
  second job.
- Link `curl-mancuernas-sentado-adosmanos`, `curl-barra`, `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`,
  `tipos-agarre`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `biceps-grandes`.

### 4. Concentration curl

- Seated, elbow braced against the inner thigh, supinated grip, curl to the top, 1 s, control down.
  3 x 10-12, 90 s. The honest strict-strength test of the biceps.
- Kill the long-head claim and the hammer-as-long-head line. Keep the error list: torso swing and
  elbow off the thigh.
- Link `curl-alterno-mancuerna`, `curl-barra`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`,
  `rutina-de-biceps-tecnica-estricta`, `biceps-grandes`, `consigue-grandes-brazos`.

### 5. Incline dumbbell curl

- Bench at 45 degrees, arms hanging, palms forward, curl without moving the upper arm, 1 s at the top.
  3-4 x 8-12, 90 s. The long head starts under stretch at its origin; the bottom is the point.
- Fix the stretch-versus-contraction explanation, the "wrist supinator" term and the bent-elbow start
  that deletes the exercise. Remove the duplicated variations block.
- Link `curl-mancuernas-sentado-adosmanos`, `curl-barra`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`,
  `ejercicios-biceps`, `biceps-grandes`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 6. Dumbbell hammer curl

- Standing, neutral grip, elbows pinned, curl without rotating the wrist, 1 s at the top. 3 x 10-12,
  90 s. Neutral grip shifts work to the brachialis and brachioradialis and keeps the elbow comfortable.
- Kill the long-head claim. Add the brief variants: cross-body and incline hammer, each one line.
- Link `curl-predicador-mancuernas-agarre-martillo`, `curl-cuerda-agarre-martillo`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `entrenamiento-del-agarre`, `ejercicios-antebrazo`,
  `rutina-de-antebrazos`.

### 7. Preacher hammer curl

- Preacher bench, neutral grip, upper arms on the pad, curl to just short of vertical, control the
  return. 3 x 10-12, 90 s. Neutral grip plus a fixed humerus leaves the brachialis with an anchored
  elbow; expect less load than either parent.
- Fix "fixed range", the cut range and the long-head framing.
- Link `curl-predicador-mancuernas-a-dos-manos`, `curl-maquina-predicador`,
  `curl-mancuernas-agarre-martillo`, `curl-scott-barraz`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`,
  `rutina-de-ejercicios-para-desarrollar-los-biceps`.

### 8. Seated dumbbell curl

- Seated on a bench without backrest or with a light one, palms forward from the bottom, elbows at the
  sides, curl, control down. 3 x 10-12, 90 s. The seated reference: no leg drive, no torso swing.
- Kill the hip and knee medical framing and the injury-risk line. Remove the video headings.
- Link `curl-alterno-mancuerna`, `curl-mancuernas-banco-inclinado-a-dos-manos`, `curl-barra`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rutinas-biceps`, `aumentar-biceps`.

### 9. Two-arm preacher dumbbell curl

- Preacher bench, a dumbbell in each hand, upper arms on the pad, curl both, 1-2 s near the top,
  control down. 3 x 8-12, 90 s. The hub of the preacher family: each arm finds its own path and the
  semi-supinated grip the bar cannot offer.
- Fix the inverted pre-exhaustion term, cut the padding and the emoji, remove the conclusion. Link the
  machine and hammer variants instead of repeating them.
- Link `curl-maquina-predicador`, `curl-predicador-mancuernas-agarre-martillo`, `curl-scott-barraz`,
  `curl-arana`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 10. Zottman curl

- Standing, supinated curl up, rotate to pronated at the top, lower with a pronated grip slowly,
  rotate back at the bottom. 3 x 8-10, 90 s. The only common curl with a pronated eccentric: a
  brachioradialis exercise wearing a biceps costume.
- Kill "exotic", the Scott recommendation and the missing execution. Give the rotation point and the
  tempo.
- Link `curl-alterno-mancuerna`, `curl-mancuernas-agarre-martillo`, `entrenamiento-del-agarre`,
  `ejercicios-antebrazo`, `rutina-de-antebrazos`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`.
