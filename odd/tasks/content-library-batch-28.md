## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **Breathing.** Page 1 tells the reader to hold the breath on the way up and claims that exhaling
   at the top shortens the range, costs force and raises injury risk. The published hub says exhale
   on the concentric. House line: exhale as the rib cage closes; a short brace before the rep is
   fine, and a held breath under a heavy load is a bracing tool, not a rule for a bodyweight crunch.
   Never claim that exhaling restricts the range or injures the spine.
2. **Rotation while the spine is flexed.** Page 1 warns that twisting a flexed spine can damage
   discs, while pages 6 and 10 prescribe exactly that. House line: rotating the ribs over a light
   load through a small range is what these exercises are; the risk lives in heavy load, a fixed
   pelvis and a long lever at the end of the range. Keep both exercises, state the load rule, drop
   the certainty about damaged discs.
3. **The rectus is one muscle** (settled in batch 26). Page 1 sells "abdominales superiores", page
   10 mentions "abdominales superiores" in the centre rep. Rewrite: the crunch shortens the distance
   between the sternum and the pelvis; the label describes where the work is felt, not a separate
   muscle.
4. **Gaze and neck.** Page 1 says not to keep looking at the ceiling, while the hub says the gaze
   goes forward and up. Resolve: the neck stays neutral and the gaze follows the rib cage; never
   crank the chin or pull the head with the hands.
5. **Side bend is not rotation.** Page 7 is titled "torsión lateral" but describes lateral flexion.
   Say what it is: the trunk bends sideways; rotation belongs to pages 3, 6 and 10.
6. **The waist-width argument.** Page 7 (and page 9 by implication) warns that heavy low-rep side
   bends widen the waist and prescribes about 25 reps. That is a hypertrophy consequence, not a
   danger: loaded side bends grow the obliques and a thicker waist is what growth looks like. Keep
   the 25-rep prescription as the archive's choice for a narrow-waist goal and say so plainly.
7. **No unverified study.** Page 3 cites an American Council on Exercise ranking. The library does
   not carry study claims; drop it and justify the exercise by its mechanics: one rep combines
   trunk flexion, rotation and hip flexion.
8. **Weak obliques and posture.** Page 4 states that weak obliques cause bad posture and back
   problems. Soften: the obliques contribute to trunk control; weakness is one factor among several,
   not a diagnosis.

## Editorial decisions per page

### 1. Encogimientos abdominales en suelo

The base exercise of the block; it has to be the cleanest page of the ten.

- Keep: feet flat, knees bent, hands behind the head with the elbows open, chin off the chest, round
  the upper back, one-second hold, lower until the shoulders rest, 5-7.5 cm of travel is all the
  range there is, and the progressions (arms overhead, plate on the chest, feet on a bench or
  against the wall).
- Fix the breathing (conflict 1) and the gaze (conflict 4). Add what the archive leaves implicit:
  the lower back stays down, the movement stops when the shoulders leave the floor, and the hands
  never pull.
- Fix the upper/lower claim: the crunch closes the rib-pelvis distance; there is no separate upper
  muscle.
- Delete: the bodybuilding.com credit, the inline images, the vosotros, the "drama" tone.
- Links: `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `encogimientos-maquina`,
  `rutina-abdominales-ab`.

### 2. Encogimientos en polea de pie

- Keep: rope on the high pulley, back to the stack, slight forward lean, neutral grip with the
  hands locked at the ears, chin off the chest, wide stance, knees soft, fold at the waist, elbows
  toward the knees, one-second hold, and the 15-rep rule before adding a plate.
- Add: standing versus kneeling (standing is easier to cheat, kneeling removes momentum), the
  errors (pulling with the arms, hips travelling back, half range), and the loading logic.
- Delete the vosotros.
- Links: `encogimientos-polea-2`, `rutina-abdominales-para-culturismo`,
  `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 3. Bicicleta abdominal

- Keep: the setup (legs at 90 degrees, shins parallel to the floor, hands behind the head), the
  execution (shoulder off the floor, cross the elbow towards the opposite knee, extend the other
  leg), and the tempo advice (moderate, not fast).
- Drop the study (conflict 7). Explain the exercise instead: it chains trunk flexion, rotation and
  hip flexion, which is why it belongs in most routines.
- Fix: "despegar la paletilla" in the singular; both shoulders alternate.
- Links: `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`,
  `encogimientos-laterales-dedos-tobillos`, `rutina-abdominales-ab`.

### 4. Encogimientos laterales dedos a los tobillos

- Keep: lying supine, both feet flat and slightly apart, arms at the sides, reach one hand to the
  heel on the same side, one rep is left plus right, return to the start between reps, and the
  stable triangle of the feet.
- Fix the anatomy claims: the obliques flex the trunk sideways and rotate it; they are not a lever,
  and weakness is one factor in trunk control, not the cause of back problems.
- Add: the reach comes from the ribs, not the arm; the hips do not rock; a light lift of the back
  increases tension.
- Links: `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `encogimientos-oblicuos`,
  `rutina-abdominales-ab`.

### 5. Encogimientos para oblicuos

- Keep: lying on one side with the legs stacked and slightly bent, the head supported, the free arm
  crossing the chest or resting on the floor, the crunch staying in the lateral plane, both legs
  lifting a few centimetres, one-second hold, and the controlled descent.
- Clarify the legs: lifting both legs is what separates this from a basic side crunch; the
  beginner version keeps the feet down.
- Fix: "ningún lado descansa" means the torso does not rest between sides, not the legs.
- Links: `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `elevaciones-piernas-oblicuos-tumbado`,
  `encogimientos-laterales-dedos-tobillos`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 6. Encogimientos para oblicuos en banco declinado

- Keep: the angle as the difficulty dial, feet anchored, one hand at the ear and the other across
  the chest or on the hip, lift and rotate until the elbow approaches the opposite knee, one second
  at the top, all reps on one side then the other.
- Add the load rule from conflict 2: light load, small range, rotate with the ribs. State that this
  is not the exercise to load heavy.
- Add the errors: pulling the neck, rotating with the arms only, hips sliding, half range.
- Links: `encogimientos-banco-inclinado`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `encogimientos-oblicuos-polea`.

### 7. Encogimiento lateral con mancuerna de pie

- Keep: flat back, head neutral, one dumbbell, the other hand behind the head or on the waist, knees
  soft, feet at shoulder width, bend sideways towards the weight, control the descent, contract on
  the way up, one-second hold, everything in the lateral plane, and the 25-rep prescription.
- Fix the name and the mechanism (conflict 5): lateral flexion, not rotation.
- Reframe the waist warning (conflict 6).
- Links: `inclinacion-lateral-polea`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `encogimientos-oblicuos`.

### 8. Elevaciones de piernas para oblicuos tumbado

- Keep: side-lying with the body extended and a slight hip and knee bend, one foot stacked on the
  other, the head supported with the elbow in line with the torso, the free hand on the hip, the
  crunch and the straight-leg raise meeting at the top, one-second hold, controlled descent.
- Add: ankle weights and the rest-pause trick (shorter rest to keep the obliques under tension).
- Add the errors: pulling the head, rolling onto the back, swinging the leg.
- Links: `encogimientos-oblicuos`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `encogimientos-laterales-dedos-tobillos`.

### 9. Inclinación lateral en polea

- Keep: D-handle on the high pulley, standing side-on with the shoulder near the handle, underhand
  grip, elbow at about 90 degrees locked, feet at shoulder width, knees soft, bend sideways by
  contracting the obliques, do not pull with the arm, one-second hold, do not let the stack touch
  down between reps, add a plate at 12 reps, drop sets, and the double-contraction rep.
- Add the errors: pulling with the arm and shoulder, hips drifting, leaning forward, too much load.
- Frame the arm as a rigid link between the cable and the ribs, which is the whole cue.
- Links: `encogimiento-lateral-mancuerna-de-pie`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `series-descendentes`.

### 10. Encogimientos para oblicuos en polea

- Keep: rope on the high pulley, kneeling far enough back, rope at the sides of the head, arms
  fixed, thighs vertical, slight fold for tension, chin off the chest, fold and rotate so one elbow
  points at the opposite knee, hands fixed, natural back curve, one-second hold, alternate sides or
  all reps per side, and the right-centre-left combination.
- Add: 12 clean reps before adding a plate; the load rule from conflict 2; errors (pulling with the
  arms, rotating with the elbows instead of the ribs, the rope sliding).
- Links: `encogimientos-polea-2`, `encogimientos-oblicuos-banco-declinado`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `rutina-abdominales-para-culturismo`.
