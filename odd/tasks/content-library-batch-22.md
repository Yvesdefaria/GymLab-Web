## House positions for this block (binding)

1. **A legendary physique page is a report, not a template.** Each page says, in one sentence, that a
   competitor's routine assumes years of base and a body that tolerates that volume. No page sells a
   physique, a diet or a method as the way for the reader.
2. **No impersonation.** The archive speaks as Andreia Brazier and quotes Shawn Ray in first person.
   Everything is third person or attributed reporting.
3. **No supplement sections at all.** Not trimmed: removed. Animal Pak, CNP, HMB, BCAAs, glutamine,
   ZMA, whey, gainers and the supplement-retailer catalogue do not appear. The same for branded
   products (Pro Tan, baby oil) and for social, YouTube or federation URLs.
4. **A diet is reported as what that person ate.** No macros promises, no "this is why he looks like
   this", no dose protocols. Where the archive gives grams, they stay as his or her intake.
5. **Dangerous practice is described, never prescribed.** Shawn Ray's water manipulation is the case in
   point: the page explains that he did it and that it is dangerous, and does not print the litre ladder.
6. **Titles carry no archive artefacts.** "100% Auténtica 2026", "100% Efectiva" and the `|` suffixes
   are deleted.
7. Every page here is training or nutrition information, not medical advice. The only house notice in
   this batch is on page 10, which discusses dehydration and diuretics.
