## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The incline is a range dial, not an isolation trick.** Page 1 says the 45° recline isolates the
   anterior deltoid better and removes trapezius work (`:34`). At 45° the prescribed "shoulder height"
   stop (`:28`) is a fraction of standing flexion, so the claim is a shorter arc sold as a better one.
   Publish the range trade and measure the arc. Scapular upward rotation at end-range flexion is not a
   stance artifact; bracing does not delete it.
2. **A supported position is comfort, not treatment.** Page 1 says the incline "no ejerce presión sobre
   la zona lumbar" and is "beneficioso para quienes tienen problemas de espalda baja" (`:36`). Keep the
   first half as a comfort advantage; delete the therapeutic promise.
3. **Setup is the Smith press.** Page 2 puts the bar at eye height (`:30`), a 90° backrest (`:28`) and a
   vertical fixed path: the bar travels through the face line. The variable the page never gives is
   bench distance, so the bar clears the chin. Thesis: the machine owns the trajectory, you own the
   distance. Delete "seguro" and "garantiza" (`:24`, `:52`).
4. **The Cuban press is a loaded external rotation, not a press.** Page 3 folds the 90/90 external
   rotation into the press and calls the cuff "la musculatura interna del hombro" (`:18`, `:36`). Name
   the rotator cuff, make the external-rotation phase its own deliberate step, and keep the load light.
   The title's "evita lesiones" (`:3`) goes.
5. **A front raise does not cross the body to find the middle head.** Page 4 says pulling the hand
   diagonally toward the midline works the middle deltoid better (`:30`). Cross-body adduction reduces
   lateral-deltoid leverage and does not change the anterior target. Keep the path in the sagittal
   plane. Thesis: the cable vector, because the dumbbell front raise is dead at the bottom.
6. **The lean moves the gravity line, not the muscle.** Page 5 says the arm passes "más allá de la
   perpendicular al cuerpo" (`:46`), which is humerus beyond 90° abduction, exactly the position the
   lean exists to avoid; the same line contradicts its own parallel-to-floor reference. The endpoint is
   shoulder height. Also delete "superaislamiento" (`:34`): the lean adds isometric trunk work and
   removes the leg drive, it does not erase the upper back. Kill the rhetorical "¿a que sí?" (`:20`).
7. **Cable height chooses the plane.** Page 6 never states the pulley height (`:31`). A standing
   cross-cable reverse fly needs the pulleys at shoulder height or above; lower than that, the pull
   becomes a row. State it. Delete the trap-hypertrophy claim (`:24`) and the ustedeo (`:35`, `:37`).
8. **Seat height on a lateral raise machine is pivot alignment.** Page 7 sets the seat by pad comfort
   (`:24`). The cam or pivot must line up with the acromion, or the resistance curve and the end range
   change. Thesis: fit the machine to the joint, not the arms to the pad.
9. **The behind-neck barbell press owns the depth criterion, not the safety debate.** Page 8 prescribes
   the bar to mid-occiput with no mobility screen (`:30`) and then recommends the Multipower as "menos
   agresiva" (`:50`); a fixed path is more constrained, not safer, and batch 48's Smith page says so.
   The verdict and the wall screen already live in `press-militar-contra-press-tras-nuca`: link it
   once. This page owns: where to stop (mid-back of the head, never lower), grip slightly wider than
   the shoulders, seated with back support, lighter than the front press, never to failure. Delete the
   belt claim (`:32`), which does not protect thoracic extension.
10. **"Abrasar" at 45° is a 45° arc.** Page 9 calls the parallel-to-floor endpoint an inferno (`:18`)
    while at 45° chest-supported it is roughly 45° of humeral flexion; its own "stop short" cue (`:30`)
    shortens it further. One endpoint. Thesis: the pad removes the cheat, the bar removes the
    independent arc and the grip choice. That is the split from the dumbbell version, which owns the
    range dial.
11. **The hub is not a hub.** Page 10 has four hollow H2 sections with no exercise list (`:86`, `:90`,
    `:94`, `:100`), five entries that duplicate already-published pages, no counts, a first-person
    voice and a CTA. Rebuild it as a real catalogue: the three families, the 28 exercises grouped by
    head and pattern, a decision table, and the rear-delt argument. No first person, no CTA, no injury
    promises, no "los mejores".
12. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: front raises 3 x 12-15 (45-60 s); Smith press 3 x 8-12
    (90 s); Cuban press 3 x 12-15 (45-60 s, light); lateral raises 3 x 12-15 (45-60 s); cable reverse
    fly 3 x 12-15 (45-60 s); behind-neck press 3 x 8-10 (90 s, light, low frequency). The hub carries no
    routine dose beyond the family defaults.
13. **No page promises injury prevention, calls a joint a muscle, or sells a region that does not
    exist.**

## Per page

### 1. Incline-bench dumbbell front raise

- Bench at 30-45°, chest on the pad, a dumbbell in each hand with a neutral or pronated grip, raise to
  shoulder height with soft elbows, pause, lower with control. 3 x 12-15, 45-60 s.
- Thesis: the incline is a range dial. The stop point is a fraction of standing flexion; measure the
  arc instead of claiming more isolation. Keep the supported position as comfort, not treatment.
- Link `elevaciones-frontales-con-barra-en-banco-inclinado`, `elevaciones-frontales-mancuerna-alterno`,
  `elevaciones-frontales-barra`, `elevaciones-frontales-disco`, `anatomia-hombros`, `rutinas-hombros`,
  `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`.

### 2. Smith machine shoulder press

- Seat or stand with the backrest at 80-85°, bench distance so the bar clears the chin, bar at eye
  height, grip just outside the shoulders, press without locking, lower to the ears. 3 x 8-12, 90 s.
- Setup is the exercise. Delete "seguro" and "garantiza"; state what the fixed path owns (trajectory,
  balance) and what it does not (joint load, scapular rotation). One line and one link to the
  behind-neck argument.
- Link `press-hombros-maquina`, `press-tras-nuca-multipower`, `press-militar-barra-de-pie`,
  `press-militar-contra-press-tras-nuca`, `rutina-con-multipower`,
  `multipower-para-superar-puntos-de-estancamiento`, `rutina-hombros`,
  `rutina-hombros-pesos-libres`.

### 3. Cuban press

- Light dumbbells, elbows at 90° and upper arms at shoulder height, external rotation as its own slow
  phase, then press overhead, reverse in the same order. 3 x 12-15, 45-60 s, light.
- Thesis: the 90/90 external-rotation step most people skip. Name the rotator cuff, not "la
  musculatura interna". No injury promise.
- Link `manguito-rotadores-hombro`, `press-arnold-2`, `press-hombro-mancuernas-sentado`,
  `anatomia-hombros`, `rutinas-hombros`, `rutina-hombros-pesos-libres`, `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`.

### 4. One-arm cable front raise

- Low pulley, D handle, stand side-on or facing away, arm almost straight, raise forward to shoulder
  height in the sagittal plane, lower under control. 3 x 12-15 per arm, 45-60 s.
- Thesis: the cable vector. The bottom of a dumbbell front raise is dead; the cable keeps tension
  there. Delete the cross-body cue.
- Link `elevaciones-frontales-barra`, `elevaciones-frontales-mancuerna-alterno`,
  `elevaciones-frontales-disco`, `elevaciones-laterales`, `anatomia-hombros`, `rutinas-hombros`,
  `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`.

### 5. Leaning cable lateral raise

- Stand side-on to a low pulley or hold a fixed upright, feet together, lean away from the anchor, arm
  almost straight, sweep out to shoulder height, pause, lower slowly. 3 x 12-15 per arm, 45-60 s.
- Thesis: the lean moves the gravity line. It adds isometric trunk work and removes leg drive; it does
  not remove the upper back. Endpoint at shoulder height, never past perpendicular.
- Link `elevaciones-laterales`, `elevaciones-una-mano-polea`, `elevaciones-laterales-maquina`,
  `anatomia-hombros`, `rutinas-hombros`, `rutina-de-especializacion-de-hombros`,
  `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`.

### 6. Standing cable reverse fly

- Two pulleys at shoulder height, cross the handles, arms almost straight, sweep out and back to
  shoulder height, pause, return under control. 3 x 12-15, 45-60 s.
- Thesis: pulley height chooses the plane. Shoulder height or above is a reverse fly; lower turns it
  into a row. Keep the scapula quiet (the batch 48 doctrine). Delete the trap-hypertrophy claim.
- Link `aperturas-invertidas-contractora`, `elevaciones-posteriores-con-mancuerna`,
  `elevacion-posterior-una-mano-polea`, `consejos-entrenar-deltoides-posterior`, `anatomia-hombros`,
  `rutinas-hombros`, `fallo-muscular`.

### 7. Machine lateral raise

- Seat so the shoulder joint lines up with the machine's pivot and the upper arms rest on the pads,
  elbows slightly bent, sweep up to shoulder height, hold 1 s, lower under control. 3 x 12-15,
  45-60 s.
- Thesis: pivot alignment. Seat height is a joint-to-cam problem, not a comfort problem. One line on
  the assisted variants if the machine offers them.
- Link `elevaciones-laterales`, `elevaciones-una-mano-polea`, `elevacion-lateral-inclinada`,
  `anatomia-hombros`, `rutinas-hombros`, `rutina-hombros-pesos-libres`, `fallo-muscular`.

### 8. Behind-the-neck barbell press

- Seated with back support, bar behind the head, grip slightly wider than the shoulders, lower to the
  mid-back of the head and no further, press without locking, feet planted. 3 x 8-10, 90 s, light,
  never to failure, low frequency.
- Own the depth criterion and the screen: the wall test is one link to
  `press-militar-contra-press-tras-nuca`. Do not recommend the Smith as safer; the fixed path is more
  constrained, not less demanding. Delete the belt claim. Be honest about why anyone still does it
  (a longer arc and a different load distribution) without selling it as a mass builder.
- Link `press-tras-nuca-multipower`, `press-militar-contra-press-tras-nuca`, `press-militar-barra-de-pie`,
  `manguito-rotadores-hombro`, `anatomia-hombros`, `ejercicios-de-gimnasio-mas-lesivos`,
  `rutinas-hombros`, `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`.

### 9. Incline-bench barbell front raise

- Bench at 45°, chest on the pad, bar with a shoulder-width pronated grip, raise to arms parallel to
  the floor, pause, lower with control. 3 x 10-12, 45-60 s.
- Thesis: the pad removes the cheat, the bar removes the independent arc and the grip choice. State
