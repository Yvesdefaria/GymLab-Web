## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **Calf shape is genetic.** Page 10 sells routines for "internos planos", "externos planos",
   "gemelos cortos o altos" and "tobillos anchos". Muscle length and insertion cannot be trained; what
   grows is the medial head, the lateral head and the soleus, and foot rotation biases which head
   works. Say that, keep the four routines if they carry numbers, and drop the shape promises and the
   "seven curves of the feminine silhouette / modern amazon" framing.
2. **Training cannot spot-reduce or reshape a body part.** No page here may claim that a routine
   changes bone width, ankle width or muscle length.
3. **Invented statistics.** Page 2 opens with "90% of routines"; page 5 with "studies and personal
   experience". Drop the percentages and any study claim.
4. **Clickbait titles out.** "¿Efectiva?", "Efectividad 100%", "Hoy TOCA PECHO" and the `▷`/`✓`
   artifacts die. Titles 45-60 characters, plain.
5. **The chest block is closed by this batch.** Page 1 links up to `rutinas-pecho` (the hub); none of
   these five repeats the hub's catalogue. Same for the calf pair against `anatomia-gemelo-soleo`,
   `como-ganar-volumen-en-los-gemelos` and `grandes-gemelos-elevaciones-de-pie-o-sentado`.
6. **Tabata is a protocol, not a label.** Page 8 must state the intervals if the archive gives them,
   and must not call any short circuit "Tabata".
