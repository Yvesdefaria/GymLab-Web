## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The reverse curl is not the best forearm exercise.** Page 1 calls it that. It trains an elbow flexor
   (brachioradialis), not the wrist flexors and extensors that `ejercicios-antebrazo` orders. Publish it
   as the pronated-grip curl that removes supination and hands the lift to the brachioradialis, with a
   light load and a real dose.
2. **A wide reverse grip moves the bar away, it does not add brachioradialis.** Page 1 says a wider grip
   forces the bar closer. Width changes comfort and wrist stress, not the target. Fix it.
3. **The drag curl is a trajectory variant, not a strictness test.** Page 2 says momentum cannot cheat,
   then prescribes elbows drifting back and partner negatives. Elbows behind the torso is shoulder
   extension, which is extra range bought with the shoulder. Describe it honestly and give it a dose.
4. **The seated barbell curl is a top-half partial by design.** Page 3 says it works both heads and
   admits it uses half the range, then places it first in the session. Publish it as the seated
   load-measurement curl with a top-half range, at the end, and link the ROM page. Kill the head claim.
5. **The cable concentration curl does not target the long head.** Page 4 repeats the claim the
   published dumbbell page already killed. The elbow is pinned and the shoulder is neutral. Keep the two
   real advantages: line tension at the bottom and drop sets you can do alone. Drop the invented 60 cm.
6. **Pulley height decides the shoulder angle on the incline cable curl.** Page 5's advanced tip raises
   the pulleys to shoulder height, which cancels the incline and turns it into a short-head curl. Publish
   both setups as two exercises, with the low pulley as the incline and the shoulder-height pulley as the
   short-head version. It needs a dose.
7. **The high cable curl repeats "constant tension".** The library already settled that the cable keeps
   the line of pull, not the tension. Fix it, rewrite the vosotros in `tú`, delete the artifact list that
   names non-existent exercises, and keep the full dose (2-3 x 10-12, 1-2 s hold).
8. **The close-grip upright row is a variant of a page that already exists.** `remo-al-menton` owns the
   upright row and its impingement caveat. This page owns the close-grip bar path: hands inside the
   shoulders, elbows lead forward, shoulder internally rotates at the top, which is why the caveat
   stands. Never teach elbows-out with a narrow grip, and never claim it builds the trapezius better.
9. **A shrug is elevation, not retraction.** Page 8's correct technique says the scapulae retract. The
   house rule is shoulders up, not back; retraction belongs to rows. Fix the cue and keep the neck rule.
10. **The trapezius is not two thirds of the back.** Page 9 states it; the lats are the largest back
    muscle. Delete the figure. Keep the load argument: the bar admits the heaviest load and is the least
    forgiving of a moving neck; front versus behind changes the angle, not the muscle.
11. **The behind-the-back shrug's breath instruction is the real risk.** Page 10 prescribes holding the
    breath through the whole rep and claims 20 % more strength. Replace with one breath in before the
    lift and an exhale on the way down; no percentage, no Valsalva for a general audience. The variant is
    a light, posture-biased shrug with a shorter range, never a load exercise.
12. **No neck-injury certainty.** Page 8's neck and capsule claims are directionally standard; hedge
    them as risk, not as a promise, and keep the head still.
13. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: 3 x 8-12 for curls (90 s), 3 x 8-10 for the drag curl and
    the seated partial (90 s), 2-3 x 10-12 for the cable curls (60-90 s), 3 x 8-12 for the close-grip row
    at light load (90 s), 3-4 x 8-12 for shrugs with a 1 s hold (90 s). The archive mostly gives none;
    every dose is this batch's own recommendation and says so once.

## Per page

### 1. Reverse barbell curl

- Standing, pronated grip about shoulder width, elbows still, curl, 1 s at the top, control down.
  3 x 8-12, 90 s, light. The only common curl with the palm down from the start: no supination, so the
  brachioradialis takes the load.
- Kill "best forearm exercise" and the wide-grip mechanism. Link `ejercicios-antebrazo` so the wrist
  actions stay where they belong.
- Link `curl-barra`, `curl-zottman-mancuernas-de-pie`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`,
  `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`, `ejercicios-antebrazo`, `rutina-de-antebrazos`,
  `entrenamiento-del-agarre`.

### 2. Drag curl

- Standing, supinated grip, elbows travel back as the bar rises along the torso, bar finishes near the
  lower chest, 1 s hold, control down. 3 x 8-10, 90 s.
- Fix the strictness claim: elbows behind the torso is shoulder extension, the variant's real shape.
  Keep the Smith negative as an option with its 3-5 s eccentric.
- Link `curl-barra`, `biceps-grandes`, `consigue-grandes-brazos`, `curl-polea-de-pie`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`.

### 3. Seated barbell curl

- Seated on a bench, back straight, bar supinated, start with the elbows at about 90 degrees and curl to
  the top, control down. 3 x 8-10, 90 s, at the end of the session.
- Publish the top-half partial honestly: it is a partial by design, not a full curl, and it goes last.
  Kill the "both heads" claim.
- Link `curl-barra`, `curl-mancuernas-sentado-adosmanos`, `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `volumen-de-entrenamiento`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 4. Cable concentration curl

- Seated or kneeling at a low pulley, elbow pinned to the inner thigh, supinated handle, curl, 1 s hold,
  control down. 3 x 10-12, 60-90 s. Drop sets by moving the pin.
- Kill the long-head claim and the 60 cm precision. Keep the line tension at the bottom and the
  solo drop set.
- Link `curl-concentracion`, `curl-polea-de-pie`, `ejercicios-en-polea-ventajas`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `fallo-muscular`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 5. Incline cable curl

- Bench at 45 degrees between two low pulleys, arms hanging, supinated handles, curl without moving the
  upper arm, 1 s, control down. 3 x 10-12, 90 s. The low pulley keeps tension at the stretched bottom.
- Publish the shoulder-height pulley as the second setup: same bench, more shoulder flexion, short head,
  short range. Name both and stop calling one an advanced tip.
- Link `curl-mancuernas-banco-inclinado-a-dos-manos`, `curl-polea-alta-tumbado-banco`,
  `curl-polea-de-pie`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`.

### 6. High cable curl

- Standing, two high pulleys, arms slightly above parallel, curl with the elbows high and the hands
  finishing behind the ears, 1-2 s hold, control down. 2-3 x 10-12, 60-90 s.
- Rewrite in `tú`, fix "constant tension", delete the artifact list. Own the perpendicular line of pull
  at the top, at the cost of a short range.
- Link `curl-polea-de-pie`, `curl-polea-alta-tumbado-banco`, `curl-cuerda-agarre-martillo`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 7. Close-grip upright row

- The close-grip variant of the upright row: hands just inside the shoulders, bar close, elbows lead
  forward, pull to lower chest, shoulder internally rotates at the top. Light load, 3 x 8-12, 90 s.
- The verdict: it is not a trapezius builder; `remo-al-menton` owns the upright row and the caveat. This
  page exists to explain the grip width, the bar path and why the elbows-out teaching with a narrow grip
  is the impingement trap. Link `face-pull` as the substitute.
- Link `remo-al-menton`, `remo-de-pie-agarre-amplitud`, `face-pull`, `ejercicios-trapecios`,
  `entrenamiento-trapecios`, `manguito-rotadores-hombro`, `rutinas-hombros`.

### 8. Dumbbell shrug

- Standing with dumbbells at the sides, shoulders up toward the ears, no rotation, 1 s hold, control
  down. 3-4 x 8-12, 90 s. The longest range of the three implements and the easiest on the neck.
- Fix the retraction cue, keep the neck still, drop the "colossal traps" framing and give the dose.
- Link `encogimientos-barra`, `encogimientos-tras-nuca-barra`, `ejercicios-trapecios`,
  `entrenamiento-trapecios`, `entrenar-los-trapecios-con-los-hombros-o-con-la-espalda`,
  `rutinas-hombros`.

### 9. Barbell shrug

- Standing with the bar in front, grip just outside the thighs, shoulders up, 1 s hold, control down.
  3-4 x 8-12, 90 s. Heaviest load of the three and the least forgiving of a moving neck.
- Delete the two-thirds figure. Keep the load and strap argument, and the front-versus-behind note as
  one line linking page 10.
- Link `encogimientos-mancuernas`, `encogimientos-tras-nuca-barra`, `ejercicios-trapecios`,
  `entrenamiento-trapecios`, `tipos-agarre`, `ejercicios-espalda`.

### 10. Behind-the-back barbell shrug

- Bar behind the body at the lower glutes, grip slightly wider than the shoulders, shoulders up, 1 s
  hold, control down, exhale on the way down. 3 x 8-10, light, 90 s. A posture-biased variant with a
  shorter range.
- Kill the 20 % figure and the breath-holding instruction; no Valsalva for a general audience. Keep the
  retraction-before-elevation note and the shorter-range admission.
- Link `encogimientos-barra`, `encogimientos-mancuernas`, `ejercicios-trapecios`,
  `entrenamiento-trapecios`, `entrenar-los-trapecios-con-los-hombros-o-con-la-espalda`,
  `face-pull`.
