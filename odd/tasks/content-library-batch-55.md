## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The standing calf machine is a straight-knee exercise.** Page 1 tells the reader to keep the
   knees bent and mocks lifters who lock them. The gastrocnemius crosses the knee; the standing
   machine exists because the knee stays extended. Publish "extended, not hyperextended" and drop the
   bent-knee cue.
2. **The seated machine is what bends the knee.** Page 1 then says seated machines with "piernas
   rectas" also train the gastrocnemius, a machine that does not exist. The seated machine flexes the
   knee and biases the soleus; the page should say so once.
3. **The donkey raise does not stretch the gastrocnemius through the hip.** Page 2 implies the
   hip-flexed position changes the stretch. The gastrocnemius does not cross the hip. The real
   difference is the torso angle: the load runs along the spine and frees the traps, which lets the
   ankle travel further.
4. **Toe direction barely changes emphasis.** Pages 2 and 3 sell toes-in and toes-out as region
   selection. `como-ganar-volumen-en-los-gemelos` already says it changes little. Publish one honest
   sentence and move on.
5. **Page 3 is written in usted and ends mid-section.** Rewrite in `tú`, complete the page, and give
   it the technique role: pad height, why the dumbbells cap the load, the pause. It must not repeat
   the 4 x 20 routine that `rutina-de-gemelos-sin-maquinas` already publishes; link it.
6. **The tibialis page names the wrong muscles.** Page 4 lists "extensor digital largo" (extensor
   digitorum longus), "peroneo lateral" (fibularis longus and brevis, evertors) and "peroneo tercio"
   (fibularis tertius, a dorsiflexor). Publish the correct names and the three that matter:
   tibial anterior, extensor largo de los dedos, fibularis tercio.
7. **The tibialis page promises injury prevention.** Page 4 says it prevents shin splints.
   `anatomia-y-entrenamiento-del-tibial-anterior` already owns that nuance and names periostitis,
   bone and fascia. Publish the training role (the dorsiflexor that brakes the foot each stride), not
   a prevention claim.
8. **The barbell curl is single-joint, not multiarticular.** Page 5 uses "multiarticular" to justify
   opening the session with it. The shoulder does not move. The real reason is load: it is the
   heaviest bilateral elbow flexion, measurable in 2.5 kg steps.
9. **Grip width does not choose a biceps head.** The library has three positions: `ejercicios-biceps`
   says it does not, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo` and `rutinas-biceps` say wide biases the short head.
   Publish the defensible rule (shoulder angle decides the head; grip width changes comfort and
   leverage) and link `ejercicios-biceps`. Do not restate the disputed claim. Record the internal
   conflict in the batch result for a later sweep.
10. **The EZ bar is a wrist trade, not a safety guarantee.** Page 6's title promises "sin lesiones".
    The semi-pronated grip unloads the wrist and costs supination, so the brachialis takes more. That
    trade is the page. Fix the truncated "Variantes" section.
11. **The spider curl setup is unusable as written.** Page 7 mixes a preacher bench and an incline
    bench, then describes sitting at a preacher bench at 45 degrees, then says "levantar las
    mancuernas" on a barbell page. Publish the real setup: prone, chest on a 45-degree incline, arms
    hanging vertical. Its mechanism is the preacher's with a free elbow; say that.
12. **The Scott page contradicts itself on the head.** Page 8 says the Scott works the short head and
    then that the Z grip shifts it to the long head. Swapping bars does not move the shoulder, so the
    short head keeps the advantage. Delete the second claim.
13. **The rope is not a fixed neutral grip.** Page 9 calls itself a neutral-grip exercise and then
    keeps the rope, which lets the hands supinate through the rep. Own the hybrid: neutral at the
    bottom, supinating as you curl, which is what a dumbbell hammer cannot do.
14. **The high-cable lying curl's geometry is missing.** Page 10 is unclear whether the pulley is
    above the head, level with the bench or behind it, and that angle is the exercise. Fix the setup:
    supine, cable overhead, elbows toward the ceiling. Gravity keeps tension at the bottom, which a
    standing curl loses.
15. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: 3 x 10-15 for calves with a 1-2 s pause (60-90 s);
    tibialis 2-3 x 12-15; curls 3 x 8-12 (90 s), preacher 3 x 10 to match `rutinas-biceps`, rope and
    cable 3 x 10-12. The archive mostly gives none; every dose is this batch's own recommendation and
    says so once.

## Per page

### 1. Standing calf raise machine

- Shoulders under the pads, balls of the feet on the platform, knees extended but not locked, heels
  below the platform, rise to full plantarflexion, 1-2 s pause, control the return. 3 x 10-15, 60-90 s.
- Own the apparatus: the machine fixes the torso and the load line, so the only variable is the ankle.
  Fix the knee cue and the "seated machine with straight legs" sentence. Replace the spine-and-knee
  scare with the real caveat: the pad compresses the shoulders, so set it at collarbone height.
- Link `anatomia-gemelo-soleo`, `grandes-gemelos-elevaciones-de-pie-o-sentado`,
  `elevacion-gemelo-maquina-sentado`, `elevacion-gemelos-barra`, `rutina-de-gemelos-sin-maquinas`,
  `como-ganar-volumen-en-los-gemelos`.

### 2. Donkey calf raise

- Hips flexed over a pad, balls of the feet on a block, knees extended, full heel drop, rise, 1 s
  pause. 3 x 12-15, or 6-10 heavy with a 1-2 s pause, 60-90 s.
- The thesis: the torso angle runs the load along the spine and frees the traps, so the ankle can
  travel further. Kill the hip-stretch myth and the toe-direction promise. Give it a real dose.
- Link `gemelo-maquina-depie`, `elevacion-gemelos-barra`, `anatomia-gemelo-soleo`,
  `grandes-gemelos-elevaciones-de-pie-o-sentado`, `rutina-de-gemelos-sin-maquinas`,
  `entrenamiento-de-gemelos-diferente`.

### 3. Seated dumbbell calf raise

- Sit with the balls of the feet on a 5-10 cm block, dumbbells on the thighs, heels drop below the
  block, rise, 1 s pause, control. 3 x 15-20, 60 s.
- Convert the usted to tú, complete the page, and make it the technique page: pad height, why the
  dumbbells cap the load, why the pause matters. Do not repeat the 4 x 20 routine; link it.
- Link `elevacion-gemelo-maquina-sentado`, `gemelo-maquina-depie`,
  `rutina-de-gemelos-sin-maquinas`, `anatomia-gemelo-soleo`, `ejercicios-pierna`,
  `rutinas-pesas-casa`.

### 4. Tibialis curl

- Seated or standing tibialis apparatus, foot strapped, full dorsiflexion, 1-2 s hold, controlled
  return; or the wall stretch as the warm-up. 2-3 x 12-15, 60 s.
- Fix the muscle names and kill "previene". Own the equipment and the range limit: the stretch is
  capped by Achilles and soleus stiffness, which is why the calf stretch comes first.
- Link `anatomia-y-entrenamiento-del-tibial-anterior`, `gemelo-maquina-depie`,
  `elevacion-de-gemelos-con-mancuernas-sentado`, `elevaciones-de-gemelo-burro`,
  `rango-completo-de-movimiento-rom`, `entrenamiento-del-agarre`.

### 5. Barbell curl

- Standing, shoulder width grip, elbows tucked and still, full extension at the bottom, no swing.
  3 x 8-12, 90 s. The anchor page of the family.
- Fix "multiarticular" and the typo. Settle the grip rule: shoulder angle decides the head, grip
  width changes comfort and leverage; link `ejercicios-biceps`. Own the measurable-load argument.
- Link `curl-barra-z`, `curl-cuerda-agarre-martillo`, `curl-arana`, `curl-scott-barraz`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `ejercicios-biceps`, `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`,
  `rutinas-biceps`.

### 6. EZ bar curl

- Same position as the barbell curl with the semi-pronated grip: the wrists sit at a fixed angle that
  costs supination and loads the brachialis more. 3 x 8-12, 90 s.
- Kill the "sin lesiones" promise, the first person, and the truncated variants section. Own the
  wrist trade and the grip width on the curves.
- Link `curl-barra`, `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`,
  `curl-scott-barraz`, `curl-cuerda-agarre-martillo`, `rutinas-biceps`.

### 7. Spider curl

- Prone on a 45-degree incline, chest on the pad, arms hanging vertical, curl without moving the
  upper arm, 1 s at the top. 3 x 10-12, 90 s.
- Fix the setup, the bench mix-up, the "mancuernas" line and the unsourced ranking. Thesis: the
  preacher mechanism with a free elbow, so the forearm travels past the pad.
- Link `curl-scott-barraz`, `curl-polea-alta-tumbado-banco`, `curl-barra`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `biceps-grandes`, `ejercicios-biceps`.

### 8. Scott curl with EZ bar

- Preacher bench, armpits on the pad, EZ bar, lower until the elbows are almost straight but not
  locked, curl to just short of vertical. 3 x 10, 90 s.
- Delete the long-head contradiction, keep the wrist comfort sentence without "desgaste", drop the
  first person and the "100% efectivo". Thesis: the pad removes the shoulder, so the short head is
  forced, and the price is the bottom position.
- Link `curl-arana`, `curl-polea-alta-tumbado-banco`, `curl-barra`, `curl-barra-z`,
  `posicion-de-muneca-en-el-curl-de-biceps`, `rutinas-biceps`, `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`.

### 9. Rope hammer curl

- Low pulley with a rope, neutral grip at the bottom, supinating as the hands rise, elbows pinned.
  3 x 10-12, 90 s. Own the hybrid grip: the only handle that changes during the rep.
- Kill the pump rationale, the vosotros and the first person. Keep the brachialis and brachioradialis
  sentence and link the anatomy page.
- Link `curl-barra`, `curl-cuerda-agarre-martillo` (self only if useful; otherwise omit),
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `entrenamiento-del-agarre`, `ejercicios-antebrazo`,
  `rutina-de-antebrazos`, `consigue-grandes-brazos`.

### 10. High cable lying curl

- Supine on a bench with the cable overhead, elbows pointing at the ceiling, curl the handle toward
  the forehead, 1 s, control the return. 3 x 10-12, 90 s. The short-head family with constant
  tension: gravity keeps the bottom of the rep loaded.
- Fix the geometry, the infinitive imperatives and the third short-head repetition; link siblings
  instead of re-arguing the head.
- Link `curl-scott-barraz`, `curl-arana`, `curl-barra`, `biceps-grandes`,
  `anatomia-biceps-antebrazo`, `consigue-grandes-brazos`.
