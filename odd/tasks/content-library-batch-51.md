## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The hub's criterion is the compound, and the curl is not one.** Page 1 lists ten "básicos"
   including a barbell curl while defining them as multi-joint. Publish the real criterion (two or
   more joints, loadable, progressable) and group by pattern: hinge, squat, push, pull, carry. The
   curl dies.
2. **The hub is not a blurb list.** It owns the order inside a session and the dose per pattern, with
   links to the dedicated pages. It must not duplicate `ejercicios-multiarticulares-y-ejercicios-de-aislamiento`
   (which owns the compound/isolation distinction) or `ejercicios-pierna` (which owns the leg
   catalogue). 700-1,000 words.
3. **The conventional deadlift has no page and this batch fills it.** Page 2 is the only source; its
   warm-up ramp (50 % x 5, 75 % x 3, 90 % x 2, top set) is the most reusable number in the batch.
   Publish 3-4 x 5-10 once a week, the setup, the errors, and the sumo comparison as links, not as a
   re-argument.
4. **"Agarre supino" in page 2 is wrong.** It means double pronation; a palms-up grip on a near-maximal
   pull loads the distal biceps tendon. The mixed grip is "alterno", and `peso-muerto-mejor-agarre`
   owns the comparison. Fix it, keep the hook-grip section, and drop "las palmas miran hacia atrás".
5. **The jerk taxonomy is broken.** Page 3's table erases the distinction it exists to teach. Publish
   one axis: split versus no split, and whether the legs re-bend under the bar. The page is the
   no-split jerk; name push press and split jerk once, then stay on its lift. Dose 4-6 x 2-4 at 70-90 %.
6. **Three clean-family pages are three lifts, not three copies.** The archive text is thin and two
   sources share a paragraph, but the clean, the clean and jerk, and the power clean are distinct:
   full-squat catch, the jerk after the clean, and the above-parallel catch. Differentiate explicitly
   and cross-link; never paste the same paragraph.
7. **The clean's grip is just outside the knees.** Page 5 says between shoulders and hips, which is
   narrower than the shoulders and jams the bar into the shins. Fix it in the clean and in the snatch.
8. **The heel cue is wrong.** Page 5 says drive with the toes, not the heels. Early plantarflexion
   lifts the hips before the bar moves and pushes it forward. Drive through the whole foot, heels down
   until triple extension; "pies en movimiento" stays as the second-pull cue.
9. **The snatch grip is the widest in the sport.** Page 6 says between shoulders and hips, which makes
   the overhead catch impossible. Publish roughly 1.5 times shoulder width, bar in the hip crease at
   the start. Keep the honest note that the lift takes months.
10. **The power clean must say what makes it power.** Page 7 never states the catch above parallel and
    ends mid-sentence. Publish the catch height, its role as a teaching step toward the clean, and a
    dose.
11. **The dumbbell sumo squat is spine-lighter, not spine-free.** Page 8 says the load is not on the
    spine and promises relief for back pain. A dumbbell at the waist still loads the spine axially;
    what falls is the moment arm. Fix the mechanism and keep the real use: a stance and adductor
    option for people who cannot rack a bar.
12. **Squat depth is thigh-parallel, not a 90° knee angle.** Page 9 uses 90° and also says the spine
    stays parallel to the shins. Publish the reliable references: thigh parallel to the floor, torso
    and shin at similar inclination.
13. **One bracing instruction per page.** Page 9 says exhale on the way up in one section and hold
    until the top in another. `sentadilla-sin-lesiones` settles it: brace on heavy sets. Fix the
    first.
14. **No stance-to-vastus map.** Page 9 claims a wider stance builds the "external quadriceps".
    `anatomia-del-cuadriceps` already rejects vasto interno/externo. Stance changes hip and ankle
    demand, not which head grows.
15. **The squat hub is not a six-week programme.** Page 9 carries a full 6-week light/heavy protocol
    that duplicates `rutina-de-piernas-con-dia-ligero-y-dia-pesado`. Publish the squat and one dose
    per goal; link the periodization.
16. **Five adductors, not six.** Page 10 invents "aductor menor". The library publishes five:
    grácil, pectíneo, aductor largo, aductor corto and aductor mayor. The semi-extended working leg is
    the cue worth keeping. Dose 3 x 12-15 with 60-90 s, and do not claim toning or spine damage.
17. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: 3-5 x 5-8 for strength lifts (2-3 min), 3 x 8-12 for
    size (90-120 s), 3 x 12-15 for isolation (60-90 s), Olympic lifts 4-6 x 2-4 at 70-90 % with full
    recovery.
