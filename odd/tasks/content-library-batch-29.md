## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **Page 2 attacks loaded rotation** ("twist crunches are potentially dangerous") while batch 28
   publishes two rotation exercises. House line: rotation over a light load through a small range is
   fine; the risk lives in heavy load, a fixed pelvis and a long lever at the end of the range. Page
   2 argues for training the lateral plane, not against other exercises.
2. **Page 2 overclaims about the spine.** Drop "heavy training compacts the spine, you lose height
   and the discs flatten until the back fails", and drop the scoliosis prevention claim. Keep the
   useful part: hanging decompresses the shoulders and spine after a heavy session and the lateral
   chain is undertrained.
3. **A weighted plank is still abdominal work.** Page 4 calls it "not an abdominal movement but a
   transverse abdominal reinforcer". It is an anti-extension isometric that loads the rectus, the
   obliques and the transverse together. Do not sell it as a different category.
4. **Bench angle, no incline/decline assertion.** Page 5 and its own image disagree on whether the
   bench is inclined or declined. Use neutral wording: an adjustable bench, and the higher the head
   end, the harder the set. The slug stays `encogimientos-invertidos-banco-inclinado`.
5. **"Lower abs" is not a muscle** (settled in batch 26). Pages 6-9 sell "abdomen inferior". Rewrite
   on the house line: the hip flexors raise the legs, the rectus stabilises and tilts the pelvis,
   and the curl at the top is trunk flexion.
6. **Page 8's psoas claim.** It says the psoas contracts concentrically to 90 degrees while the abs
   work isometrically. Say what is true and consistent with the batch 27 family page: the hip flexors
   lift the legs, the rectus brakes the pelvis and adds the posterior tilt, and the top of the rep
   is trunk flexion.
7. **Page 10's protocol is unusual.** Keep it as the archive's finisher (short, fast kicks inside two
   seconds, at most 45 degrees) and say plainly that it is a local-endurance finisher, not a
   strength lift.

## Editorial decisions per page

### 1. Cortar troncos en polea

- Keep: D-handle on the high pulley, stand 60-90 cm to the side, feet wider than the hips with the
  toes out, knees soft, grasp with both hands, arms long, pull diagonally across the body to the far
  shin, bend the knees more as the handle comes down, brief stop low with the abs tight and the back
  flat, slow both phases, start very light, the eleven-to-five clock line (one-to-seven from the
  other side), and the low-pulley variant on alternate weeks.
- Add: the rotation comes from the hips and the rib cage; the lumbar stays neutral. Errors: pulling
  with the arms, rounding the lower back, letting the stack snap back.
- Delete the vosotros and the bodybuilding.com credit.
- Links: `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `giros-en-disco`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`,
  `encogimientos-oblicuos-polea`.

### 2. Elevación lateral de piernas colgado

The longest source of the block. It must not read as a warning page.

- Keep: overhand grip on a high bar, straight arms, hanging with straight legs and a slight knee and
  hip bend, locked legs, the pelvis and legs rise laterally together, the waist is the pivot, legs
  stay in the lateral plane, the 30-45 degree ceiling from vertical, breathe in on the way up and out
  on the way down, hamstring flexibility as the prerequisite, ankle weights once it is easy, the
  QL as the main lateral flexor, and the "do not pull with the arm" error.
- Fix: drop the disc-compaction and scoliosis claims (conflict 2), and reframe the attack on
  rotation (conflict 1).
- Structure: what the lateral plane trains, setup, execution, range, errors, prerequisites and how
  to program it.
- Links: `abdominales-ejercicios-colgado`, `elevaciones-piernas-colgado`,
  `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 3. Press Pallof

- Keep: cable at chest height, both hands on the handle at the chest, stand side-on, feet wider than
  the hips, tension on the cable, press out until the arms are straight, hold a few seconds, return;
  three sets of 10-15 per side; anti-rotation.
- Add: the category (the arms move, the trunk resists, which makes it anti-rotation isometric work
  for the trunk), the half-kneeling stance, the errors (turning the torso, hips drifting, rushing),
  and the breathing rule (do not hold the whole set).
- Links: `abdominales-isometricos`, `giros-en-disco`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 4. Plancha con peso

- Keep: plate of 10-15-20 kg on the glutes or lower back, a partner to place it, body straight on
  forearms and toes, back flat, abs tight, 60 seconds as the target, three sets of one minute,
  +10-15 seconds once the target is easy, then more weight; the safety warning.
- Fix the category claim (conflict 3). Add: prerequisites (a clean unweighted plank), the backpack
  alternative when training alone, the stop rule (hips sag or the lower back arches), and why load
  beats time past a minute.
- Links: `abdominales-isometricos`, `rodar-con-barra`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 5. Encogimientos invertidos en banco

- Keep: adjustable bench, grip the top edges, arms almost straight, knees at 90 degrees, feet
  together, lift the hips off the pad, exhale at the top, controlled descent, add angle at twelve
  clean reps, ankle weights, and the drop set by lowering the angle after failure.
- Use the neutral angle wording (conflict 4).
- Add: the pelvis curls, the lumbar stays supported, the errors (swinging, only the knees moving,
  half range), and the regression to the floor version.
- Links: `encogimientos-invertidos`, `encogimientos-banco-inclinado`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 6. Elevación de rodillas sentado (Pull-In)

- Keep: sit on the edge of a flat bench, lean back, feet off the floor and together, grip the bench
  at the sides, balance on the glutes, bring the knees to the chest with a slight forward lean,
  one-second hold, return with the feet still off the floor; ankle weights; higher feet, harder set;
  the option of eight light sets between other exercises.
- Fix the "lower abs" framing (conflict 5). Add the lower-back rule: the back stays neutral, not
  rounded; stop if it rounds or the hip flexors complain.
- Links: `abdominales-ejercicios-colgado`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`, `elevaciones-piernas-banco-plano`.

### 7. Elevaciones de piernas en banco plano

- Keep: lie supine with the legs off the end of the bench, hands under the hips or gripping the
  bench, legs as straight as possible, lift to 90 degrees at the top, slow both phases, exhale on
  the way down, a light dumbbell between the feet as a progression, lumbar flat throughout.
- Fix "the muscles of the lower abdominal group" (conflict 5). Explain the risk the archive names
  but never explains: the lower back arches when the legs go too low, so the bottom of the range is
  where the set ends.
- Add: the bent-knee regression, the errors (fast reps, hands pulling the bench, too much range).
- Links: `pull-in-banco-plano`, `encogimientos-invertidos`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `ejercicios-dolor-de-espalda`.

### 8. Elevaciones de piernas colgado

- Differentiate from batch 27's family page: this is the straight-leg exercise.
- Keep: overhand grip, hanging with straight arms, straps when the grip fails first, straight legs
  with a slight knee and hip bend, locked, lift above horizontal, the lower spine curls up at the
  top, hold and exhale, control down to a dead hang, no swinging, ankle weights, the reversed
  version starting at the top, and the bent-knee regression.
- Fix the psoas claim (conflict 6).
- Links: `abdominales-ejercicios-colgado`, `elevaciones-piernas-paralelas`,
  `ejercicios-de-abdominales`, `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 9. Elevaciones de piernas en paralelas

- Keep: forearms on the pads, back against the pad, body hanging, legs straight and together, lift
  above horizontal, the pelvis rotates, the glutes leave the pad, exhale at the top, controlled
  descent, no swing, finish the set with bent knees when fatigue arrives, shorten the rest once
  12-15 clean reps get easy.
- Add: why it is easier than hanging (the grip is not the limit), the bent-versus-straight dial,
  errors (knee raise only, swinging, half range).
- Links: `elevaciones-piernas-colgado`, `pull-in-banco-plano`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.

### 10. Tijeras de piernas

- Keep: supine, arms at the sides with the palms down, legs straight with unlocked knees, feet about
  20 cm off the floor, head off the floor, alternating short kicks, feet never above 45 degrees, two
  seconds per rise, exhale upward, feet never touching the floor.
- Frame it as a finisher (conflict 7), add the lumbar rule (hands under the hips if needed, stop when
  the back arches), the slower three-second version, and ankle weights.
- Links: `bicicleta-oblicuos`, `elevaciones-piernas-banco-plano`, `ejercicios-de-abdominales`,
  `rutinas-ejercicios-abdominales`.
