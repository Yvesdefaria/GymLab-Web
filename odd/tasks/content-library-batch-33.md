## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The calf rule, again.** Bent knee works the soleus, straight knee the gastrocnemius (batch 25).
   Pages 1, 2 and 4 put a seated calf raise first and sell it as a gastrocnemius builder. Keep the
   exercises and their numbers, label each one honestly, and say that the straight-knee work is the
   gastrocnemius work.
2. **Foot rotation and stance width do not isolate an inner or an outer quad.** Page 6 sells
   "prioridad zona interna del muslo" and "muslo externo". The vastus medialis and lateralis both
   extend the knee; stance and foot angle change comfort and which fibres feel the load. Keep the
   exercise numbers of the retained templates, drop the isolation claims, and keep the one legitimate
   use of a unilateral test: comparing left and right strength.
3. **No local fat loss.** Page 6's "rutina para definición" cannot burn fat from the thigh. Short
   rests and high reps build work capacity; definition is a body-fat level. The template survives as a
   density session, never as a fat-burning prescription.
4. **"Sin descanso" must match the rest it prescribes.** Page 6's giant set carries 3 min between
   rounds and its "supercongestión" template 30-45 s between 30-rep sets. Keep the numbers, call the
   rest what it is, and say that a 30-rep set is not a hypertrophy method by itself.
5. **Triceps heads are not one exercise each.** Pages 8 and 10 imply one movement per head. All three
   heads extend the elbow; shoulder position biases the long head, which is why overhead work is in
   the plan. Keep every exercise, set and rep.
6. **There is no inner or outer pectoral.** Page 9 claims four sections plus a "porción inferior del
   pectoral superior". One pectoral with a clavicular and a sternal head; the incline angle biases the
   clavicular part (ceiling 30 degrees, batch 25). Decline pressing is an option, not a requirement.
7. **Rest-pause is a cluster, not a doubling.** Page 9 sells "el doble" of reps from a 7-8 RM load.
   The load does not change; the cluster adds reps at that load. The page carries two rest figures
   (15-20 s and "15-10 s"); it uses 15-20 s and never reproduces the typo.
8. **Invented claims die.** "Las piernas son más del 50 % de la masa muscular", "1,5 veces tu peso
   corporal como mínimo", "los hidratos no alimentan las células de grasa", "la sentadilla pone todo el
   cuerpo en estado anabólico", "hace 50 años no había suplementos ni aparatos", "las máquinas son
   inútiles" and the eight-week "cambio espectacular" promise.
9. **Page 3 is the Pierna hub, not a routine page.** The archive page is two empty H2s plus a rant
   against machines. Rebuild it as the block hub: why legs, the five movement patterns, minimum
   effective dose with set ranges, and links to the eight sibling Pierna pages and the three Rutinas de
   Pierna pages. It carries no routine table of its own.
10. **Volume honesty.** Page 2 prescribes 13-15 quad sets plus 11 calf sets in one session; page 1 runs
    seven exercises to fallo-1. Keep both routines and their numbers, state the working-set total, and
    add the reduction rule: start at two thirds of the volume, add sets only while the last set is
    still technically clean.
11. **Old-school squats keep their caveats.** Page 7's "no supplements, no machines 50 years ago" is
    false; the overhead dumbbell squat is limited by overhead stability; the barbell hack squat needs
    a setup cue or an honest "skip it". The three-week block stays.
12. **Keep the prescription, fix the reasoning.** Pages 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10 carry real routines:
    exercise order, sets, reps and rest stay. What changes is the explanation, the register and the
    promises.
