## Conflicts the batch settles (binding)

1. **The incline ceiling is 30°, not 45°.** Every source in this batch says 30-45°. Above 30° the
   anterior deltoid takes over; 45° is nearly a shoulder press. Publish 30° and link
   `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho` for the full angle argument instead of restating a range.
2. **A dumbbell press starts with the palms facing the feet.** Page 1 says "palmas hacia arriba" and
   then contradicts itself one line later. Palms-up is a different exercise.
3. **Page 1 is the per-arm half of the incline press, not the general guide.** It owns the path of
   each dumbbell, the longer stretch at the bottom and the lower load ceiling; page 2 owns the bar.
4. **Page 2 is a modern rewrite, not an archive scrape.** It has 1,952 words, first person, voseo,
   no sets, reps or rest, and it duplicates three published pages. Rebuild it from scratch as the
   barbell incline technique page; never adapt its voice or its anecdotes.
5. **An incline fly at 45° is self-defeating.** Page 3 prescribes 45° "para trabajar el pecho" and
   then says high inclines hand the work to the deltoid. Publish 30°, light load, and make the page's
   subject the free-weight fly and where it loses tension compared with the cable version.
6. **A neutral grip does not bias the upper chest in a decline press.** Page 4 says it does. The
   decline biases the sternal fibres through the torso angle, not through the hand position.
7. **Loading a decline dumbbell press alone is the real cost.** Page 4 warns about fainting and blood
   pressure. Keep a short head-position caution, then teach the setup: kick the bells up, set the feet,
   or use a partner. No scare, no medical promise.
8. **The pectoralis minor is not a humeral adductor.** Page 5 lists it as a working muscle in the
   decline fly. It depresses and protracts the scapula.
9. **Unracking a decline bar over the throat is the worst cue in the batch.** Page 6 tells the reader
   to start with the bar over the neck and later teaches contact at the lower chest. Publish the lower
   chest as the reference and set the hooks so the unrack never crosses the throat. Spotter or safeties.
10. **The decline press does not activate more fibres.** Page 6 claims it beats the flat press in
    fibre recruitment; EMG comparisons are equivocal. The honest case is shorter range and more load.
11. **The flat fly's resistance peaks at the stretch and vanishes at the top.** That is its use: light
    load, long range, no strength claim. Page 7's "pec deck doubles muscle involvement" is fabricated
    and dies; the real argument (constant tension) is already in `maquinas-pesos-libres-al-entrenar-el-pecho`.
12. **Joining the dumbbells at the top does not build the inner chest.** Page 8 claims it does. There
    are two heads and no inner region; the bells meet because the arms adduct, nothing more. Page 8
    also contains a decline-bench paragraph pasted by mistake, which goes.
13. **The high crossover loads the sternal portion, not the upper chest.** Page 9 says high pulleys hit
    the lower pec and then tells the reader to lean forward to reach the upper chest. Leaning changes
    the line of pull, not the region. Correct it once and link `anatomia-del-pecho`.
14. **A fly holds a fixed elbow bend of about 10-15°, not 130°.** Page 9's "flexión de unos 130º" is
    not a usable cue. State the bend once, keep it fixed, and stop selling the squeeze: sell tension
    time through the arc.
15. **The pec deck is two machine families.** Page 10 mixes elbow-pad machines with handle-only
    machines in the same setup instruction. Publish both, and add the shoulder caveat the archive
    omits: the load runs from external to internal rotation, so seat height and range matter.
16. **Every row states its dose.** Defaults: 3-4 x 6-10 for presses (2-3 min), 6-12 for presses when
    the goal is size (90-120 s), 2-3 x 12-15 for flies (60-90 s), add load when the last set is clean.
17. **No page sells a region of a muscle that does not exist.** "Pectoral interno", "porción media" as
    separate heads and "parte externa" die in every file of the batch.

## Per page

### 1. Incline dumbbell press

- 30° bench, dumbbells over the upper chest, palms facing the feet, elbows about 45° from the torso.
  Press up and slightly in without clashing the bells at the top. 3-4 x 8-12, 90-120 s.
- Own the per-dumbbell path, the deeper stretch at the bottom and the lower load ceiling than the bar.
  Fix the palms-up bullet and drop the "one-arm with the shoulder off the bench" variant.
- Link `press-inclinado-barra`, `aperturas-mancuernas-banco-inclinado`,
  `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho`, `press-inclinado-multipower`, `anatomia-del-pecho`,
  `mancuernas-entrenando-pecho`, `rutinas-pecho`.

### 2. Incline barbell press

- 30° bench, grip slightly wider than the shoulders, elbows about 45°, bar to the upper chest at the
  clavicle line, feet planted, spotter or safeties. 3-4 x 6-10, 2-3 min as the strength default;
  3 x 8-12 when the goal is size.
- Do not adapt the archive's voice or its first-person history. Own the barbell-specific setup, the
  bar path, the sticking point and the errors. One section on the Smith version linking page 1 and
  `press-inclinado-multipower`, not a duplicate of them.
- Link `press-inclinado-mancuernas`, `aperturas-mancuernas-banco-inclinado`,
  `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho`, `press-inclinado-multipower`, `press-banca-barra`,
  `mejorar-fuerza-press-de-banca`, `rutinas-pecho`.

### 3. Incline dumbbell fly

- 30° bench, palms facing each other, elbows fixed at about 10-15° of bend, arms open to shoulder
  level and no lower, 2-3 x 12-15, 60-90 s. Light load; the stretch is the point.
- The thesis: the free-weight fly loses tension at the top, and the incline bench makes that hardest
  to hold. That is the argument for the cable version, not a reason to skip this one. Kill the 45°
  prescription and the "upper region" continuum.
- Link `press-inclinado-mancuernas`, `press-inclinado-barra`, `aperturas-inclinadas-polea`,
  `ejercicios-inclinados-para-pecho`, `aperturas-planas-mancuernas`, `anatomia-del-pecho`.

### 4. Decline dumbbell press

- Bench at 15-30° of decline, feet secured, knees bent, dumbbells over the lower chest, press up and
  slightly in. 3-4 x 8-12, 90-120 s. Teach the kick-up and the partner option; a short head-position
  caution, no fainting story.
- Fix the neutral-grip claim and the "inner pectoral" line. The decline biases the sternal fibres;
  the hands do not.
- Link `press-declinado-barra`, `aperturas-mancuernas-banco-declinado`, `ejercicios-pecho`,
  `anatomia-del-pecho`, `fondos-pecho`, `rutinas-pecho`, `rutinas-de-pecho-principiantes`.

### 5. Decline dumbbell fly

- Bench at 15-30° of decline, arms open to shoulder level, elbows fixed, 2-3 x 12-15 light, 60-90 s,
  sit up between sets. The thesis: it is the only fly where gravity helps at the bottom, so it is a
  stretch-tolerance drill, not a tension drill.
- Kill the pectoralis minor line and the "more force in decline presses" aside. Keep it short.
- Link `press-declinado-mancuernas`, `press-declinado-barra`, `aperturas-planas-mancuernas`,
  `pullover-en-banco-declinado`, `rutinas-de-pecho-principiantes`, `anatomia-del-pecho`.

### 6. Decline barbell press

- The batch's hub for the decline press. Bench at 15-30°, feet secured, grip about shoulder width,
  bar to the lower chest, unrack with the bar over the lower chest and never over the throat, spotter
  or safeties. 3-4 x 5-8, 2-3 min; 3 x 8-12 as the size variant.
- The honest case: shorter range, more load, sternal emphasis. No fibre-recruitment claim and no
  injury-prevention promise. The archive's wrong-versus-right bar-path pair becomes a three-row
  comparison table, not images.
- Link `press-declinado-mancuernas`, `aperturas-mancuernas-banco-declinado`, `press-banca-barra`,
  `press-inclinado-barra`, `fondos-pecho`, `ejercicios-pecho`, `anatomia-del-pecho`,
  `rutinas-pecho`, `rutina-de-especializacion-de-pecho`.

### 7. Flat dumbbell fly

- Flat bench, arms open to shoulder level or slightly below, elbows fixed at 10-15°, feet on the
  floor, 2-3 x 12-15, 60-90 s. Light load, long range.
- The thesis: resistance peaks at the stretch and vanishes at the top, so it is a stretch tool, never
  a strength lift. Kill the "pec deck doubles involvement" figure and the tree-hugging cue.
- Link `press-plano-mancuernas`, `aperturas-mancuernas-banco-declinado`, `aperturas-peck-deck`,
  `cruces-polea`, `maquinas-pesos-libres-al-entrenar-el-pecho`, `rutinas-pecho`.

### 8. Flat dumbbell press

- Flat bench, feet planted, dumbbells over the mid chest, palms facing the feet, press up and slightly
  in, lower until the upper arms are level with the torso. 3-4 x 8-12, 90-120 s.
- Own the missing hub between `mancuernas-entrenando-pecho` and `press-banca-barra`: each side its own
  range, the load ceiling, the kick-up setup, the errors. Kill the decline paragraph, the striae myth
  and the joining-bells claim.
- Link `aperturas-planas-mancuernas`, `press-banca-barra`,
  `press-de-banca-mas-efectivo-con-mancuernas-o-con-barra`, `mancuernas-entrenando-pecho`,
  `ejercicios-pecho`, `anatomia-del-pecho`, `rutinas-pecho`, `tecnica-ejecucion-ejercicios`.

### 9. High cable crossover

- High pulleys, one foot forward, torso leaned 15-30° so the line of pull runs through the chest,
  elbows fixed at 10-15°, hands meet in front of the lower chest, 1 s pause, 3 x 12-15, 60-90 s.
- Correct the emphasis: high pulleys load the sternal portion. Leaning changes the line of pull, not
  the region. Sell tension time through the arc, not the squeeze at the shortest position.
- Link `aperturas-peck-deck`, `aperturas-inclinadas-polea`, `ejercicios-en-polea-ventajas`,
  `maquinas-pesos-libres-al-entrenar-el-pecho`, `ejercicios-pecho`, `anatomia-del-pecho`,
  `rutinas-pecho`, `fallo-muscular`.

### 10. Pec deck fly

- Both machine families: elbow-pad machines and handle-only machines. Seat so the upper arms align
  with the shoulders or slightly below, 1-2 s hold, 3 x 10-15, 60-90 s. Add the shoulder caveat: the
  load runs from external to internal rotation, so seat height and range matter.
- Fix the mixed setup instruction, keep it practical, drop the bodybuilding credit.
- Link `cruces-polea`, `aperturas-planas-mancuernas`, `maquinas-pesos-libres-al-entrenar-el-pecho`,
  `ejercicios-pecho`, `programa-de-pecho-con-sistema-de-piramide`,
  `mejores-ejercicios-en-maquina-de-musculacion`, `rutinas-pecho`.
