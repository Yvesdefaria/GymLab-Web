   repeats page 1's scheme and an "efectividad 100 %" routine. It becomes the hub: what a cut changes
   (calories, cardio, load retention), what it does not, three rules, and links to the eleven published
   cutting pages. No sample routine.
2. **Three pages cannot share the same 6/12/25 triset.** Pages 1, 3 and 6 use it. Page 1 owns the
   eight-week high-frequency plan with triset and compound sets; page 6 owns the three-day
   opposing-muscle superset plan with density progression; the hub only links them.
3. **"Serie compuesta" is a mislabel.** What page 1 prescribes is a superset/triset, not a compound
   set in the mechanical sense. Keep the term once, explain it, and use superset and triset in the rest.
4. **Page 1's week 8 leaves one rest day.** Keep the 8-week wave and every table, but publish the
   deload option and say when to use it, instead of burying it in the weeks 6-7 note.
5. **Loading is never defined.** Every cutting page raises volume and none says how load moves. Pages
   1, 2, 6, 7, 8 and 10 must state one rule: keep the load, add reps or density until the target set is
   clean, then deload.
6. **Page 2 promises a guide and delivers two unconnected tables.** Keep the "wrong day" versus
   "right day" comparison as one table, then publish the weekly split, the progression and the cardio
   dose. Drop "más pierna que el 70 % de los usuarios de tu gimnasio" and the "sobre todo en las
   mujeres" aside.
7. **Torso/pierna is misfiled in the archive.** It is a volume and strength split sitting in the
   cutting block. It stays in this block because the checklist files it here, and the page says
   plainly that the split works in a surplus or a deficit; it does not duplicate the volume hub.
8. **Page 4's advanced six-day variant does not exist.** The archive gives three, four and six days in
   the text and only four days of template. Publish the 3/4-day maps and say the sixth day is the
   fourth session repeated with the light week's load; do not invent a sixth template.
9. **Tabata is a cycle-ergometer study, not a barbell wod.** Page 5 keeps 20 s / 10 s x 8, states the
   original context (about 170 % of VO2max on a bike), gives the bodyweight version and says that a
   loaded front squat needs a load that allows the eight rounds. Drop "pasaréis por el infierno",
   vomiting as normal, and the metabolism acceleration claim.
10. **100 reps at 20-30 % of a 10RM does not add up.** Page 9 keeps the 100-rep set, the "rest as many
    seconds as reps remain" cluster rule, the two-week cap, twice a week and two to three days between
    sessions, and corrects the load to a weight that allows about 50-60 reps fresh (roughly 30-40 % of
    the 1RM). It states that 600-700 reps per session is not low volume.
11. **Five disconnected sessions are not a plan.** Page 7 keeps the 30-minute template and its rep
    blocks, picks one weekly order, and adds the density progression. The other four sessions become
    options inside the same week, not separate plans.
12. **Page 8 is a short shock block, not high frequency for legs.** Keep the five-day push/pull/legs
    rotation and the drop sets written as numbers, fix the 1-3 week versus 4 week contradiction, and

13. **Page 10 is a twelve-week plan with no progression.** Keep the five days, the 4x12/8/8/8 basics,

14. **No page prescribes under-eating.** These are training plans; the calorie side links
