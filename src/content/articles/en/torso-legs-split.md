This is the simplest split after full body: one day for the upper half, one for the lower half. With four days that gives each muscle two sessions a week without repeating the same movement, and it lets you load squat, deadlift, bench press and row for real.

The page sits in the cutting block because the routine is popular in that phase, but it is not a deficit-only plan. In a surplus it builds mass; in a deficit it keeps what you have. The [cutting routines](/en/cutting-routines/) index collects the plans for the cut and the [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) index the ones for the other end.

## The split

With three days you alternate torso and legs: one week two torso sessions and one leg session, the next two leg sessions and one torso session. With four days you do two of each. With six, three of each.

The six-day plan adds a third torso session and another leg session. The sixth session repeats the fourth with the light week's load: same work, less weight, because frequency is already high.

## The rep wave

Eight weeks with the same structure and reps in a wave: down from 10 to 7 and back to 10. When reps come down, the load goes up, even if only a little. It is the usual rule applied to the split: fewer reps, more weight; more reps, less weight.

Exercises with the same letter run as a superset: one set of A1, no rest, one of A2, then the week's rest. The Pendlay row starts with the torso parallel to the floor and the bar on the ground every rep. On back, biceps and abs, hold the contraction for one or two seconds before lowering.

## The cardio

After weights, 30 to 50 minutes at a moderate intensity, between 60 and 75 % of maximum heart rate. Three or four times a week. The reason to place it after is simple: weights burn glucose and glycogen, and the aerobic work that follows uses more fat as fuel.

If the deficit bites and cardio costs you strength, cut minutes before sessions. Other plans in the block take the opposite route: fewer cardio sessions and a minutes ladder.

## The light week

When week 8 ends, take a full week of very light training, three days. If you are wrecked, take it as full rest. That week's load is what the sixth session of the six-day plan uses.

Then return to week 1 with the load you have gained. If week 4 reps do not come out clean, repeat the wave lighter instead of skipping the light week.

## When to change plans

Eight weeks is a reasonable frame. If you want a longer plan, with blocks and another progression, the [twelve-week hypertrophy plan](/en/twelve-week-hypertrophy-plan/) spreads the work over twelve weeks. If the goal is maximal strength, the [strength routines](/en/strength-routines/) drop reps and lengthen rests.

To pick exercises for each half, the [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/) and [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) indexes have variants by level and equipment.
