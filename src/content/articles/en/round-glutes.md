The question of how to round your glutes has a less glamorous answer than the headline suggests: build muscle and, if there is surplus fat, lose it. The rest is genetics and patience. What you do control is load, because the gluteus maximus is a powerful muscle that responds to weights most routines never reach.

## Shape is decided by the frame

The pelvis sets the width; the gluteus maximus sets the volume; the fat around it sets the definition. You cannot change bone, and no kickback will do it. You can add muscle mass and you can lower body fat, in that order. When both move, the shape appears; when fat drops with no muscle underneath, the result looks flatter, not rounder. Two people can follow the same routine and end up with different silhouettes, because the frame they started with is not the same.

## The three glutes and what each one moves

The **gluteus maximus** extends the hip: it drives the pelvis forward when you stand from a chair. The **gluteus medius** moves the leg away from the body and stabilises the pelvis when you walk. The **gluteus minimus** rotates and helps support the hip. The guide to [hip and glute anatomy](/en/hip-anatomy/) covers attachments and functions.

There is no sleeping muscle to wake up here. If an exercise does not feel like it is working the glute, it is almost always a matter of load, range or technique, not mind-muscle connection.

## Why ankle weights run out fast

An ankle weight adds 5 to 10 kilos and puts them far from the hip axis. It works for the first weeks, but the glute moves much heavier loads once the pattern is learned. With the ankle strap the ceiling arrives early, and the usual fix is more reps, which is not the best route to mass. The [cable hip extension](/en/hip-extension-glutes/) lets you raise the weight little by little and keeps the same movement.

## The progression that works

The order is always the same: bodyweight first, then the bar.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glute bridge | 3 | 12-15 |
| Hip thrust | 3 | 8-12 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 | 8-10 |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 | 8-12 |

Before the first set, five minutes on the bike, fifteen bodyweight bridges and one hip thrust set with an empty bar. The specific warm-up for this session is in [how to warm up for glutes](/en/glute-workout-warm-up/). Pick a load that leaves two reps in reserve and rest 90 seconds between sets. When you reach the top of the range across all three sets, add 2.5 or 5 kilos. If the Romanian deadlift leaves your back tired, shorten the range and check that the hips travel back before the torso.

## Fat does not come off with kickbacks

No exercise burns the fat sitting over the muscle it works. Fat is mobilised systemically when energy balance is negative, and where it shows first is decided by genetics. If the goal is fat loss, a moderate deficit and enough protein protect muscle while you drop. If the goal is mass, you need calories and years of work, not weeks.

## What to expect and when

Strength rises in four to six weeks; size takes months to show and depends on total load. Nobody can promise a date or an exact shape, because every starting point is different. What you can measure is the bar: if the hip thrust goes up, the stimulus is arriving. Take a photo every six weeks under the same light and keep a note of the load; the mirror is a poor record and the scale says little about where the change happened.
