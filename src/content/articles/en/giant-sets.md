Giant sets run from the hardest movements to the easiest, so the muscle fibres are worked from different angles. They work best for large muscle groups and for the abs, because those areas tolerate volume and back-to-back work.

## What they are and how they differ from supersets

A giant set is four or more different exercises chained with no rest between them. Two exercises in a row are a [superset](/en/supersets/), and three are a tri-set. The difference is the number of movements, not the intent.

You can run a giant set for one muscle, for example dumbbell flyes, bench press, pec deck and dips. You can also mix muscles, for example bench press, barbell row, pulldowns and push-ups. Two areas where they work especially well are the shoulders and the abs. If you want a short example to close back day, [the back superset finisher](/en/back-superset-finisher/) uses the same principle with two exercises.

## Shoulder giant set

This giant set works all three heads of the deltoid. Use relatively light dumbbells and chase the pump: with a heavy load you will not reach the end of the block.

Before you start, do a few sets of a basic compound exercise with normal rest. That warms the shoulder and keeps the block from turning into a fight with the weight.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rear raise | 4 | 10 |
| Lateral raise | 4 | 10 |
| Front raise | 4 | 10 |
| Dumbbell upright row | 4 | 10 |

The four sets in the table are four passes through the block. Rest between exercises is short; the long rest comes after the fourth pass.

## Abs giant set

If the first exercise with straight legs feels very hard, bend your knees. You can also adjust the machine resistance to make sure you hit the planned reps.

Place a mat near the cable station so you do not lose time between movements. Rest once you finish the four exercises.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg raise with straight arms | 3 | 15 |
| Cable crunch | 3 | 15 |
| Crunch with feet on the floor | 3 | 15 |
| Oblique crunch | 3 | 15 |

## How long to rest between giant sets

Inside the block, rest 30 to 60 seconds between exercises. Between blocks, take 3 to 10 minutes depending on the load: the heavier the block, the closer to the top end. The guide to [rest between sets](/en/rest-between-sets/) helps you fit that number into the rest of the routine.

A chest example with 30 seconds between exercises and 6 minutes at the end:

1. Bench press: 8 reps.
2. Incline bench press: 10 reps.
3. Pec deck: 12 reps.
4. Cable crossover: 12 reps.
5. Push-ups: as many as you can.

Rest 30 seconds after each of the first four exercises and 6 minutes after the push-ups.

## How many to do per workout

You can do up to five giant sets per workout. That is a ceiling, not a quota: if you have never used the format, do not start at the maximum.

Many experienced bodybuilders switch from conventional routines to giant sets and supersets when those stop producing results. Others train them all year. Either way the block sits inside the rest of the plan, and [high-intensity training](/en/high-intensity-training/) techniques are the next step when you need more stimulus. Giant sets appear among the [Weider principles](/en/weider-principles/) as a shaping technique for attacking a weak area.

> If you do not have much time for a long workout, supersets and giant sets can be a good option.

## How to run them in a busy gym

The classic advice is to do them in an empty gym, because you need several machines and weights and you cannot wait between exercises. In a busy gym, that waiting is exactly what breaks the block.

Pick a block that fits one station or two machines next to each other. If the plan needs four different machines, go at a quiet hour or ask whether you can reserve the equipment. You can also build the block with a pair of dumbbells and a bench, which take little space and do not depend on someone getting up.

If a machine is taken, swap the exercise for an equivalent one at the same station before you break the rest. The giant set works through continuity: if you must change a machine, pick a movement for the same area and keep the order of difficulty.
