The quads, hamstrings and glutes are close to half of the body's muscle mass. When that half falls behind, the rest of the plan does not make up for it. The answer is giving the legs more frequency and more volume for a short block.

## What a specialization block does

A specialization block raises the frequency of one muscle group and drops the rest of the plan to maintenance. Here the legs go from one session a week to two, with different volumes: the first chases strength and the second accumulates reps. The block lasts a few weeks and is not a permanent plan.

What changes is how many times a week the muscle is stimulated and how many sets it accumulates; genetics and muscle length stay where they are. If the quads do not respond to two sessions, the problem is usually load or recovery, not a missing trick.

## Day 1: forced reps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 3 | 6 to 10 |
| Leg press | 3 | 6 to 10 |
| Hack squat | 3 | 6 to 10 |
| Leg extension | 4 | 6 to 10 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 | 6 to 10 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 6 to 10 |

Forced reps are the hardest part of the block: you reach failure on your own and a partner helps you finish the last two or three reps of each set. They are not required to grow and they are not used on every exercise. They make sense on the squat, the leg press and the hack squat, where the partner can follow the path without loading the bar.

On the hamstring and extension exercises, the heavy weight already leaves little room. If you do not have someone who knows how to help, stop at failure and extend the set with a pause before the last rep.

## Day 5: pump work

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 3 | 20 |
| Leg press | 3 | 20 |
| Hack squat | 3 | 20 |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 | 20 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 | 20 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 20 |

The second day drops the weight and goes up to twenty reps. The Bulgarian split squat appears here because it is hard even with little weight, and the goal is to accumulate work without the joint fatigue of the heavy sets. It is the day that leaves the most pump and the one that interferes least with recovery from the first.

## How to pick the weight and when to stop

Warm-up sets do not count and do not go to failure. Pick the working weight so that you reach failure on the prescribed rep: if the table asks for 6 to 10 and you get to 10 with room left, the weight is low; if you stop at 6 with broken technique, it is high.

The reference for adding weight is the first exercise. If the day 1 squat comes out complete, with the same weight on every set and clean technique, go up. If the last set falls apart, repeat the weight the following week.

## After the block

The block ends when the squat stops climbing or when sleep and legs no longer recover between sessions. Then go back to the [light and heavy split](/en/light-heavy-leg-routine/) or to your previous plan with a single leg session.

The extra volume does not hold itself up: keep two leg sessions for a couple more weeks and cut the sets in half. After that, the normal plan works again without losing what you gained.
