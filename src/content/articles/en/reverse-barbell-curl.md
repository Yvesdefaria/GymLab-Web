Almost every curl turns the palm up at some point. This one does not. The bar is gripped overhand from the first inch and stays that way, and that detail changes which muscle does the work.

## The pronated grip

With the palm facing down, the forearm cannot supinate. Supination is the biceps' mechanical advantage: when the radius turns, the muscle shortens better and moves more weight. Without that turn the biceps still flexes the elbow, but it shares the work with the brachioradialis, which attaches to the radius and helps flex with the hand pronated.

That is the real point of the exercise: it trains a function the supinated curl leaves aside. The [anatomy of the biceps and forearm](/en/biceps-forearm-anatomy/) shows where each one attaches and why the share changes with the turn.

## The position

Standing with the feet at hip width and the knees slightly bent. The bar is gripped overhand at shoulder width, with the thumb closed around it. The arms start extended and the hands sit in front of the thighs.

The elbows stay at the sides. The bar rises to chest height, holds for one second at the top and lowers under control to full extension. The torso does not help: if it swings, the arm is no longer carrying the load.

[Wrist position in the biceps curl](/en/wrist-position-biceps-curl/) matters more here than in the supinated curl. With the palm down, a wrist bent backwards puts the bar far from the elbow and the joint pays for it. Neutral and firm through the whole range.

## Why it is not a forearm exercise

Precision helps here, because the label appears on many exercise cards. The reverse curl does not train the wrist flexors or the wrist extensors. Those muscles move the hand over the forearm, and in this exercise the wrist does not make that movement: it stays still.

What it trains is an elbow flexor, the brachioradialis, which also shows up in the hammer curl and the Zottman. Wrist work has its own exercises, and the [forearm exercises](/en/forearm-exercises/) order them by action.

That is why the reverse curl does not replace a forearm routine. It adds a different pattern to biceps day, and the [forearm routine](/en/forearm-routine/) remains the place for wrist flexion and extension.

## Grip width

A wider grip does not bring the bar closer to the body or increase the work of the brachioradialis. The bar stays where the hands hold it, and the distance to the elbow is set by the position of the arm, not by the space between the hands.

Width changes two things: shoulder comfort and wrist stress. A grip slightly wider than the shoulders usually feels better for the pronated wrist; a very narrow one crowds the elbows and adds no stimulus. Choose it for comfort and keep it for the whole set.

## How much weight and how many sets

Three sets of 8 to 12 reps with 90 seconds of rest and a one-second pause at the top. The dose is a recommendation from this page; the original gives no numbers.

The weight is light, lower than in the supinated barbell curl. The pronated palm removes mechanical advantage and the wrist tolerates less load; if the bar rises with momentum or the wrist bends, the weight is too much. Progress in clean reps before adding plates.

## The mistakes

- **Grip too wide.** It opens the elbows and adds no work for the brachioradialis.
- **Wrist bent backwards.** It lengthens the lever and leaves the tension in the joint.
- **Elbows drifting forward.** The shoulder pushes and the curl turns into a pull.
- **Starting with the weight of a normal curl.** Pronation allows less load and shows it in the first set.
- **Cutting the descent.** Without control on the way down, the work stays on the way up.

## Where it fits

It goes after the supinated curls, when the elbow is warm and weight is not the goal. It also works as the first exercise of a forearm day, before the wrist work.

The [barbell curl](/en/barbell-curl/) sets the reference for load and position, and the [Zottman curl](/en/zottman-curl/) covers the same muscle from the other side: it rises supinated and lowers pronated. [Grip strength training](/en/grip-strength-training/) closes the hand work this curl does not cover.
