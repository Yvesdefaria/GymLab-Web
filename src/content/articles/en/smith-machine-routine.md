## What changes when the bar is guided

On a Smith machine the bar runs on two fixed rails. You push or pull, the path does not drift and the weight does not tip. That lets you add plates and take a set to failure without thinking about balance.

The cost is that the stabilisers do less work. The machine does not replace the barbell and the dumbbells; it complements them on the heavy sets and on the exercises where you want to reach the limit safely. It is one piece of a [volume routine](/en/volume-routines/), not the whole plan.

## The four-day routine

Four sessions, one block each. Day 1 adds up to 26 sets; the other three stay at 20. On your first week at this volume, do two thirds of the sets and add the rest only while the last one stays clean. Finish with 15 or 20 minutes of easy cardio, without turning it into a second weights session.

### Day 1: chest and triceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline press on the Smith machine | 4 | 6-12 |
| Barbell bench press | 3 | 8-10 |
| Decline press | 3 | 10-12 |
| Cable crossovers | 4 | 10-15 |

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close-grip bench press on the Smith machine | 4 | 6-12 |
| EZ-bar French press | 2 | 8-10 |
| Overhead dumbbell triceps extension | 2 | 10-12 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 4 | 10-15 |

> **Special sets:** at failure, keep going with partial reps on the incline press and the close-grip press. On the decline press and the cable pushdown, strip 20 to 30 % of the weight and keep repping until you hit failure again.

### Day 2: legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine squat | 4 | 6-12 |
| Leg press | 2 | 8-10 |
| Leg extension | 2 | 10-12 |
| Smith machine Romanian deadlift | 4 | 6-12 |
| Seated leg curl | 4 | 10-15 |
| Standing calf raise | 2 | 15-20 |
| Seated calf raise | 2 | 15-20 |

> **Special sets:** a 20 to 30 % drop set on the squat and the Romanian deadlift. If the session runs long, cut the leg curl before the squat.

### Day 3: shoulders and traps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine overhead press | 4 | 6-12 |
| Smith machine upright row | 2 | 8-10 |
| Lateral raises | 2 | 10-12 |
| Rear delt raises | 4 | 10-15 |
| Smith machine behind-the-neck shrug | 4 | 6-12 |
| Dumbbell shrugs | 4 | 10-15 |

> **Special sets:** a 20 to 30 % drop set on the upright row and the behind-the-neck shrug.

### Day 4: back and biceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine row, pronated grip | 3 | 6-12 |
| Smith machine row, supinated grip | 3 | 6-12 |
| Cable lat pulldown | 4 | 8-10 |
| Barbell curl | 3 | 6-12 |
| Smith machine drag curl | 3 | 10-15 |
| Incline dumbbell curl | 2 | 8-10 |
| Hammer curl | 2 | 10-15 |

> **Special sets:** a 20 to 30 % drop set on the pronated row and the drag curl.

The **lat pulldown** is a vertical pull for the back and the **cable pushdown** is an elbow extension for the triceps. They share a cable stack in most gyms and nothing else.

## Why day 4 is trimmed

As printed, day 4 adds up to 28 sets: 16 for the back and 12 for the biceps. With one-minute rests there is no way to hold the weight on the rows, and the last curl swings.

The table above leaves 20 sets and keeps the shape of the day: two rows, one vertical pull and four curls. Out go the T-bar row, the seated cable row, the straight-arm pulldown and the Scott curl. If you have energy left, take an extra rep on the rows rather than an extra exercise.

## How the load moves

One rule for all four sessions: when the last set is clean, with the prescribed reps and no body English, add the smallest plate the machine takes. If the last set breaks early, repeat the weight next week.

The special techniques do not change the progression rule. Partials and drop sets close a heavy set; they are not a way to cover a weight you do not own yet. Before adding sets to this plan, check what [training volume](/en/training-volume/) says about the work you can recover from.

On chest and triceps days it pays to alternate the Smith machine with free weights now and then; [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/) and [triceps routines](/en/triceps-routines/) give you variants so you are not pressing the same way for months. Legs are the same story: the [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) add patterns the rails do not allow.

## Ballistic work: the other use of the machine

A guided bar lets you release the weight at the end of the range without losing it, which opens the door to ballistic training. That means 3 to 5 reps per set at 30 to 50 % of your maximum, never to failure, chasing speed rather than a pump.

It sits outside this volume plan: use it at the start of a session with a fresh system, or on its own day. The detail is in [ballistic training](/en/ballistic-training/).

## When to change the plan

This block works for six to ten weeks. After that, the same rails and the same exercises stop surprising the muscle and the progression stalls. Change the order, alternate the Smith machine with free weights or move to a plan with a different split for a month.

What does not change is the base: heavy sets, controlled reps and a load that goes up when it should. The Smith machine is a convenient way to do that safely, not a shortcut.
