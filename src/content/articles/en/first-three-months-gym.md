The first months decide whether the gym becomes a habit or a memory. Early enthusiasm pushes people to do too much, and the body answers with soreness, fatigue and technique that breaks the moment the weight goes up. This plan runs the other way: movement first, load after. The [beginner volume programme](/en/beginner-volume-program/) is the next step.

## Month one: learning the movement

Three days a week, Monday, Wednesday and Friday. The whole body in every session, one exercise per group and the same movement for four weeks: a repeatable rep is what lets you correct it.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 3 | 10 |
| Front pulldown | 3 | 10 |
| Bench press | 3 | 10 |
| Barbell military press | 3 | 10 |
| Cable triceps extensions | 3 | 10 |
| Standing barbell curl | 3 | 10 |
| Crunches | 3 | 10-12 |

Before the first set, five to ten minutes on the treadmill or the bike at an easy pace. At the end, twenty minutes of moderate cardio. The order runs from largest to smallest: big groups first, while energy lasts, and small ones at the end.

The weight has to let you reach ten reps with some effort and no loss of form. If the last set looks like the first, the weight was too light.

## Month two: same days, new exercises

The second month keeps the three days and changes the exercises. The goal is to learn new paths and feel the muscle from another angle, with a maximum rest of 45 seconds between sets.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg press | 3 | 10 |
| Seated cable row | 3 | 10 |
| Dumbbell bench press | 3 | 10 |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | 3 | 10 |
| Lying triceps extension | 3 | 10 |
| Alternating dumbbell curl | 3 | 10 |
| Leg raises | 3 | 12-15 |

The warm-up and the twenty minutes of cardio at the end stay. The weight follows the same rule: ten comfortable reps with effort, no bouncing and no jerking the lower back.

## Month three: the first split

The third month moves to four weekly sessions, Monday and Thursday for legs and shoulders, Tuesday and Friday for back, chest and arms. Each group is trained twice a week and the weekend stays free.

The original list ran to nine exercises on one day and ten on the other. Here it stops at seven, abs included: that is the volume a two-month beginner can recover from before the next session. Out go the machine rear-delt fly, the one-arm triceps extension and the concentration curl: they repeat work the big movements already cover.

### Day 1: Monday and Thursday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guided machine squat | 3 | 10 |
| Leg extensions | 3 | 10 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 10 |
| Calf raises | 3 | 12 |
| Dumbbell shoulder press | 3 | 10 |
| Lateral raises | 3 | 10 |
| Crunches | 3 | 10-12 |

### Day 2: Tuesday and Friday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close-grip pulldown | 3 | 10 |
| Dumbbell row | 3 | 10 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 3 | 10 |
| Dumbbell flyes | 3 | 10 |
| Bench dips | 3 | 10 |
| Preacher curl | 3 | 10 |
| Reverse crunches | 3 | 10-12 |

Both days open with five to ten minutes of easy cardio. The order inside the session stays largest to smallest, big movements first and abs last.

## The load rule

Three sets per exercise, in all three months. The first set fixes the weight: if the last rep comes up clean with two left in reserve, the weight is right.

When the last set of an exercise comes up complete and with good form, add 2.5 kilos on the upper body and 5 kilos on the lower body the next session. If you miss the ten reps on the third set, repeat the weight: load is not won in a week.

After four to six weeks on the same structure, take a deload week with half the sets at 60 % of the weight. The body needs that breath to keep answering, and technique improves when fatigue drops.

## What changes and what does not

Month one changes technique: same exercises, same order and a weight that allows repeats. Month two changes the exercises and keeps the three-day structure. Month three changes the split, with four sessions and two groups per day. What stays is the number of sets and the rule for adding weight.

That order avoids the classic beginner mistake: changing everything at once and having nothing to compare. One month, one variable.
