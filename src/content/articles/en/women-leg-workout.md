The original plan was right about the essentials: two leg days, two upper-body days and a base phase followed by a harder one. What was missing was day 2 of the first phase, which appeared empty. It is complete here, and both phases are labelled the same way.

## Who it is for

For someone with around twelve months of training who is comfortable with the front squat, the deadlift and lunges. With less experience you can run phase 1 with light loads and help to check technique; the load progression comes later, not first.

## The week

Two leg sessions and two upper-body sessions. The gap between the two leg days is two or three days, which is what a large muscle group needs to recover.

## The warm-up

Five minutes on a bike or treadmill at an easy pace, then hip and ankle mobility. After that, ramp-up sets: empty bar, 50 per cent and 75 per cent of the working weight, three or four reps each. The warm-up prepares you without draining you.

## Phase 1: four weeks of base

Straight sets with margin. The first phase builds the pattern and leaves two reps in reserve on the hardest set. The front squat and the heels-elevated squat train the same pattern from two angles, and the Romanian deadlift covers the hinge.

### Day A: squat

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front squat | 4 | 6 |
| Heels-elevated squat | 3 | 8 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 10 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 | 10 |
| Standing calf raise | 3 | 12 |

### Day B: hinge

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conventional deadlift | 4 | 6 |
| Dumbbell lunges | 3 | 10 per leg |
| Leg extension | 3 | 12 |
| Back extension | 3 | 12 |
| Seated calf raise | 3 | 15 |

## Phase 2: four weeks of pairs

The same movements, now paired. Each pair runs without rest between the two exercises, with ten seconds to change the weight. Then two or three minutes of rest and another round.

### Day A: squat

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Front squat | 4 | 6 |
| A2. Heels-elevated squat | 4 | 8 |
| B1. Lying leg curl | 4 | 8 |
| B2. Romanian deadlift | 4 | 10 |
| C1. Standing calf raise | 3 | 12 |

### Day B: hinge

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Conventional deadlift | 4 | 6 |
| A2. Dumbbell lunges | 4 | 10 |
| B1. Leg extension | 4 | 6 |
| B2. Back extension | 4 | 12 |
| C1. Seated calf raise | 3 | 15 |

Phase 2 is demanding. In the first week do three rounds instead of four and add the fourth when you arrive with margin. If the reps do not come out clean, lower the weight before cutting sets.

Both phases move the same work. What changes is density: in phase 1 every set rests as long as the body asks, two to three minutes. In phase 2 the rest shrinks inside the pair and stays long after it. That change is enough to make the same work cost more without adding exercises.

## Load and progression

In phase 1 leave two reps in reserve on the hardest set and in phase 2 leave one. When you complete every rep with that reserve, add 2.5 kilos to the bar or the machine. If you fall short, keep the weight for another week.

Technique comes before the number. A front squat that pitches forward or a deadlift that loses a neutral back calls for less load, not more. The [front squat](/en/front-squat/) and [Romanian deadlift](/en/romanian-deadlift/) guides have the execution cues.

## The cardio

The original asked for thirty minutes of aerobic work after every weights session. That is not needed. Two or three sessions of twenty to thirty minutes at moderate intensity, after weights or on separate days, support the work without taking strength out of the legs.

If the goal is fat loss, the deficit does the work and cardio helps. The [cardio and weights for women](/en/cardio-and-weights-for-women/) guide orders the combination.

## After the eight weeks

When phase 2 ends, take a deload week: same exercises, half the sets and a weight that leaves four reps in reserve. It gets you to the next block willing rather than sore.

Then repeat phase 1 with the load you have gained. If the front squat stalled for two blocks in a row, change the order and put it after the deadlift, or cut the sets and raise the quality of each rep.
