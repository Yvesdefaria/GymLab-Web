Some weeks everything lands at once: dinners, commitments, little sleep and half-finished sessions. The result shows in the mirror and in the sets. This block exists to recover the lost ground in a few days, not to become your routine.

The rotation is A, B, C, A, B. Day A trains the upper body with pushing movements, B with pulling movements and C the legs. With that order each upper-body muscle works twice a week and the legs work once.

## How the drop sets read

The notation **4 x 10,10,10,10+10** means four sets of 10 reps, and on the last one, after reaching failure, you lower the weight and take 10 more straight away. The first three sets run with normal rest and without hitting absolute failure; the fourth carries the double push.

The drop only applies to the last set and only on the exercises marked with **+**. Rest one minute between sets and two to three minutes between exercises. The method is covered in detail in the [drop sets](/en/drop-sets/) guide.

Push and pull repeat in the second half of the week, so the chest works again when the shoulder has already recovered. The legs take the whole load in a single session and need the rest of the week to come back from it.

## Day A: push

Eight exercises. The only warm-up is one set of 20 reps on the first exercise with a light weight.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine bench press | 4 | 10,10,10,10+10 |
| Incline press on the plate-loaded machine | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Machine flyes | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Seated dumbbell press | 4 | 10,10,10,10+10 |
| Lateral raises | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Seated cable reverse flyes | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| French press supersetted with close-grip bench press | 4 | 10 |
| Cable pushdowns | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |

## Day B: pull

Same numbers and the same scheme, with abs at the end. The obliques run back to back, with no rest between sides.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable front pulldowns | 4 | 10,10,10,10+10 |
| V-grip pulldowns | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Seated cable row | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Barbell curl | 4 | 10,10,10,10+10 |
| Simultaneous dumbbell curl | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Incline bench reverse curl | 4 | 15-20 |
| Floor obliques | 3 | 20 per side |
| Cable crunches | 4 | 15-20 |

Abs go at the end, when the rest of the session is done, so they do not drain the big lifts. Two or three exercises are enough; adding more takes time away from recovery.

## Day C: legs

Legs train once a week in this block, so the session is long and complete. The calves close with a drop of their own.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 4 | 10 |
| Leg press | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Dumbbell lunges | 3 | 10 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Seated leg curl | 3 | 10,10,10+10 |
| Machine abductors | 3 | 12 |
| Calf press | 3 | 15,15,15+15 |
| Seated calf machine | 3 | 15,15,15+15 |

## The cardio

After the weights, 20 to 30 minutes of cardio on every training day. There is no exception by body type: the block is already demanding and cardio is one more piece of the load, not an optional extra.

## How long the block lasts

One week is the minimum dose and four the maximum. Past that point, go back to your usual routine. Stretching it adds nothing: frequency and volume pile up and recovery breaks before adaptation does.

This routine asks for an advanced lifter. With less than two years of consistent training, the [training volume](/en/training-volume/) of this scheme will run you over. It also asks for eight hours of sleep and decent food; without that, the block turns into a week of fatigue.

Plan the exit before the first session: pick the routine you go back to and put the date in the calendar.

The exercises are a guide. Swapping pull-ups for pulldowns is fine; swapping pulldowns for deadlifts is not. If you repeat days A and B in the same week, you can vary the exercises on the second pass: presses on the first push day and flyes on the second, rows on the first pull day and pulldowns on the second.

## Where it fits in the plan

This is a shock block inside a longer phase. The base of that phase is usually a [volume routine](/en/volume-routines/), and the [cutting routines](/en/cutting-routines/) index lays out the alternatives when the goal is fat loss.

If you want intensity without the frequency, [high-intensity training](/en/high-intensity-training/) chases the same goal with fewer sessions. And if the block leaves you flat, the [overtraining](/en/overtraining/) guide lists the signs that mean it is time to cut it short.
