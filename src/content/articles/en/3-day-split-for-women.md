The original plan loaded four sets per exercise from day one and asked you to add weight until failure without saying where to start. A beginner cannot do both at once. This version keeps the order and the exercises, and adds the starting point that was missing.

## The week in the 3-day split

The leg day repeats on Monday and Thursday. The two sessions sit two days apart: Tuesday trains the upper body and Wednesday is rest. The upper body splits between Tuesday and Friday.

## Week one: the load reset

Three sets per exercise, not four. Leave three or four reps in reserve and use a weight that lets you do three or four more than the table asks for. The goal of week one is to learn the order and set the seat on every machine. Do not chase failure: this week is for repeating the session, not for draining yourself. With the seat and pad already set, week two does not lose minutes to adjustments. Four sets and the rep ladder arrive then, once the order comes without thinking about it.

Finish with reverse crunches, crunches and back extensions, two sets of 15 to 20. That is the abs and lower-back work the original placed at the end of day 3.

## The warm-up

Six minutes on a bike or rower, hip, shoulder and ankle mobility, and two ramp-up sets at half the working weight on the first exercise. On leg day, one set with the empty bar if you use a bar. The warm-up is the same on all three days; only the exercise you ramp up on changes.

## The load and the rest

One minute between sets and two on the basics. In week one, three or four reps in reserve. From week two to week four, four sets on the 20, 15, 12 and 10 ladder, adding weight on each set. The last set may finish one rep from failure; the earlier ones do not. If the last set finishes with more than two reps in reserve, the weight was too light: add some next session.

## How to progress over four weeks

Week two: add the fourth set and start the ladder. Week three: repeat the scheme with 2.5 kilos more on the first exercise of each day if the last set finished two reps from failure. Week four: same weight, same sets, and log everything. Week five: cut the sets in half and then build again.

After week four, change one exercise per day and restart the ladder. The structure lasts for months; the exercises wear out. Wednesday and the weekend are not lost days: they are part of the plan. If a week only allows two sessions, do both leg days or one leg day and one upper-body day; do not put the two leg days together.
