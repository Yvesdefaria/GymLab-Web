Four sessions let you train every group twice a week without stacking two back days. The original offers two ways to spread those four days and one exercise table for the first. Here are both splits, the full tables and the food side that supports the work.

## Two splits, two logics

The first split puts chest and back in the same session and leaves legs and arms for the next. It is an upper and lower layout: the large upper-body groups share a day, and the lower body takes the arms, which are small and do not compete for the same energy.

The second split separates push from pull. Chest, shoulders and triceps go together; legs, back and biceps do too. It is the push, pull and legs logic compressed into two sessions. If you want the push and pull plan with separate days for each pattern, it is in the [push-pull routine](/en/push-pull-routine/) guide.

## Monday and Thursday: upper body

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |
| Incline press | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |
| Close-grip pull-up | 3 | 8-10 |
| Dumbbell row | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |
| Seated barbell shoulder press | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |
| Dumbbell lateral raise | 3 | 10 |
| Lying calf raise | 3 | 10-12 |
| Seated calf raise | 3 | 12-15 |

Eight exercises and 24 sets. Chest opens the session and back comes in afterwards, so the second group arrives with some fatigue but without having drained the first. Close-grip pull-ups need at least eight clean reps; if they are not there, use a lat pulldown with the same grip.

## Tuesday and Friday: legs and arms

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline bench crunch | 2-3 | 40 |
| Incline knee raise | 2-3 | 30 |
| Squat | 4 | 10, 8, 6, 6 |
| Leg press | 3 | 12, 10, 8 |
| Leg curl | 3 | 10, 8, 8 |
| Barbell deadlift | 3 | 10, 8, 8 |
| Close-grip bench press | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |
| French press | 3 | 10, 8, 8 |
| Incline curl | 3 | 10, 8, 8 |
| Dumbbell curl | 3 | 10, 8, 6 |

Ten exercises and about 31 sets, the longest session in the plan. The squat takes four sets and the deadlift three, with the legs split between them. Abs open the table; if you prefer to close with them, change the order and leave both exercises for the end.

The second split shares those same exercises across two sessions: bench press, incline press and shoulder press land on the push day; the row, the pull-ups and the curls land on the pull and leg day. Calves and abs change day with the layout.

## How to read the descending reps

A cell like 10, 8, 6 means three sets: ten reps with the lightest load, eight with a little more and six with the heaviest. Weight goes up each set and reps go down. When the last set of six is clean with room left, the next session starts heavier on the first set.

On fixed-rep exercises, such as lateral raises at 3 x 10, the rule is the usual one: when all three sets come out with the same technique, add the smallest plate. Abs and calves live in high rep ranges and do not need failure on every set.

## Protein and meal frequency

The block's reference is 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilo of bodyweight per day, spread over four to six meals two or three hours apart. For an 80 kg person that is 128 to 176 grams a day, not a single figure.

Frequency matters less than the total, but eating every two or three hours helps reach that number without one giant meal. The [build muscle](/en/build-muscle/) guide develops the split and the surplus.

## Fitting the plan into the week

Both splits ask for four days and leave three free. Monday and Tuesday, Wednesday off, Thursday and Friday, with the weekend free is the easiest layout. The first two days sit together without repeating a muscle: the upper session takes chest, back and shoulders, and the lower session takes legs and arms. If you train Saturday instead of Friday, keep at least one day between the two upper or push sessions.

Chest and triceps get their own coverage in the [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/) and [triceps routines](/en/triceps-routines/) guides; legs, in [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/). To compare with other plans in the block, the [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) index lists them.
