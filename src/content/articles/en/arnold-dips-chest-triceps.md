Since the modern gym filled up with machines, bodyweight work lost ground. That is a shame, because parallel-bar dips are among the most effective chest exercises and bench dips close a triceps session better than most pushdowns.

Arnold Schwarzenegger used them often in his training. The text that records his routine describes three uses of the same movement, chest, strength and pump, plus a bench variation for the triceps.

## Why dips work

A dip is a shoulder flexion and an elbow extension with the body as the load. The more you lean the torso forward, the more the pectoral takes; the more upright the body stays, the more the triceps works. That change of angle lets you use one exercise for two goals and adjust it without changing machines.

## Parallel-bar dips for the pectorals

To emphasise the lower chest, dips are an alternative to the decline press. Set the hands wider than shoulder width and lean the torso forward to shift most of the tension to the pectorals.

The angle is everything. If you do not feel the chest working during the movement, you did not lean forward enough. When the hands come closer together and the body turns upright, the pectoral stops being the protagonist and the work shifts to the triceps.

## Parallel-bar dips for strength

Arnold used them to build strength in the chest and the arms. His bodyweight was not enough to fail between 6 and 8 reps, so he hung a heavy dumbbell from a lifting belt. Today there are machines that add weight with the same effect.

The strength set stays in that low range, with the torso leaning and no bouncing. If you cannot reach six reps with added weight, take weight off: technique comes before the number.

## Parallel-bar dips for a pump

Because he could do many reps with his own weight, dips served him well for pump sets. The problem appeared at the end of a session, when bodyweight was too much for the reps he wanted: a training partner then held his feet with the knees bent to take load off.

Assisted machines now do that job and let you choose how much weight to lift. They are useful for drop sets, where you lower the assistance set by set instead of the load.

## Bench dips for the triceps

To isolate the triceps, Arnold used bench dips. He set up perpendicular between two benches, feet on the bench in front and hands on the bench behind his body.

Lower until you feel the triceps stretch and come back by extending the elbows. If bodyweight is not enough, add one or more plates across the hips.

## Arnold's dip routine

The reference text presents it as a short chest and triceps session that uses dips as the only exercise. The numbers are the ones in the archive.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bodyweight parallel-bar dips, warm-up | 2 | 10 |
| Weighted dips, torso leaning forward, for chest | 4 | 6 to 8 |
| Bodyweight dips, torso leaning forward, for chest | 3 | To failure |
| Weighted dips, upright body, for triceps | 3 | 8 to 10 |
| Bodyweight dips, upright body, for triceps | 3 | To failure |
| Bench dips | 3 | 2 to 15 |

> **Note:** the warm-up does not count as work. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets and a little longer before the sets to failure.

## The prerequisite before you start

Before this routine you need a controlled dip with your bodyweight: eight to ten reps, full range, no bounce at the bottom. If you do not have that yet, start with bench dips or the assisted machine and earn those reps before adding weight.

## The shoulder rule

Lower only to the depth you control. Dips punish two things equally: bouncing at the bottom and chasing more depth than the joint allows. If the shoulder pinches or clicks, shorten the range, bring the hands slightly closer and try again with less weight.

Keep the shoulder blades down and the chest up throughout. When the shoulders rise toward the ears, the dip stops being a chest exercise.

## Fitting dips into your week

The full routine works as a single chest and triceps session, once a week. If you prefer to split it, use the leaning sets after the press and keep the upright sets for arm day. Bench dips are a good triceps finisher in any split.
