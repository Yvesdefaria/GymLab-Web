This is an advanced routine because of the training volume. One year in the gym does not make you advanced, and two do not either. Experience shows in how you apply intensity, not on the calendar.

## How the back width and thickness routine works

The split is simple: one day pushes width, the other pushes thickness. If you cannot do pull-ups because of a high body weight, use the assisted pull-up machine. If you clear the rep target easily, add weight.

A clear sign you are not advanced is asking whether you can add more sets. If you cannot complete a brutal back workout with this number of sets, the problem is not volume. You still do not know how to apply intensity.

## Day 1: width with pull-ups and pulldowns

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pull-ups | 4 | 8-10 |
| Front lat pulldowns | 3 | 8-10 |
| Behind the neck pulldowns | 3 | 8-10 |
| Deadlift | 3 | 12 |

[Pull-ups](/en/pull-ups/) open the session and set the tone. [Front lat pulldowns](/en/front-lat-pulldowns/) add vertical range with a load that is easier to regulate, and [behind the neck pulldowns](/en/behind-the-neck-lat-pulldowns/) complete the width work if your shoulder mobility allows.

## Day 2: thickness with heavy rows

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell row | 4 | 8-10 |
| Dumbbell row | 3 | 10-12 |
| Reverse grip barbell row | 3 | 8-10 |
| Barbell good mornings | 3 | 12 |

The [barbell row](/en/barbell-row/) is the main lift of the day. The [dumbbell row](/en/one-arm-dumbbell-row/) adds unilateral work and the reverse grip row changes the pulling angle. Good mornings close with the posterior chain.

## Rest, loads and how long to run it

You could train day one on a Monday and day two on a Thursday or Friday. That guarantees the 72 hours of muscle recovery growth needs.

Rest 1 to 1.5 minutes between sets and 2 to 4 minutes between exercises. Use the longer breaks to stretch the area you just trained.

The weights should let you reach near muscular failure on your sets. When the routine says "pull-ups: 4 x 8-10", it means four sets of 8 to 10 reps that finish close to failure.

Run the routine for a month and assess the results. After that, reducing back volume for full recovery makes sense.

Do not reach failure on every set or you will overload your nervous system, especially because you train your back again in the same week. If you want absolute failure, do it only at the end of the last set of each exercise. That does not mean the other sets are low intensity: few people can reach true muscular failure.

## Who this routine is for

It is meant for lifters with a training base who need more volume for a lagging back. If you are just starting, the volume will overwhelm you before your back gets the stimulus.

If you want fewer days or a different goal, the [back routines](/en/back-routines/) selection includes strength, definition and beginner plans.
