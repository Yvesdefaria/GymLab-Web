Growth hormone is sold as a switch. Reality is less theatrical: it rises with training, it does metabolic work, and it is not the main cause of muscle growth. What holds a six-week program together is the usual pair: progressive tension and enough food. This plan serves that, and the honest explanation comes first.

## What growth hormone does

The pituitary releases it in pulses, with the highest peak during sleep. In adults it acts on the liver, mobilises fat and takes part in tissue repair. Strength training raises it acutely, and that rise has been read as the explanation for growth. The reading falls short. Muscle grows from progressive mechanical tension, with protein and energy available, and a one-hour hormone peak does not replace months of overload.

The peak that follows a set does not decide the outcome of the session either. It works as a signal that the work was demanding.

## The week

Four sessions on alternate days. One possible layout: Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, then the cycle starts again. Exercises change halfway through: weeks 1 to 3 use one list and weeks 4 to 6 another with the same patterns.

## The three waves

Sets go up, reps go down and rest gets shorter. Each wave has its own way of moving past failure.

## The exercises

**Day 1, chest, triceps and abs.** Incline barbell press, dumbbell bench press, incline dumbbell flyes, cable crossovers, triceps pushdowns, lying barbell extensions, overhead rope extensions, hanging leg raises and shrugs.

**Day 2, quads, hamstrings and calves.** Squats, leg press, dumbbell lunges, leg extensions, Romanian deadlift, lying leg curl, standing calf raises and seated calf raises.

**Day 3, shoulders, traps and abs.** Barbell overhead press, dumbbell lateral raises, front raises, bent-over rear raises, barbell shrugs, dumbbell shrugs, reverse shrugs and cable shrugs.

**Day 4, back, biceps and forearms.** Wide-grip pulldowns, one-arm dumbbell row, T-bar row, straight-arm pulldowns, barbell curl, incline seated curl, alternating hammer curl, barbell wrist curl and reverse wrist curl.

Weeks 4 to 6 change the exercises and keep the layout. Day 1 moves to Smith machine bench press, incline dumbbell press, decline flyes, contractor flyes, close-grip bench press, overhead dumbbell extensions and rope pushdowns. Day 2 moves to leg press, Smith machine front squat, hack squat, leg extensions, dumbbell Romanian deadlift, seated leg curl and calves. Day 3 moves to dumbbell and Smith machine overhead presses, cable lateral raises, contractor reverse flyes, shrugs and incline-board leg raises. Day 4 moves to bent-over row, pulldowns, seated cable row, reverse-grip pulldowns, preacher curl, EZ-bar curl, cable curl and wrist curls.

Abs follow the same wave: 3 x 10-12 with 90 seconds in week 1, 4 x 12-15 with 60 in week 2 and 5 x 15-20 with 30 in week 3. Weeks 4 to 6 repeat it unchanged. Abs are trained at the end of days 1 and 3, after the main lifts.

## The techniques

**Forced reps.** Pick a load that leaves you 15 hard reps. When you hit failure, a partner helps you through two more. Do not change the load between sets: on the second and third you will reach failure earlier and the assist completes the fifteen.

**Drop sets.** At failure, cut the load by 25 or 30 % and keep going for as many reps as you can.

**Rest-pause.** At failure, wait 10 seconds and do another round with whatever is left.

Some of these techniques need a partner. On your own, [partial reps](/en/partial-reps/) cover the work after failure without help.

## Progression

The original plan carries no load rule, so give it one: when you complete the top of the rep range with clean technique on every set, add load in the next wave. Inside the wave the load stays put; sets go up and rest comes down. In week 3, with five sets and 30 seconds of rest, pick the load with all five sets in mind.

## What drives the result

No amino acid or supplement is needed to raise growth hormone: training already does it and the peak is not the goal. What drives the result is duller: progressive overload, 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kilo of body weight and a surplus of 200 to 300 kcal a day. The [how to gain weight](/en/how-to-gain-weight/) guide explains how to build that surplus with normal food. Sleep counts too: that is when the hormone peaks and when muscle repairs.

The [training volume](/en/training-volume/) of week 3 is high: five sets per exercise with 30 seconds of rest. If recovery falls short, the [muscle failure](/en/muscle-failure-training/) guide helps dose sets taken to the limit, and the [rest-pause](/en/rest-pause/) guide explains the technique in detail.
