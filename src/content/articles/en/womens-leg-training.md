You can run it twice a week, with at least 48 hours between sessions. The session starts with the squat and the leg extension, which are the most fatiguing, and ends with the calves. If you trained glutes with deadlifts the day before, change the order or drop a set: the hamstrings arrive tired for the leg curl.

## The full session

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Machine squat | 4 | 15 |
| Leg extension | 4 | 15 |
| Incline leg press | 3 | 15 |
| Hack squat | 3 | 15 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 12-15 |
| Calf raise | 3 | 15-20 |

## Warm-up

Five minutes on the bike at an easy pace, ten knee and hip circles, and two squat sets at the machine's lightest setting. On the leg press, one set with the feet on the platform and no plates. The goal is for the first working set not to be the test set.

## Station by station

### Machine squat

Place your shoulders under the pads, keep your back supported and set your feet shoulder-width apart. Lower under control until the thighs reach horizontal and stand without locking the knee. The usual mistake is rising onto the toes: if the heels lift, bring the feet closer or shorten the range. Start with a weight that leaves three reps in reserve.

### Leg extension

The roller sits on the ankle, not the instep, and the back stays against the pad. Extend fully and lower over three seconds. Its role is to add to the squat and leg press; the quads already get most of their stimulus from the big movements. The [leg extension machine](/en/leg-extension/) has its own guide. The mistake is driving with the hips to move more weight.

### Incline leg press

With the feet in the middle of the platform, the effort is shared between quads and glutes. Feet lower down give more quad; feet higher give more glute and hamstring; feet wider give more adductor. Lower as far as you can without the pelvis lifting off the backrest. If you have a back or knee injury, your doctor or physiotherapist decides which machine you can use; this page cannot give that clearance. The guide to the [incline leg press](/en/incline-leg-press/) expands the setup.

### Hack squat

Your shoulders stay fixed under the pads and your back against the rest. Lower to horizontal and stand without locking the knee. The further forward the feet, the more glute; the wider they are, the more adductor. The mistake is losing contact with the backrest at the bottom. The [hack squat](/en/hack-squat/) has its own setup guide.

### Lying leg curl

Face down, the hips stay on the bench and the ankles under the roller. Curl all the way and lower under control; do not let the weight drop. The mistake is lifting the hips to shorten the range. The page on the [lying leg curl](/en/lying-leg-curl/) explains the position.

### Calf raise

Toes on the platform, heels free, full range. Rise as high as you can, hold for a second and lower until you feel the stretch. The calf responds better to higher reps and a pause at the top than to maximal loads. The mistake is bouncing.

## How much weight and how much rest

Work with two or three reps in reserve. The first time, pick a weight that lets you complete the prescribed reps without reaching failure; if you do not know, start light and move up in the second session. Rest 90 seconds between sets of squat, leg press and hack squat, and 60 on the extension, leg curl and calves. The original table's one minute is too short for the first three.

## How to progress

When you close the top of the range across all sets with two reps in reserve, move up one machine step or add 2.5 kilos. If the last rep loses technique, keep the weight for a week. For the first four weeks, do three sets instead of four and leave three reps in reserve: the goal is learning each machine's setup, not accumulating fatigue.

## Two days, not five

Two sessions a week are enough to start and enough to maintain. If you want a third, separate it from the other two and reduce the squat volume. [Circuit training](/en/circuit-training/) is an alternative when time rules, and the [30-minute workout](/en/30-minute-workout/) trims the session to the essentials without losing the order.
