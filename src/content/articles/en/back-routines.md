A wide, muscular back gives your physique the **V-taper** bodybuilders and gym-goers chase. Bodybuilding is built on **proportions**, and a narrow back makes the rest of your physique look smaller than it is.

For back routines to work you need good planning and variety, with the best [**back exercises**](/en/back-exercises/) in your arsenal. If you are looking for effective back routines, you are in the right place.

## How to get more out of your back routines

**Back routines** should include exercises aimed at **width**, such as **pull-ups** and lat pulldowns, but also exercises aimed at density, such as rows.

You can **combine** both kinds in the same session, or train your back twice a week and devote one day to width and the other to density. The choice depends on your goal and your capacity to recover.

The back is also one of the hardest muscle groups for **beginners** to feel, because it needs a **full range of motion** to stretch and contract the muscle correctly.

A mind-muscle connection matters a great deal here. It takes time, but within a few months back day will become one of your favourites.

So let us start with our selection of back routines. Do not forget your **gym gloves**, because you are about to lift heavy.

> **Note:** _The sessions below do not include warm-up sets. Unless stated otherwise, rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets._

## Our most effective back routine

As with other muscle groups, I like to **mix** the order of width exercises (pulldowns, pull-ups) and density exercises (rows).

- You can **swap exercises** for similar ones. For example, front pulldowns for machine pulldowns, or one-arm dumbbell rows for one-arm cable rows.
- I like to **pair** this routine with triceps or legs for a more anabolic overall stimulus when I have plenty of energy.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front pulldowns | 4 | 12,10,8,6+12\* |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 | 10,10,10+10\* |
| Dumbbell pull-over | 3 | 10 |
| Assisted pull-ups | 3 | 12 (slow) |
| Back extensions | 3 | 30 (slow) |

> **\*** The "+" means we finish with a drop set for maximum intensity: end the set, take a lighter weight immediately and complete the extra reps.

## Giant set session\*

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deadlift | 4 | 10,10,10,10 |
| Barbell row | 4 | 10,10,10,10 |
| Two-dumbbell row, simultaneous | 4 | 10,10,10,10 |
| Pull-ups | 4 | To failure |

> **\*** A **giant set** is four or more exercises performed without resting. It is a way to raise training intensity: one set of each exercise, back to back, until all are done. Rest at least 3 minutes between giant sets.

## Definition routine

You are supposedly in a **calorie deficit**, so lower the weights and make the session more metabolic: more reps, and focus on feeling every muscle fibre contract.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Machine row | 5 | 20,15,12,12,10 |
| Straight-arm cable pulldowns | 4 | 15,15,12,12 |
| Dumbbell pull-over | 4 | 20,20,15,15 |
| Front cable pulldowns | 4 | 20,15,12,10 |

> **\*** Do not rest more than 30 to 45 seconds between sets.

## Advanced tips for your back training

The back is a hard muscle to stimulate correctly, because a proper **mind-muscle connection** is difficult to build, especially at the start.

Once you get past that, the back is one of the most rewarding muscles, with the greatest potential for strength and growth.
