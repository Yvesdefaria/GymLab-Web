Isolating one head of the triceps is impossible: all three work on every elbow extension. What does change with arm position is the split, and there the long head has an advantage the other two do not.

## Why the triceps long head responds to a high arm

The long head is the only part of the triceps that acts at the shoulder. With the arm hanging, it works in its short range; with the arm overhead, it lengthens before it contracts. No other position changes its length as much. To load it, raise the arm.

A muscle that contracts from a stretched position tends to use the range better. That is why overhead extensions are used for this head and pushdowns for the other two.

Everything that puts the arm above the shoulder falls into this group: two-arm dumbbell extension, EZ-bar extension on an incline bench and the cable version with a rope.

## Cable pushdown: elbow at the side

The pushdown is a good triceps exercise and a bad choice if you are after the long head. With the elbow pinned to the torso, the long head stays shortened and the effort falls on the lateral head, the one that adds size when you look at the arm from the front. It works the whole triceps and that is why it appears in almost every routine; it does not prioritise the long head.

Its advantage is control: the cable keeps tension and allows a two-second squeeze without risk. With a rope, the hands rotate and the work is shared with the medial head. Its best use is the end of the session, when the triceps is already tired and you only need to add work with a light weight.

## Overhead extension: the stretch that counts

Raise the arm above the shoulder and let the dumbbell or bar lower behind the head. At the bottom, the long head is at its longest; at the top, it contracts from there.

Two details: the elbows point forward and do not flare, and the elbow sets the range, not the lower back. If you arch your lumbar spine to go lower, you have changed the exercise. If the elbows open, you lose the stretch and the work moves to the lateral head.

With an EZ bar on a 45-degree bench you can use more weight than standing. Seated with one dumbbell, the range is a little shorter and control is easier.

## Long head or lateral head: which to choose

If the goal is overall arm size, alternate the two positions through the week: one overhead movement and one pushdown cover the whole triceps.

If the long head lags, start the session with the extension, while you are fresh, and leave the pushdown for the end. The pushdown brings control and a squeeze; the extension brings a stretch under load, which is the stimulus you are after here. Neither replaces the other.

With one triceps session a week, two exercises per head are enough. With two sessions, put one in each and let the muscle arrive fresh.

## The long-head routine

It is the same routine that appears in the triceps routines guide, with the numbers exactly as listed. Two exercises and four sets each.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EZ-bar extension on a 45-degree bench | 4 | 12, 12, 10, 10 |
| Overhead dumbbell extension | 4 | 10, 10, 8, 8 |

> **Note:** built for one triceps session a week. Leave two days before the next chest or shoulder press.

If the elbow complains at the bottom, shorten the range before lowering the weight. [Range of motion](/en/range-of-motion-training/) explains when that makes sense, and [muscle failure](/en/muscle-failure-training/) clarifies how many reps to leave in reserve.
