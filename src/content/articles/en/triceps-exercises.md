The triceps is the largest muscle in the arm and the one that adds the most mass behind it. It is also the one that receives the most indirect work: every bench press and every overhead press loads it before its own session starts. So choose the exercises that cover what the presses do not, instead of adding more.

## How to choose triceps exercises

Four situations cover almost any week. The table says what to pick in each one, how many weekly sets to give it and where to place it in the order.

## The order of the session

First the exercise that moves the most weight: close-grip bench press, parallel bar dips or bench dips. Then a movement with the arm overhead. Last, isolation with a cable or a dumbbell.

Order matters because the triceps reaches the last block with accumulated fatigue. A bar pushdown at the start of the session is done with weight; at the end, with whatever is left. If the routine starts with isolation, the close-grip press becomes the exercise you cannot finish.

## How many sets per week

Between 10 and 20 direct sets per week, depending on experience and how much pressing you do. The triceps already works in the bench press and the overhead press, so someone training chest twice a week can progress on 10 direct sets while someone doing only back and legs needs closer to 20.

Split those sets across two sessions if you can. A single triceps session per week forces you to stack many exercises back to back and quality drops in the last ones.

## Arm overhead

This is the most skipped slot and the only one that puts the long head in a stretched position under load. The [seated dumbbell extension](/en/overhead-dumbbell-triceps-extension/) is the easiest version to set up; the [low-pulley extension](/en/low-pulley-triceps-extension/) keeps tension and allows a squeeze at the end. Two or three sets of each is enough.

The [triceps long head](/en/triceps-long-head/) guide explains why this position matters and what does not change with it.

## With the elbow close to the body

Pushdowns and machine extensions load the triceps from an angle that is comfortable for the elbow. The [bar pushdown](/en/cable-triceps-pushdown/) is the standard; the [rope](/en/rope-triceps-pushdown/) lets the hands rotate and the [reverse grip](/en/reverse-grip-triceps-pushdown/) shifts the work towards the medial head. The [machine extension](/en/machine-triceps-extension/) adds the advantage of a fixed path when the elbow asks for calm.

Three or four sets of 10 to 15 reps per exercise, with 60 seconds of rest. These are isolation exercises: weight does not rule, technique does.

## Lying on the bench

The [lying triceps extension](/en/lying-triceps-extension/) and the [dumbbell extension](/en/lying-dumbbell-triceps-extension/) work the triceps with the arm perpendicular to the body. The [close-grip bench press](/en/close-grip-bench-press/) is the heavy exercise of the block, and the [Smith machine close-grip press](/en/smith-machine-close-grip-bench/) lets you secure the bar when the set approaches the limit. The [lying EZ bar extension](/en/triceps-superset-extension-close-grip/) chains the extension and the press as a single round.

Three to five sets of 6 to 12 reps. This is the block where the most weight moves and where you should arrive fresh.

## Bodyweight

[Parallel bar dips](/en/triceps-dips/) are the triceps exercise that moves the most weight with the whole body. [Bench dips](/en/bench-dips/) are the most accessible version and the [assisted dip machine](/en/assisted-dip-machine/) lets you go below bodyweight when eight clean reps are still out of reach.

[Triceps push-ups](/en/triceps-push-ups/) close the block with no equipment and work as a finisher in a superset. Three or four sets of 8 to 15 reps.

## At home with one dumbbell

The [dumbbell kickback](/en/dumbbell-triceps-kickback/) and the [cable kickback](/en/cable-triceps-kickback/) are the lowest-load exercises in the catalogue and the ones that fatigue the elbow least. They work as the last exercise of the session, with sets of 12 to 15 reps and a one-second squeeze.

## The heads are not selectable

The three heads of the triceps share one distal tendon and work together in every elbow extension. Arm position changes the length and leverage of each one. No handle and no incline disconnects a head. The [triceps anatomy](/en/triceps-anatomy/) article describes where all three start and their role at the shoulder.

## Programming mistakes

- **Pushdowns only.** It is the easiest exercise to set up and the one that covers the least: without an overhead movement, the long head never reaches its stretched position.
- **Ten exercises in one session.** With 10 to 20 weekly sets there is no room for all of them; pick three or four and add weight.
- **Failure on every set.** The triceps works in every press; taking it to the limit three times per session is paid for in next week's press.
- **Changing exercises every week.** Without repeating the same movement there is no way to know whether it is progressing.
- **Skipping the warm-up.** Two light sets on the first exercise are enough for the elbow to arrive ready for the load.
