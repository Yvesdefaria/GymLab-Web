Someone training at seven in the morning and someone training at eight at night do not need different diets. They need the same diet in a different order. Meal timing around training means deciding which meal acts as the pre-workout one, how big it is and what it contains, then letting the rest of the day cover the figures.

## The rule that does not change: the daily total

Daily protein, fat and carbohydrate are set first, exactly as in any plan. The clock only decides where each portion lands. If the session falls mid-morning, breakfast becomes the pre-workout meal; if it falls at night, lunch is the pre-workout meal and dinner the post-workout one.

That order explains why two people on the same plan eat different plates at the same hour. It also explains why a large pre-workout meal does not fix a weak day: the total rules, and distribution only helps you arrive in shape. The guide to [bodybuilding nutrition](/en/bodybuilding-nutrition-basics/) sets those figures before touching the clock.

## Training first thing in the morning

With a session at seven or eight, the pre-workout meal is decided the night before. A normal dinner with protein, vegetables and a portion of carbohydrate leaves glycogen ready. In the morning, half an hour out, there is room for a piece of fruit, a yogurt or a small slice of toast; anyone who tolerates training on an empty stomach can wait for breakfast.

Breakfast afterwards covers the missing protein and the carbohydrate for the first half of the day. A practical example: two eggs, bread and a piece of fruit, or oats with milk and fruit. The meal after training has its own guide in [post-workout meal](/en/post-workout-meal/).

## Training at midday

Breakfast sits three or four hours before the session and acts as the main pre-workout meal. Mid-morning, one or two hours out, a small portion of carbohydrate with some protein works: fruit with yogurt, toast with turkey or a handful of oats with milk.

Lunch follows and closes the block with protein and carbohydrate. The usual mistake at this hour is a light breakfast that leaves you hungry, or a huge meal immediately before training with a full stomach. The guide to [pre-workout meals](/en/pre-workout-meal/) covers amounts and timing.

## Training in the afternoon or evening

Lunch is the main pre-workout meal and a snack covers the last hour. If the session starts at seven, lunch at two or three leaves enough margin; at six, a piece of fruit or a slice of toast solves it without weight in the stomach.

Dinner acts as the post-workout meal and can carry carbohydrate without fear. Eating carbohydrate at night does not make you fat on its own: the daily total decides. For anyone training late and eating soon after, dinner is the most important meal of the day.

## Size and fibre in the last meal

Fibre slows gastric emptying, so a meal heavy in vegetables, legumes and whole grains stays in the stomach longer. That is welcome three hours before a session and uncomfortable thirty minutes before. When the pre-workout meal is close, reduce fibre and fat and keep a small portion of [carbohydrate](/en/carbohydrates/) with 20 to 30 grams of protein.

Loading the pre-workout meal with a huge portion of carbohydrate is more than most people tolerate on a full stomach, and it offers no advantage over a moderate portion. Fruit counts as a carbohydrate source in any meal of the day, the pre-workout one included, and needs no special treatment.

## What stays the same

Water stays between 2 and 3 litres a day, plus what sweat demands. Protein is spread in 25 to 40 gram portions per meal. Supplements remain an extra: none replaces food or does the work the plan already does.

## How to arrange it this week

Pick your training slot and mark the meal that acts as the pre-workout one. Match its size to the distance from the session and let the other meals cover the day's figures. If you train fasted and it works, [intermittent fasting](/en/intermittent-fasting-and-weights/) can live alongside weights as long as the daily total is met.

Review the result after two weeks: energy in the session, digestion and weekly weight. [Supplement mistakes](/en/supplement-mistakes/) close the list of what does not need adding once the schedule is in order.
