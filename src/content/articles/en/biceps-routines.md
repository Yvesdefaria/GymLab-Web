The biceps has one main action, elbow flexion, and a handful of curl variations that change the angle and the grip. With so little material you can build very different sessions, which is why there are so many routines. The ten below cover the usual goals and share the same ordering rules.

## How to choose your biceps routine

Pick a single plan and keep it for six to eight weeks. Changing routine every week makes it impossible to tell whether anything works.

Leave about 48 hours between two biceps sessions and do not chain two routines from this page in the same week. The biceps is a small muscle and it also works when you train back, so volume adds up fast.

Inside each session, order the exercises from heaviest to lightest: barbell and dumbbell curls first, then the Scott bench and the concentration curl, then cables and machines. It is the same logic the [training volume](/en/training-volume/) guide applies to any muscle group.

> **Note:** the routines do not include warm-up sets. Unless a routine says otherwise, rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

## Biceps routine for size

Basic exercises, heavy weights and high volume. If you are advanced and know how to train near the limit, you can drop one set from each exercise.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing barbell curl | 4 | 6, 8, 10, 12 |
| EZ-bar preacher curl | 4 | 6, 8, 10, 12 |
| Incline dumbbell curl | 3 | 8, 8, 10 |
| High-cable curl | 3 | 8, 10, 12 |

## Biceps routine for beginners

Exercises that are easy to perform and produce results early. The goal in the first weeks is to learn the full range and feel the muscle, not to move weight.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing EZ-bar curl | 3 | 10, 12, 12 |
| Standing dumbbell curl | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |
| Biceps machine curl | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |

## Home biceps routine

One bar and two dumbbells are enough to train your arms at home. If you only have dumbbells, swap the barbell curl for the alternating curl.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing dumbbell curl | 4 | 10, 12, 12, 20 |
| "21" curl \* | 4 | 21 |
| Concentration curl | 4 | 12, 12, 15, 20 |

> **\*** The "21" curl is split into three blocks of seven reps: seven from the lower half of the range, seven from the upper half and seven full reps.

## 15-minute biceps routine

For days when time is short. Cut rest to the minimum and concentrate the work in three exercises.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low-cable curl | 3 | 6, 10, 15 |
| High-cable curl | 3 | 6, 10, 15 |
| Concentration curl | 3 | 6, 10, 15 |

> **Note:** do not rest more than 30 seconds between sets.

## Long-head work with a close grip

This routine used to be sold as the secret of the biceps peak. The shape of the muscle and where it inserts are genetic, so no exercise changes the peak. What a close grip does is load the long head more, since that is the head that crosses the shoulder. The [biceps anatomy](/en/biceps-forearm-anatomy/) page explains why.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing barbell curl, close grip | 4 | 10, 10, 12, 12 |
| Incline dumbbell curl | 4 | 8, 8, 10, 10 |
| Alternating hammer curl | 4 | 10, 12, 15, 15 |
| Low-cable curl | 3 | 10, 10, 12 |

## Routine for short-head priority

Exercises with the arms in front of the body and wide grips load the short head more, without neglecting overall biceps development.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-arm dumbbell preacher curl | 4 | 6, 10, 12, 15 |
| EZ-bar preacher curl | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |
| Standing dumbbell curl | 3 | 8, 10, 12 |
| Wide-grip barbell curl | 3 | 8, 10, 12 |

## Biceps giant set

Four exercises back to back with no rest. It is a session to try now and then, not a regular plan. [Giant sets](/en/giant-sets/) explains the format and its limits.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing barbell curl | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| EZ-bar preacher curl | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| Incline dumbbell curl | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| Standing dumbbell curl | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |

> **Note:** a giant set is the four exercises in a row, with no rest between them. Rest at least three minutes before the next round.

## Biceps routine for strength

Heavy weight, basic exercises and long rests. Low reps demand strict form and a partner nearby on the heavy sets.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing EZ-bar curl | 4 | 4, 4, 6, 8 |
| Drag curl | 4 | 4, 4, 6, 8 |
| Alternating hammer curl | 4 | 6, 6, 8 |

> **Note:** rest two to three minutes between sets.

## Biceps routine for definition

Lighter weights and short rests. Diet decides definition; training only decides how much muscle sits underneath.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seated alternating dumbbell curl | 5 | 10, 12, 12, 15, 20 |
| EZ-bar preacher curl | 4 | 12, 12, 15, 15 |
| Dumbbell "21" curl | 4 | 21 |

> **Note:** rest 30 to 45 seconds between sets. The "21" curl keeps the same split of three blocks of seven reps.

## Biceps routine for maximum pump

Useful for breaking a plateau. Long rep ranges fill the muscle with blood, although the pump does not measure growth. The [muscle pump](/en/muscle-pump/) guide separates one from the other.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing barbell curl | 3 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |
| Low-cable curl | 3 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |
| Hammer curl | 3 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |

> **Note:** do not rest more than 30 seconds between sets.

## How to fit it into your week

With ten options, the temptation is to try them all. Pick one, keep it for six to eight weeks and write down the loads for each exercise so you know whether they rise. When the routine stops producing progress, change the plan rather than the technique mid-set.

The biceps responds to complete work more than to accumulated volume. The [full range of motion](/en/range-of-motion-training/) matters on every rep, and [progressive overload](/en/weider-principles/) is still the rule that makes any routine grow. If you want a plan that combines strength, mass and shape in the same session, the [bigger biceps](/en/bigger-biceps/) guide orders the three movements.
