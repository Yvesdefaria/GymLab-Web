It is a variation of the barbell deadlift that recruits the glutes, the lumbar region and the quads alongside the hamstrings.

## How to do the stiff leg deadlift with a barbell

Set your feet about shoulder width apart and arch your back very slightly backwards. Flex your torso and lift a barbell or two dumbbells off the floor.

With a barbell, use an overhand grip. With dumbbells, the natural semi-pronated grip sits between neutral and pronated.

Stand up using the strength of your hamstrings and glutes while your legs stay semi-extended. Once upright, hinge at the torso without bending your legs to return to the start. The weights do not have to touch the floor before the next rep.

## Stiff leg and Romanian deadlift: the range is the difference

This exercise stands out from other hamstring movements because it provides a much more powerful stretch. The key difference from the Romanian deadlift is the range of motion.

## Which muscles it works

The stiff leg deadlift with a barbell recruits the hamstrings, glutes, lumbar region and quads. The hip is the main joint, while the rest of the posterior chain assists.

## Mistakes that load the spine

Rounding the spine and pulling too much with your back instead of your hamstrings lets you use more load or do more reps, but it reduces the work on the hamstrings and makes the exercise dangerous for the spine.

Keeping your legs straight may be hard at first. You can bend them slightly, but bending them too much reduces the tension on the hamstrings.

## Advanced tips: range, tension and failure

If you are flexible, try leaning forward until your torso is parallel to the floor, then stand up. If you feel excessive tension in the spine or the hamstrings, stop well before parallel.

Do not bring your torso all the way to perpendicular on every rep. By not completing the full rise you keep continuous tension on the hamstrings. Only at muscular failure should you cover the full range and rest a few seconds for a few extra reps.

When the lower back tires, holding the natural arch gets hard and the spine starts to round. At that point, reduce the range of motion so you can always keep the back straight. The same problem shows up in the [barbell good morning](/en/standing-good-mornings/).

## Soreness and precautions

It is one of the best exercises for working the hamstrings hard. Because it stretches them so much, it can cause noticeable soreness that lasts several days. It may look easy, but it is more dangerous than it seems. Keeping your balance and good technique is not always simple.
