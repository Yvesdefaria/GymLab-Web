It is a plan for someone coming out of a volume phase who wants to lose fat without losing what they built. Diet comes first: without a deficit of about 500 calories a day, training does not move the scale. The [cutting routines guide](/en/cutting-routines/) explains how to set that deficit up.

The structure is simple: weights on Monday, Wednesday and Friday; cardio on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday; rest on Sunday. Eight hours of sleep is not decorative advice: with six sessions a week, the nervous system gets overloaded when sleep is short.

Cardio comes in two formats. Sixty minutes at a low pace, where you can hold a conversation, or 45 minutes at a high pace, where talking is hard. Pick one and do not change it midweek. The [aerobic exercise guide](/en/aerobic-exercise/) explains how to measure that intensity without relying on a formula.

Low intensity holds without stopping and lets you talk. High intensity forces you to slow down at some point, which is why it lasts less. You do not need a heart-rate monitor or a formula: the conversation is the test.

## Monday: chest and triceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell bench press | 4 | 8, 8, 6, 6 |
| Barbell incline press | 4 | 8, 8, 6, 6 |
| Cable crossovers | 4 | 8 |
| Close-grip bench press | 3 | 8 |
| Lying triceps extensions | 3 | 8 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 3 | 8 |

> **Note:** each week, alternate the flat press between dumbbells and barbell, and do the same with the incline press. The angle changes without changing the scheme.

## Wednesday: back and biceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wide-grip pull-ups | 4 | 8 (slow) |
| Close-grip pulldowns | 4 | 8, 8, 6, 6 |
| Cable row | 4 | 8 |
| Barbell curl | 3 | 8, 8, 6 |
| Incline bench dumbbell curl | 3 | 8 |
| Dumbbell preacher curl | 3 | 8 |

> **Note:** if you cannot reach 8 reps on the close-grip pulldown, finish with slow negatives. If you cannot do pull-ups, replace them with heavy front pulldowns.

## Friday: legs and shoulders

Legs open the day and shoulders close it. If the squat leaves you without strength for the military press, change the order and start with shoulders: the plan does not break.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |

| Stiff-legged deadlift | 3 | 8 |
| Machine leg curl | 3 | 8, 8, 6 |
| Leg extension | 3 | 8, 8, 6 |

| Military press | 4 | 8, 8, 6, 6 |

The [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) and [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/) guides cover the variants of each exercise if one does not fit your gym.

## Why the load stays heavy

Lighter weights and higher reps are the usual answer in a cut, and this plan does the opposite. A heavy load is the signal that tells the body to keep the muscle the deficit wants to take. Reps come down because the weight goes up, not because the plan changes its goal.

## The load

The weight stays heavy. On the first set of each exercise, pick a load that leaves one or two reps in reserve. If you complete every rep of every set, move up to the smallest increment the following week. If you miss a rep, repeat the weight.

The numbers 8, 8, 6, 6 mean the first two sets are 8 reps and the last two are 6, with more weight on the 6s. They are not four sets at the same weight.

## The deload

Every six or eight weeks, take one week with half the sets and 70 % of the load. Cardio drops to two 30-minute sessions. The [deload week guide](/en/deload-week/) explains why that week is not lost time.

## Abs

They go at the end of the three lifting days, not before cardio. Three exercises, three sets of 12 to 15 reps: cable crunch, hanging leg raise and oblique crunch.

Twenty minutes of abs before every cardio session leaves the core tired right when you are about to row or run. Here the work stays at nine weekly sets, at the end of the weights.

## How long it lasts

Two months, deload included. If the scale does not move in three weeks, the problem is in the kitchen before it is in the plan. If strength drops two sessions in a row, the deficit is too large.

When you finish, return to a volume phase or repeat the block with updated loads. The [training routine guide](/en/five-month-training-plan/) helps you choose the next step.
