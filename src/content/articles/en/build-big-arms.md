The curl looks like the simplest exercise in the book, and it is the one most often ruined. The bar goes up, the elbow stays still and even so the biceps does not always do the work. The problem is almost always in the grip and in the position of the arm.

## Why your big arms do not grow from the curl

With the palm facing up, the biceps is the main flexor of the elbow and the strongest supinator of the forearm. When the hand turns to a neutral position, as in the hammer curl, the biceps loses that advantage and part of the work moves to the brachialis and the brachioradialis. They are two muscles that push the arm up, but they are not the biceps.

That gives the first decision of the session. If you want to load the biceps, the palm faces up through the whole range. If you want thickness on the thumb side, the neutral grip is the right one, and it counts too. The [anatomy of the biceps and forearm](/en/biceps-forearm-anatomy/) explains the split.

The second decision is the angle of the arm against the torso. A standing curl, with the arm hanging, leaves the long head in a neutral position. An incline-bench curl, with the arm behind the body, stretches it and changes the emphasis.

## The two-hand cable curl, step by step

The cable keeps tension through the whole range and lets you pin the elbows to the body without fighting gravity.

1. Set both cables at the lowest position of the frame, one on each side.
2. Clip a metal handle or a V rope to each cable.
3. Choose a weight that lets you keep strict form. More reps beats cheating with too much weight.
4. Grab one handle with each hand, palms facing up.
5. Pin the elbows to the body and lift without letting them drift apart or forward. If the elbows rise, the tension moves to the front deltoid and the pec minor.
6. Lift to a full contraction, squeeze at the top and lower under control.

The protocol is 10 reps per set, under 60 seconds of rest and 6 to 10 sets in total. One day you can do this movement alone and add two or three sets of concentration curls to finish.

## How much weight to use and how to add

The cable forgives more than a free bar, and that invites overloading. Start with a weight that lets you complete all ten reps without moving the elbows or leaning back. When all ten come out clean, move up one plate on the next session.

Progression works the same as in any other exercise: more kilos or more reps with the same technique. If you have spent three weeks with the same weight and the same reps, nothing has changed and neither has the exercise.

## How to split the sets and the rest

Six sets are enough if the cable curl is the only exercise of the session. Ten make sense when you pair it with a barbell or incline-bench movement, because the total volume is already high.

Short rest keeps the pump and shortens the session. What you should not do is cut it to the point where you fail the next set for lack of air.

Leave at least 48 hours before working the biceps again. If you train back the day before, the elbow flexors already have work behind them and the arm session starts uphill.

## Grip, angle and wrist

- **Wide-grip straight-bar curl:** loads the short head a little more, the one that does not cross the shoulder.
- **Incline-bench dumbbell curl:** stretches the long head and puts it to work from the bottom.
- **Concentration curl:** the cleanest isolation. The free hand helps on the last reps and the body stays still.
- **Hammer or neutral-grip curl:** brachialis and brachioradialis. It is the one that adds thickness on the thumb side.

The wrist stays neutral, in line with the forearm, and does not bend on the way up or down. A small tilt with the pinky higher is a variant you can try on a light set; it corrects nothing and does not replace the neutral position. The guide to [wrist position in the biceps curl](/en/wrist-position-biceps-curl/) covers it.

## When to change the exercise

If the cable curl stops accepting more kilos for three weeks in a row, change the angle before the number of sets. Move to the incline bench, the preacher bench or the concentration curl, keep the same scheme and compare.
