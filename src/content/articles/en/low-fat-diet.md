Fat supplies 9 calories per gram, against 4 for protein and carbohydrate. That is why a little fills a lot, and why it pays to measure: two tablespoons of oil a day are 180 calories that almost nobody counts.

## How much fat a low-fat diet has

In a 2,200-calorie diet, 25 % is 61 g of fat, and 30 g of oil already covers half of it. For a 70 kg person, the practical range is 0.5 to 1 g per kilo, which means 35 to 70 g a day. The guide to [dietary fats](/en/dietary-fats/) explains the types and how much of each.

Going below 10 % improves nothing. Fat carries vitamins A, D, E and K, forms part of cell membranes and takes part in hormone production. A plan with very little fat becomes hard to sustain and does not speed up loss.

When the goal is fat loss, the cut has to come from total calories. A low-fat diet with the same calories as before does not change body weight.

## Where the fat you cannot see lives

The oil in the pan, the dressing on the salad and the cheese on the toast add up to more than it seems. These are the sources that mislead most.

Frequency and amount decide: the same cured cheese on one toast a day breaks nothing, and three servings a day change the total. The guide to [trans fats](/en/trans-fats/) points out the ones worth leaving off the usual shop.

The day adds up to about 1,850 calories and 50 g of fat, 24 % of the total. Protein lands near 135 g, 1.9 g per kilo at 70 kg. The portions are: 60 g oats, 200 ml milk, 20 g almonds, 4 egg whites, 180 g chicken, 70 g dry rice, 10 g oil at lunch, 2 slices of toast with 80 g fresh cheese, and 180 g hake with 5 g oil at dinner.

Carbohydrate covers a little over half the calories and fibre reaches 30 g. It is a comfortable split for training and for arriving at dinner with hunger under control.

## The mistakes that leave it short

The first is going below 10 %. When fat falls too far, food loses flavour, satiety suffers and the plan lasts a short time. The second is making up for it with sugar: many low-fat products carry more sugar to keep their texture, and total calories do not fall.

The third is not measuring oil. One tablespoon is 10 g and 90 calories; four tablespoons a day are 360 calories that nobody counts. The fourth is reading the label without reading the serving: a product with 3 g of fat per 100 g can carry 15 g per pack.

And the fifth is expecting the diet to work alone. The guide to [healthy diet myths](/en/healthy-diet-myths/) goes through the beliefs that repeat most, and a [calorie deficit](/en/calorie-deficit-for-weight-loss/) is still the condition for losing fat.

## How to fit it around training

With weights three or four days a week, protein does not go below 1.6 g per kilo and fat stays in the lower part of the range, between 20 and 25 %. Carbohydrate covers the rest and supports the sessions.

Before training, a meal with carbohydrate and little fat works best, because fat slows stomach emptying. Afterwards, protein and carbohydrate replace what was used.

If the plan runs longer than eight weeks, raise fat to the upper part of the range and check that weight falls by 0.5 to 1 % a week. To see how fat is split in a cutting diet with more steps, the guide to [bodybuilder cutting diets](/en/bodybuilder-cutting-diet/) lays out eight levels with their macros, and the one on [maximum definition](/en/maximum-definition-diet/) takes the example down to 1,900 calories.
