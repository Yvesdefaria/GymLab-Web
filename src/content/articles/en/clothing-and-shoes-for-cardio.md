Clothing itself is not complicated. Shoes deserve more attention: they are the most important piece in weight-bearing activities, and where the rules and the numbers are.

## Why shoes matter most

In activities where you carry your own body weight, such as walking or running, adequate shoes give you **cushioning**, **stability** and **comfort** while staying flexible.

The main factor that determines the quality of a running shoe is its compression capacity, and 50% of that capacity is lost after roughly 500 to 800 km. Most shoes should be replaced after that distance or every six months, whichever comes first.

Runners with a high body weight or an unusual gait, such as **overpronators** or **supinators**, may need to replace their shoes more often.

### How to recognise your gait

**Overpronation** happens when the foot rolls too far inward on each step as the weight loads the arch. **Supination** happens when the foot lands excessively on the outer edges with very little inward roll. A **neutral gait** is the third option, and the table below summarises which last and which motion control go with it.

## How to choose your shoes

The choice depends on two things: the activity you are going to do and the characteristics of your gait.

Running shoes are usually built on **three types of last**: straight, semi-curved and curved.

**Overpronators** can benefit from shoes with a **straight last** and motion control. **Supinators** can choose a **curved last**, which allows a wider range of movement for the foot. People with a **neutral gait** can use shoes with a **semi-curved last** and moderate features for direction and foot control.

To get the choice right, it helps to visit a **podiatrist** who can analyse the **biomechanics** of your running. You can also check the wear pattern on an old pair of shoes for more clues.

## Clothing and layers

In hot conditions, clothing should be as light as possible. In cold conditions, wear several layers and add **hats, gloves and scarves**, because in the cold a large amount of **body heat** is lost through the head and extremities.

Comfort governs the rest: loose clothing that lets you move easily.
