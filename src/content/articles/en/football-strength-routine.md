Football asks for both at once: the endurance to last ninety minutes and the strength for the first step, the jump and the challenge. The plan spreads four sessions across the week and keeps the weekend for the match.

## Strength and speed in the same plan

The block lasts eight weeks and splits into two equal halves. Each half has three weeks of strength and one of speed. In the strength weeks the weight goes up; in the speed week lower-body volume drops and you run.

Execution speed matters. Even when the phase is fast, the movement stays controlled: no bouncing and no hyperextending knees or elbows at the end of the range. A player injured in the gym does not help the team.

## The week

Two upper-body days and two lower-body days. Each pattern repeats after 48 hours, and Saturday belongs to the match. Two sessions of each pattern keep technique sharp without stacking fatigue before the match.

The warm-up is ten minutes of mobility and two light sets of the first exercise. Long stretches go after the session, not before.

## Upper body

The sets and reps are this page's recommendation. The last set of each exercise is demanding, without reaching failure on pull-ups or dips.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 6 |
| Pull-ups | 4 | 6-8 |
| Barbell shoulder press | 3 | 8 |
| Dumbbell curl | 3 | 10 |
| Dips | 3 | 10 |
| Abs | 3 | 15 |

## Lower body

Lower body carries more volume than upper body because it produces the sprint and the jump. The squat opens the session and the calf raise closes it. If the match moves to Friday, keep only the Wednesday leg session and cut it to three sets.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 5 | 5 |
| Leg extensions | 3 | 10 |
| Lying leg curl | 3 | 10 |
| Cable adductors | 3 | 12 |
| Dumbbell lunges | 3 | 8 |
| Cable abductors | 3 | 12 |
| Seated calf raise | 4 | 12 |

## The speed week

In the fourth week of each block, lower-body volume drops and two sprint sessions come in. A typical session: six sets of 20 metres with 60 seconds of rest, four sets of 40 metres with two minutes and ten easy minutes of jogging to finish. That is 280 metres at top speed per session.

Warm up with two easy 40-metre runs and a few skips before the first sprint. Leave 48 hours between the two sprint sessions. The rest of the week keeps upper body and cuts legs to two sets per exercise at 60 % of the previous week's weight.

## Where it fits in the season

Pre-season runs the full eight-week block. In season, with one match a week, keep two sessions: upper body on Tuesday and lower body on Wednesday, with half the sets. The match replaces the sprints.

If there is a winter break, that is the moment to run the block again, with the first week at light load. Aerobic base work stays with two easy runs of 25 to 30 minutes, away from leg days.

## How it progresses

Add 5 kilos on the squat and 2.5 on upper-body lifts when the last set comes up clean, with every rep and no loss of technique. If you miss two sessions in a row on the same exercise, drop 10 % and climb again. A player who cannot finish the last set at the prescribed reps repeats the same weight for another week. After the eight weeks, take a deload week at half the weight before starting another block.
