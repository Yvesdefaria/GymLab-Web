The plan is meant for someone who has already done a circuit and wants something more ordered, without jumping to a routine with several days per muscle group. Every week changes the split and every session keeps the same skeleton.

## How the rotation works

Three sessions a week: Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Each day carries five exercises: the first two at four sets, the last three at three. Reps are always 8 to 10, on every exercise and in every week. At the end of week 5, week 6 is left for rest and another plan begins.

The rotation changes the group that opens each session. It is not a fixed split: Monday of week 1 starts with chest, week 2 with back, week 3 with legs. That change of order forces the body to respond without piling fatigue on the same group. Five exercises cover the whole body when the order changes and the volume stays low.

Weekly volume stays low on purpose: between 4 and 8 sets per large group. The rotation changes emphasis, not the number of sets. Hamstrings appear only in week 3 and traps in week 5, so the plan does not cover those two groups steadily. [Training volume](/en/training-volume/) breaks down how sets are counted and what each goal asks for.

## How to choose the weight

The reference is 8 to 10 reps. If all ten come easy, add weight; if you cannot reach eight, lower it. When the last set is clean, with every rep and no loss of form, add 2.5 kilos to the upper body and 5 to the legs. In a five-week plan there is no room for two weeks of testing: the weight is set in the first session.

Rest between sets and exercises follows what the next set asks for: just enough to repeat the movement with the same form. [Progressive overload](/en/progressive-overload-principle/) sums up the criterion: more weight when the last set is clean, never at the cost of range of motion.

## After week 5

Week 6 is for rest. Then comes a plan with a longer progression: the [beginner volume programme](/en/beginner-volume-program/) covers twelve weeks and orders volume in three phases. If you want to stay on three days, [beginner weight routines](/en/beginner-weight-routines/) keep the whole body and a four-week wave.

If the plan falls short and you want a split by muscle group, [full-body routines](/en/full-body-routines/) offer variants by level.
