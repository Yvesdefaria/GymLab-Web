The split is classic bodybuilding: one large group per session, two small groups on the arm and shoulder days, and Sunday off. The order follows the bodybuilding manual. The set count is what changes the plan. Monday adds up to 40 and Saturday to 27.

## What can be claimed about this routine

The routine circulates attributed to the Harrison twins, two brothers who train together. The version copied from site to site adds a biography, magazine features and a diet with timings, but it brings neither a calorie count nor a verifiable source. What stays here is what can be used: the split, the exercises, the sets and the reps.

There is no written progression in the original either. The one below is what makes the plan work without changing its numbers.

Five sessions in a row with a single rest day in the middle. If Thursday becomes a second day off, the plan still works; what it cannot take is running Friday and Saturday without sleeping well.

## Monday: back and chest

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell bench press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Incline barbell press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Cable crossover | 4 | 8-10 |
| Incline dumbbell fly | 4 | 8-10 |
| Machine fly | 4 | 8-10 |
| Deadlift | 4 | 8 |
| T-bar row | 4 | 8 |
| Lat pulldown | 4 | 8-10 |
| Hammer Strength high row | 4 | 8 |
| Hammer Strength low row | 4 | 8 |

Five chest exercises and five back exercises, 40 sets in total. At one minute of rest the session comes close to ninety minutes on sets alone. The chest takes 20 sets and the back another 20, so fatigue arrives before the end of the table.

## Tuesday: abs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decline crunch | 4 | to failure |
| Lying leg raise | 4 | to failure |
| Oblique crunch | 4 | to failure |
| Decline oblique crunch | 4 | to failure |

Sixteen sets to failure in a stand-alone day is a lot for the abs. Two or three sets per exercise give the same stimulus and let you reach Wednesday ready to train legs.

## Wednesday: legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 4 | 10 |
| Leg press | 6 | 8-10 |
| Leg extension | 4 | 8-10 |
| Lying leg curl | 4 | 8-10 |
| Barbell lunge | 3 | 50 m |
| Calf raise | 3 | 8 |

The leg press takes six sets, the highest count for a single exercise in the plan. Lunges are measured in metres, not reps: 50 metres per set with the bar on your shoulders and a long stride. If the floor does not allow 50 metres, measure the route in steps and repeat the same distance every week.

## Friday: shoulders

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seated dumbbell press | 4 | 8 |
| Seated barbell press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Seated lateral raise | 4 | 8-10 |
| Seated barbell front raise | 4 | 8-10 |
| Rear delt raise | 4 | 8 |
| Dumbbell shrug | 3 | 20 |

Two presses back to back, one with dumbbells and one with a barbell. That is a high volume for the front delt, which already worked on Monday in the bench press. If the shoulder complains, drop one press and keep the other.

## Saturday: arms

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 21s with EZ bar | 3 | 21 |
| Straight-bar biceps curl | 4 | 8 |
| Seated hammer curl | 4 | 8-10 |
| Cable Scott curl | 4 | 8-10 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 4 | 8-10 |
| French press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Incline cable extension | 4 | 8-10 |

21s split 21 reps into three blocks of seven: half flexion, half extension and full range, without letting go of the bar. Saturday closes with 27 sets, 15 for biceps and 12 for triceps.

## How long to rest

One minute between sets and two to three minutes between exercises. That is short rest for the volume the session carries, which is why Monday chokes. If one minute does not let you complete the last set's reps, move to 90 seconds on the basics before cutting exercises. The [rest between sets](/en/rest-between-sets/) guide explains how to adjust it.

## How to trim the volume without changing the plan

Start at two thirds of the sets: about 26 on Monday and 16 on Saturday. Keep every exercise and every rep; what drops is the number of sets per exercise. Add one set per week only if the previous set of that exercise was clean, with full technique and range.

The rule matters because the published total does not distinguish between someone with eight years of training and a beginner. With 40 sets at one minute of rest, the second half of the session happens with an empty chest and broken technique.

## How the load moves

When every set of an exercise reaches the top of the range with the same technique, the next session adds the smallest plate and keeps the reps. If the last set loses one or two, repeat the load. On fixed 8-rep exercises the signal is the same: eight clean reps with the new weight.

Volume only works if the weight moves. Repeating the same 40 sets with the same load for months leaves the body in maintenance.

## What about the diet

The diet that circulates with the routine spreads eight meals across timings but gives no calories and no amounts per kilo, so it can neither be checked nor scaled. If the goal is mass, the practical reference is the one used across the [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) block: a small surplus, enough protein and bodyweight measured every week.

Chest, triceps and legs get their own coverage in the [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/), [triceps routines](/en/triceps-routines/) and [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) guides. To understand how many sets a session this size can take, [training volume](/en/training-volume/) is the missing piece.
