Ectomorph describes someone who gains weight with difficulty. The label sets the strategy; it does not explain on its own why the scale will not move. If you have spent months trying to gain and the weight stays the same, the problem is usually food and training volume.

## Why three days and low volume

In a cut, the body trains on fewer calories and recovery costs more. Adding sets does not make up for it: every extra set costs recovery and adds no new signal. Load is a signal, because it tells the body the muscle is still needed even as food drops.

The plan has three sessions and three exercises per session. It stays that way. More volume does not fit a cutting week without taking out cardio or rest.

## The A/B week

The two workouts alternate. One week starts with A and the next with B, so each routine lands three times in two weeks.

The day between sessions is not negotiable. Tuesday and Thursday or Wednesday and Saturday both work; what matters is a day in between.

## Workout A

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 5-6 |
| Stiff-legged deadlift | 4 | 5-6 |
| Pull-ups | 4 | 5-6 |

All three are basic and heavy. There are no flyes or extensions: the chest, the hamstrings and the back already get the stimulus they need.

## Workout B

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat or leg press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 4 | 5-6 |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 4 | 5-6 |

Legs handle 8 to 10 reps well; the incline press and the row do not. That difference is the only one in the plan, and it comes from muscle size and from what you can load without losing position.

## Double progression

Start with a weight you can lift comfortably for 10 reps on each exercise; on the squat or leg press, one that leaves you 12. From there:

- Add 2 kg per week (1 kg on the one-arm row).
- On pull-ups, add 2 or 3 reps in total, not in every set.
- If on any set you cannot reach 5 reps, repeat the weight.
- If you miss again, drop 4 kg (2 kg on the row) and start climbing again.

At week 5 the weight stayed at 68 kg and the set did not come out complete. Week 6 repeated it and missed again. Week 7 dropped 4 kg. By week 9 the same weight came out in 4 x 6, and week 10 moved to 70 kg.

In a cut, the priority may be holding rather than adding. Even so, try to add weight at least once every two weeks: strength is what keeps muscle when calories drop.

## Arms and shoulders

Arms work indirectly in the presses and rows; shoulders, in the incline press. If you have room, add 3 sets of 7 or 8 reps of overhead triceps extension to workout A and another 3 of barbell curl to B. Lateral raises go on whichever day you prefer, without turning the session into a shoulder day.

If the main plan already costs you, add nothing. Arms and shoulders get enough indirect work.

## Food and weight

As an ectomorph, the fight to gain weight gets the same seriousness as the weights. If you train well and think you eat a lot but the scale stays low, you are not eating enough. Eat at least five times a day and add calories until the scale moves. The guide on [how to gain weight](/en/how-to-gain-weight/) explains how without filling up on junk food.

If you are underweight, cutting is not the priority. Gain muscle first; that muscle then helps burn fat.

## When to change

This plan is minimal by design. After 8 or 12 weeks of double progression with the weight moving up, you can add a little volume: 4 sets of 8 to 10 with two exercises per group, at most.
