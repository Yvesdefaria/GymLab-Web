The name ABAB falls short of what the plan actually asks for. Train Monday to Friday and the week reads A, B, rest, A, B: the third day is rest and the fourth repeats the push session. That spread leaves 72 hours between two sessions for the same muscle group, above the 48 hour minimum the plan demands.

## What this routine solves

This plan is not the twelve-week programme or the three-day alternating plan. It is the four-day option, with two sessions repeated twice a week. For block-by-block progression, the [beginner volume programme](/en/beginner-volume-program/) orders twelve weeks into three phases. If you prefer three days, [beginner weight routines](/en/beginner-weight-routines/) keep the whole body with fewer sessions.

The calendar leaves Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday for rest. The 48 hour rule is met with room to spare: chest, shoulders and triceps train on Monday and Thursday; back, biceps and legs on Tuesday and Friday.

## How each set runs

Weight matters less than form. Every rep is complete, without bouncing and without losing the position of your back. Between sets and between exercises you rest 30 to 45 seconds, the minimum that restores some strength without cooling the muscle.

The session starts with ten minutes on the stationary bike and ends with a basic abs circuit. You can add 20 to 30 minutes of easy cardio afterwards. Sets do not reach failure: the last rep has to come out clean.

## Session A: push

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 12-9-9-11 |
| Incline press in the Smith machine | 3 | 12 |
| Machine chest fly | 3 | 15 |
| Smith machine shoulder press | 4 | 12-9-9-11 |
| Lateral raises | 3 | 12 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 3 | 10 |

The 12-9-9-11 reads set by set: 12 reps in the first, 9 in the second and third, and 11 in the last. It is the notation of the original plan and stays the same in the bench press and the shoulder press.

## Session B: pull and legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front lat pulldown | 4 | 12-9-9-11 |
| Seated cable row | 3 | 10 |
| Dumbbell shrugs | 3 | 10 |
| Cable biceps curl | 3 | 12-10-10 |
| Smith machine squat | 4 | 12-9-9-11 |
| Seated leg curl | 3 | 10 |
| Standing calf raise | 3 | 15 |

The squat opens the leg block and the leg curl closes it from behind. Calves come last, with 15 reps and a weight that lets you finish them without bouncing.

## What gets cut

The two original tables added up to 23 exercises and 63 sets per cycle. A beginner does not need that volume to learn: with 20 sets in A and 23 in B there is room to recover and repeat the week.

Session A loses the parallel bar dips, the front raise, the pec deck, the triceps kickbacks and the overhead extension. Session B loses the back extension, the Scott curl, the alternating curl, the leg press and the leg extension. The squat and the leg curl cover the legs; the pulldown and the row cover the back; the cable curl and the triceps pushdown cover the arm.

The rule is simple: one movement per pattern stays, and the following week it comes back with more weight. Extra volume arrives when the body already tolerates the plan, not in the first week.

## How to add weight

When the last set comes out clean, with every rep and no loss of form, add 2.5 kilos to upper-body exercises and 5 kilos to leg exercises. If you fail two sessions in a row on the same exercise, drop 10 % and build back from there.

The mechanism is always the same. [Progressive overload](/en/progressive-overload-principle/) demands more work over time, and that extra can come from weight, one rep or one set. In this routine, weight is enough. Every four to six weeks, take a deload week with fewer sets and less weight.

## When to change plan

Stay on ABAB while you add weight almost every week. If you stall for two or three weeks, or once you reach eight, change the scheme. Before moving to the next plan, [training volume](/en/training-volume/) breaks down how many sets each group needs.

If you want to stay with the whole body, [full-body routines](/en/full-body-routines/) have variants by level. For barbell strength work, [strength routines](/en/strength-routines/) set the percentages.
