A **stretching routine** after your weights lets you train for far longer and reduces the number of injuries linked to poor flexibility.

When you stretch a muscle, more oxygen and nutrients reach its fibres, which improves recovery after exercise. Heavy bodybuilding work can also reduce the range of motion of several joints. Doing this stretching programme regularly prevents that problem.

## How the circuit works

We use postural stretches, the easiest to learn and perform. They must be done slowly so you do not stimulate the antagonist muscles. Once you reach a comfortable position, hold it for the stated seconds so the muscle fibres relax, lengthen and take in oxygen.

Do this circuit **after** your weight-training session, and keep the exercises in the order shown.

## The eight stretches and their hold times

1. **Shoulders (rear):** 20 seconds per arm.
2. **Triceps:** 20 seconds per arm.
3. **Chest:** 30 seconds per arm.
4. **Forearm flexors:** 30 seconds.
5. **Hips, glutes and lats:** 45 seconds per side.
6. **Glutes, psoas and hamstrings:** 45 seconds per leg.
7. **Quadriceps:** 45 seconds.
8. **Calves and hamstrings:** 30 seconds per leg.

## How to fit it into the session

It runs as a circuit: do all eight exercises in a row, then start again for another round. Two to four circuits per session are recommended.

If you are short on time, do only the first four after a torso session, or only the last four after a leg session.

At the end of every workout, do not forget to stretch your spine by hanging from a fixed bar.
