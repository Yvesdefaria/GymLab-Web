The quads take part in every squat, every leg press and every lunge, and they tolerate too many sets badly. The four routines here share the basic movements and differ in load, volume and rest. Picking one and keeping it for six to eight weeks beats rotating through all four.

The totals exclude warm-up work: two light sets on the first exercise are enough.

## Quad routine for size

A heavy movement, a leg press, a hack squat and leg extension to finish. The weight drops set by set and the reps rise, so the heaviest work comes first.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell squat | 5 | 6, 6, 8, 10, 12 |
| Leg press | 5 | 8, 8, 10, 10, 12 |
| Hack squat | 4 | 8, 8, 10, 12 |
| Leg extension | 3 | 8, 10, 12 |

That is 17 working sets. If the last twelve reps on the leg press come up clean and without bouncing, add weight next week. On the extension, the full range is worth more than two extra plates.

## Quad routine for strength

Fewer sets, heavier loads and long rests. The aim is not to pile up fatigue but to move a heavy weight with the technique intact.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell squat | 4 | 4, 4, 6, 8 |
| Single-leg leg press | 4 | 4, 4, 6, 8 |
| Hack squat | 3 | 4, 6, 6 |

> **Note:** rest two to three minutes between sets and keep the same weight on the first two sets of each exercise.

The single-leg press is the usual exception in a strength routine, and it has one concrete use: comparing the left side with the right. If one side moves two fewer reps at the same weight, that gap explains part of what happens in the two-legged squat. Start with the weak side.

## Fifteen-minute quad routine

Three exercises and nine sets with short rests, for a day with little time.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg extension | 3 | 6, 10, 15 |
| Leg press | 3 | 6, 10, 15 |
| Jump squat | 3 | To failure |

> **Note:** rest 30 seconds between sets.

A 30-second rest turns the session into density work: the heart rate stays high and the reps fall fast. That does not burn fat from the thigh. Fat is distributed according to body-fat level, not according to the muscle you train. What you do get is the ability to tolerate more work in less time.

The jump squat uses bodyweight and lands with the knee in line with the foot. If the landing hurts or makes noise, swap it for extensions.

## Quad routine for beginners

Four simple exercises, no free barbell at the start. The Smith machine squat guides the path and lets you learn the pattern without fighting your balance.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine squat | 4 | 10, 10, 12, 12 |
| Horizontal leg press | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |
| Lunges | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |
| Leg extension | 3 | 10, 10, 10 |

That is 13 sets. Three sets per accessory is the minimum for progress without piling up fatigue. For the first four weeks, pick a weight that leaves two reps in reserve on the last set.

## Foot position: what changes and what does not

A wider stance on the press or the squat adds adductor work and changes the hip angle. It does not isolate the vastus medialis or the vastus lateralis: the four heads of the quad share one tendon and contract together in any knee extension. The [quadriceps anatomy](/en/quadriceps-anatomy/) explains why, and the [quad training myths](/en/quadriceps-training-myths/) go through the variations sold as isolation. For the exact setup on the press, the guide to the [leg press foot position](/en/leg-press-foot-position/) has the details.

## Density, giant sets and the rest that is real

The old definition template chained extension, press, Smith front squat and sissy squat with under 30 seconds of rest. Its numbers still work as a density variant: 5×10, 12, 12, 15, 20 on the extension, 4×12, 12, 15, 15 on the press, 4×15, 15, 20, 20 on the front squat and 4×10, 12, 15, 20 on the sissy squat. It is a short, hard session, and it does not burn fat from the thigh.

The giant set is another density option, not a growth method on its own: four exercises back to back with no rest between them (extension, hack, press and front squat), ten reps each, four rounds and three minutes between rounds. That rest is part of the set: without it, the second round becomes a loose set.

The other old template puts two sets of thirty reps at the end of three exercises, with 4×10, 10, 30, 30 on the squat, the press and the extension and 30 to 45 seconds of rest. It is endurance work at a light weight. Thirty reps do not turn a set into a hypertrophy method because the number is round: they work if you get close to failure, and then technique breaks before the muscle does.

## How to progress without adding sets

Add weight when the last set comes up clean, and add a set only if you handled the previous one without losing technique. If the set count rises every week and the reps fall, you are not progressing: you are accumulating fatigue.
