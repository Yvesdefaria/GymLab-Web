It is one of the most demanding [cutting routines](/en/cutting-routines/): few exercises, short rests and sets that do not end when they should. The idea is simple: reach failure with a weight, remove load and keep going. The muscle works longer than a normal set allows.

Each session has cardio on both sides: 10 minutes at a moderate pace before, to warm up, and 20 to 30 minutes after. The [aerobic exercise guide](/en/aerobic-exercise/) explains how to dose it without eating into strength.

The 10 minutes before are not a full warm-up: they are the transition from the street to the bar. The 20 or 30 after add work without taking strength, because they arrive once the session is over.

## How to read the notation

When there are two numbers, the second is the total for the set after the drop: 9/11 means 9 reps to failure, remove weight and continue to 11. When there are three, each number is the failure point of one drop: 10/8/6 means 10 to failure, 8 more after the first drop and 6 more after the second.

The starting weight is the one that lets you reach failure on the first number. If 9/11 appears on the last bench press set, pick a weight you fail at rep nine, not one you could press for 15.

## Rest and warm-up

Between sets, 45 to 60 seconds. On exercises with more than three sets, the first is a warm-up and does not count; from the second on, the weight is the one that takes you to failure at the written reps. The clock is part of the method here, not an afterthought.

Failure stays on the last set of each exercise, and no set takes more than two drops. Chain three drops across five exercises and the session becomes an endurance test, with form breaking before the muscle does. The [muscle failure guide](/en/muscle-failure-training/) explains where the useful limit sits.

## Progression

When you complete every rep of the notation on every set, move up to the smallest increment next session. If the last set does not reach the first number, repeat the weight.

Sunday is rest. With sets to failure, six days in a row do not hold; walking does not count as training.

## Day 1: chest and biceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell bench press | 5 | 15, 12, 7, 7, 9/11 |
| Superset: incline dumbbell press + incline bench flyes | 3 | 8+10 |
| Cable crossovers | 3 | 12, 12, 12/15 |
| Parallel bar dips | 2 | to failure |
| Low-cable biceps curl | 3 | 15, 10/12, 10/12 |
| Seated alternating dumbbell curl | 3 | 12, 12, 12/15 |
| Scott bench curl | 3 | 10/12, 10/12, 15 |
| Concentration curl | 2 | 10/8/6 |

The compound set of incline press and flyes runs back to back, with no rest between the two exercises. On crossovers and curls, the number after the slash is the drop.

If an exercise is not in your gym, swap it for one with the same pattern: incline press for machine press, crossovers for flyes, dips for close-grip press. The sets and the notation stay.

| Superset: French press + close-grip bench press | 4 | 15+15, 10+10, 10+10, 10+10 |
| Reverse-grip pulldowns | 3 | 10/12, 10/12, 15 |
| Triceps kickbacks | 2 | 10/8/6 |

## Day 3: legs and shoulders

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg press | 5 | 15, 12, 7, 7, 9/11 |
| A1. Squat | 3 | 15, 10, 10 |
| A2. Lunges | 3 | 12, 10/12, 10/12 |
| Sissy squat | 3 | 15 |
| Barbell military press | 4 | 15, 7, 9/11, 9/11 |
| Lateral raise | 3 | 15, 10/12, 10/12 |
| Dumbbell reverse flyes | 3 | 15, 10/12, 10/12 |
| Shrug | 3 | 15 |

Squat and lunges run as a superset: one set of each, back to back, then rest. The scheme repeats three times. The sissy squat finishes the quads without loading the spine.

## Abs

On two of the three cardio days, at the end, do this circuit:

- Hanging leg raises + reverse crunches: 4 × 15 + 20-30.
- Floor oblique crunches: 4 × 30, no rest between sets.
- Cable crunch + right-angle leg crunches: 4 × 15 + 20-30.

That is twelve sets in total, split across two sessions. The circuit is short on purpose. If leg day falls the next day, cut it to two exercises.

## What stays out

Do not add sets or exercises. The plan already carries eleven sets of biceps on Monday and three triceps exercises on Wednesday; more only lengthens recovery. If performance drops two weeks in a row, check sleep and food before the plan. That rule applies to the whole block, not just one bad day. The [overtraining guide](/en/overtraining/) describes the signs.

To understand why this technique works and when it does not, the [drop sets guide](/en/drop-sets/) and the [high-intensity training guide](/en/high-intensity-training/) go deeper.
