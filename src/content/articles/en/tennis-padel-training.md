On court you win with your legs before your arm. A stronger player reaches the ball earlier, holds the shot with less fatigue and repeats the movement in the third set. The programme covers that work without turning it into a bodybuilding routine.

## Why strength matters in tennis and padel

Every shot starts on the ground. Force is produced in the hips and legs, travels through the trunk and ends in the shoulder and wrist. If one link fails, the shot loses power or the next joint pays the price.

Strength does not fight mobility. If every exercise runs through a full range without bouncing, range of motion holds and the sporting movement gains room.

## Hips and legs

The full squat is the main lift of the block. The calves drive the serve and the volley, and the adductors brake and push in lateral movement. That is why the programme repeats adductors twice a week instead of leaving them as an optional accessory.

## Back and rotation

Leg power does not reach the shoulder without a strong back. A tennis player twists, brakes and repeats hundreds of times per match, and the lower back, traps and lats absorb part of that work. Good mornings cover the posterior chain and the clean trains hip extension with speed.

## Shoulders, chest and wrist

The incline press and the overhead press allow more range than the flat press, which suits the serving pattern. Wrist rolls use a weight tied to a rope and a stick: three sets until the forearm burns, no swinging.

## Dips and the elbow

Dips stay on Friday with one condition: the elbow does not lock and the body does not bounce at the bottom. Lower until the arm forms a right angle, shoulders away from the ears, and push up through the chest.

A player with elbow or shoulder pain swaps dips for a close-grip dumbbell press. What does not work is programming five sets of an exercise and warning in the same article that the exercise is dangerous.

## The three-day programme

Monday and Friday are the heavy days; Wednesday drops the load and repeats the patterns. Wednesday uses 70 % of Monday's squat weight. Ramp-up sets do not need counting: two or three light sets and then work.

### Monday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full squat | 5 + 1 | 5 and 8 |
| Power clean | 5 | 5 |
| Incline bench press | 5 + 1 | 5 and 8 |
| Calves | 3 | 30 |
| Wrist rolls | 3 | Max |

### Wednesday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat at 70 % | 5 | 5 |
| Good mornings | 4 | 8 |
| Overhead press | 3 + 3 + 1 | 5, 3 and 8 |
| Straight-arm pullover | 2 | 20 |
| Adductor machine | 2 | 20 |
| Wrist rolls | 3 | Max |

### Friday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full squat | 3 + 2 + 1 | 5, 3 and 8 |
| Upright row | 5 | 5 |
| Incline bench press | 2 + 3 | 5 and 3 |
| Dips | 5 | 8 |
| Calves | 3 | 30 |
| Adductor machine | 2 | 20 |
| Wrist rolls | 3 | Max |

## How each lift progresses

The rule is the same for the five main lifts: when the last set of five comes up clean and the back-off set of eight needs no help, add 2.5 kilos on presses and 5 on the squat and the clean. On dips, add a rep before adding weight.

If you miss the same exercise twice in a row, drop 10 % and build again. On wrist rolls, add weight once the sets of max reps pass twenty.

## Rest and flexibility

Rest three minutes between squat and clean sets and two minutes on the other big lifts. The [rest between sets guide](/en/rest-between-sets/) explains why cutting it changes the stimulus.

Stretch between sets and at the end, with attention to the adductors and calves. Tennis and padel already carry a high aerobic load; adding two running sessions a week takes recovery away from the programme.
