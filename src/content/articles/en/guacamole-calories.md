One hundred grams of avocado supply about 160 kcal, 15 g of fat, 7 g of fibre and 2 g of protein. Homemade guacamole adds tomato, onion and lime, so the figure stays similar. The problem appears with the spoon that does not stop.

## How many calories guacamole has

The base recipe carries no fat beyond the avocado. A medium avocado yields about 200 g of flesh, 320 kcal, and with tomato, onion, coriander and lime it becomes three or four portions of 40 to 50 g. Each one lands at 60 to 80 kcal.

The maths changes as soon as an extra appears. A spoon of cream or cream cheese adds saturated fat and calories the avocado does not need. Fried nachos add more than the salsa: 100 g sit near 500 kcal.

## The base recipe, without additions

Ripe avocados, very finely chopped onion, seeded tomato, coriander, lime juice and salt. Mash with a fork, do not blend. Texture matters: a smooth paste is eaten faster and fills less.

The creaminess is already in the avocado fat. Adding cream, mayonnaise or cream cheese doubles the calorie density and covers the flavour. If the guacamole tastes flat, lime and salt fix it before any dairy does.

## Why it fills you up and why the portion grows

Avocado fat is mostly monounsaturated, and fibre sits near 7 g per 100 g. That combination slows digestion and keeps hunger away for a while. The guide to [fats](/en/dietary-fats/) explains why quality matters, and also why 9 kcal per gram do not vanish because the fat is good.

In salsa form you chew less. The same amount of avocado in slices lasts longer on the plate than mashed in a bowl. Serve the portion and put the rest away before you sit down, instead of eating from the shared bowl.

## The supermarket jar: read the label

Packaged guacamole ranges from avocado salsa to a product with oil, starch, sugar and preservatives. The label settles it in two steps. First, the ingredient order: avocado should come first. Second, the avocado percentage, if the pack declares it.

Always compare per 100 g, not per pack. Two jars of the same size can differ by 100 kcal per 100 g. If refined oils, syrup or modified starches appear on the list, the product is closer to an industrial sauce than to the recipe. The [low-fat diet](/en/low-fat-diet/) guide reminds you that cutting fat is not the same as cutting calories when the manufacturer compensates with sugar or starch.

## Carotenoids need fat

Vitamins A, D, E and K and carotenoids are fat-soluble: without fat, the intestine absorbs a smaller share. Studies with salads show that adding a fat source increases carotenoid absorption between two and four times, not without limit. The amount needed is small, a few grams per meal.

A spoon of guacamole on the salad does that job and adds flavour. That way part of the vitamins is not left unabsorbed.

## How much and when, by goal

In a deficit, 40 to 50 g as a garnish, and subtract the oil from the same meal if you want to keep the total. In a surplus, 80 to 100 g over rice, potato or toast adds quality calories without filling the stomach.

The vehicle decides half the result. Carrot, celery, cucumber or pepper sticks add volume and micronutrients. A [rice cake](/en/rice-cakes/) or wholemeal toast works when you need something crisp. Nuts and avocado share the same density warning: a small portion, as the guide to [nuts](/en/nuts-nutrition/) recalls, adds up quickly.

Rye crispbread is another possible base, at 30 to 50 kcal per slice. Its page explains how to use it without turning it into a meal in disguise.

## Questions that always come up

Guacamole keeps for 24 to 48 hours in the fridge if it is covered with film pressed onto the surface and a few drops of lime. The brown colour is oxidation. Remove the layer and the rest is fine. Eating it daily is possible if it fits the total and does not displace protein or vegetables.

A whole avocado and guacamole supply the same, except that the salsa adds tomato and onion. The difference is how much you eat without noticing. For a cutting meal, the [1,420 kcal day](/en/fat-loss-diet/) shows where a 40 g portion fits without breaking the balance.
