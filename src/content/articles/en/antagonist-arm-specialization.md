The arms are the smallest group in the programme and also the one that gets the most work without asking: every bench press loads the triceps and every row loads the biceps. Specialization gives them their own session, with the muscle rested and an order that uses the alternation.

## Placing the arm session in the week

During the four weeks of specialization, take a little volume off the rest of the body. If the chest, back and shoulders keep the same number of sets, the arms arrive tired at their own session and the specialization does not show.

The arm session goes as far as possible from the upper-body days. A tired arm takes away from the bench press, and a sore arm after a back session cannot perform the next day.

## Why pair biceps and triceps

Pairing antagonists saves time: two exercises in the time of one and a half, with no waiting between them. It also keeps the joint warm while you work, because the elbow moves through both directions of the range.

The rest is physiology with a name: a muscle performs slightly better when its antagonist contracted just before. The effect exists and shows up in an extra rep or two, but it does not turn the superset into a growth trick. If volume and load do not climb, the order of the exercises will not save the session. The full family of variations lives in the guides to [supersets](/en/supersets/) and [alternating sets](/en/alternating-sets/).

## The weekly split

| Day | Session | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Biceps and triceps in supersets | Triceps emphasis |
| Tuesday | Legs | Lower body |
| Wednesday | Chest and shoulders | Push |
| Thursday | Rest | Recovery |
| Friday | Triceps and biceps in supersets | Biceps emphasis |
| Saturday | Back | Pull |
| Sunday | Rest | Recovery |

## Day 1: triceps emphasis

Each pair of rows is one superset. The first exercise uses a weight that would allow fifteen reps, but you only complete five or six and never reach failure: it primes the antagonist without exhausting it. The second exercise uses a weight that allows the prescribed reps with good form.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline-bench dumbbell curl | 3 | 5-6 |
| Triceps pushdown | 3 | 6-8 |
| Scott bench curl | 3 | 5-6 |
| Overhead dumbbell extension | 3 | 6-8 |
| Alternating dumbbell curl | 3 | 5-6 |
| Skull crusher (French press) | 3 | 8-10 |
| Barbell curl | 3 | 5-6 |
| Weighted dips | 3 | 10-12 |
| Standing EZ-bar curl | 2 | 4-6 |

The last row is the compensatory set: a heavy biceps exercise, taken to failure, that balances the triceps emphasis of the session.

> **Note:** the supersets run back to back, with no rest between the two exercises. Between one superset and the next, rest as long as you need to repeat the same load with the same technique. Warm-up sets are not counted.

## Day 2: biceps emphasis

The order reverses. Now the triceps opens each pair and the biceps benefits from the previous work, which is exactly what did not happen on Monday.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partial skull crusher | 3 | 5-6 |
| Partial barbell curl | 3 | 6-8 |
| Reverse-grip cable extension | 3 | 5-6 |
| Barbell curl | 3 | 6-8 |
| Machine triceps dip | 3 | 5-6 |
| Dumbbell preacher curl | 3 | 8-10 |
| Overhead dumbbell extension | 3 | 5-6 |
| Incline-bench dumbbell curl | 3 | 10-12 |
| Close-grip bench press | 2 | 4-6 |

The compensatory set on day 2 is for the triceps, taken to failure with a heavy weight, for the same reason: what was in surplus on Monday is missing on Friday.

## How to adjust the routine without breaking it

The exercises can be swapped for ones that suit you better, as long as the structure holds: one exercise for each arm, alternated, with the heavier one of the pair second. The 5-6 with a light weight is part of the method and does a specific job: it primes the antagonist without exhausting it.

If a superset does not let you recover for the next one, stretch the rest between pairs before lowering the weight. And if the elbow complains, swap the skull crusher for cable extensions or machine dips, which keep the arm closer to the body.

## After the four weeks

When the cycle ends, go back to one or two arm sessions a week, or split biceps with back and triceps with chest. Keep the superset if time is the problem; if load is the goal, separate the exercises and rest longer between them. Give it at least eight weeks before repeating the block.
