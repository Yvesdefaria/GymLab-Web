The Mr. Olympia decides who is the best bodybuilder in the world for the following year. Joe Weider launched it in 1965 at the Brooklyn Academy of Music, and it has been held every year since. The [Weider principles](/en/weider-principles/) are the other half of what Weider left the sport.

The table covers all 62 editions, from Larry Scott in 1965 to Nick Walker in 2026. Twenty different men have won the title at least once.

## The era of two divisions

Between 1974 and 1979 the contest awarded a heavyweight and a lightweight title alongside the overall champion. The table lists the overall winner, the name that stays in the record: in 1976 Ken Waller took the heavyweight and the overall title went to Franco Columbu; in 1979 the overall was Frank Zane and the heavyweight was Mike Mentzer.

The trophy the champion receives is a statuette of Eugen Sandow, the late nineteenth-century bodybuilder the award is named after.

## Title records

Lee Haney and Ronnie Coleman share the record with eight consecutive wins. Arnold Schwarzenegger and Phil Heath have seven, Dorian Yates six and Jay Cutler four. Sergio Oliva and Frank Zane won three times each.

Eight champions have won once: Chris Dickerson, Samir Bannout, Dexter Jackson, Shawn Rhoden, Brandon Curry, Hadi Choopan, Samson Dauda and Nick Walker.

The table also shows how the judging criteria have changed. The titles of the seventies rewarded symmetry and lines; from the nineties on, the lean went to mass and density. Both eras sit in the same list, which is why the argument over the best physique in history never closes.
