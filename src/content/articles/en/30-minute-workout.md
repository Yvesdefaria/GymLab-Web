The original plan asked for one set per exercise and failure on every movement. That leaves no way to measure the load or repeat the session. Here each pair has three sets, a two-rep margin and a weight you can write down.

## How the 30-minute workout is built

A pair is a big exercise followed by one that complements it. Do the first set of the first movement, move to the second without sitting down, and take the long rest when both are done. Five pairs cover legs, chest, back, shoulders and arms. Abs are an optional two-set finisher. The long rest is what lets the next pair move a real weight; without it, five pairs turn into a light circuit.

## The fixed warm-up

Five minutes on a bike or rower, hip and shoulder mobility, and 30 bodyweight split squats. Then two ramp-up sets on the first exercise of pair A. No 10 or 15 minutes of cardio: the warm-up prepares you and does not eat into the session.

## The five pairs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Dumbbell squat | 3 | 10 |
| A2. Romanian deadlift | 3 | 10 |
| B1. Incline dumbbell press | 3 | 8-12 |
| B2. Cable fly | 3 | 12 |
| C1. One-arm dumbbell row | 3 | 8-12 |
| C2. Lat pulldown | 3 | 10 |
| D1. Machine shoulder press | 3 | 8-12 |
| D2. Lateral raise | 3 | 12 |
| E1. French press | 3 | 10 |
| E2. Seated dumbbell curl | 3 | 10 |

> **Note:** Each pair runs back to back. The long rest comes when the pair is done, not between its two exercises.

## How to choose the weight

Leave two or three reps in reserve on the first set and one or two on the last. If technique breaks before rep 8, lower the weight. Rest 60 to 90 seconds between the two exercises of a pair and two minutes after completing it. In 30 minutes, the clock does not forgive long sets. The last set should feel hard and repeatable at the same time; if you lose control of the lowering, stop the set there and note the weight.

## The progression

Add reps within the range until you hit the ceiling. When you reach the top on all three sets, add 2.5 kilos and return to the bottom of the range. Every four weeks, swap one exercise in each pair for another of the same pattern. That rotation prevents a plateau and keeps the session at 30 to 40 minutes. If two weeks pass without moving the weight or the reps, drop 10 percent and build again. A stall is usually recovery, not effort.

## The week

Monday, Wednesday and Friday. If you only have two days, keep legs, back and chest; sacrifice arms. Abs are optional and should not lengthen the session. If a day runs short, do not cut the warm-up: drop the arm pair. A missed day is not made up the next day: carry on with the session that is due. Three consistent days beat four owed ones.

The plan is working if the first set of each pair matches last week's weight with one more rep, or adds 2.5 kilos at the same reps. If three sessions pass without moving either number, check sleep and food before adding sets. And if Friday arrives with joint pain, drop the arm pair and lower the squat weight by 10 percent.

## What to cut when the clock is tight

The arm pair is the first to go. Next, the second exercise of each pair, as long as the first covers the main pattern. Never remove the warm-up or the ramp-up sets: they are what lets the first exercise run well.
