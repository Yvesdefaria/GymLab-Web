Two days, four sessions, two months. The structure is so simple that the temptation is to add work: a third day, more exercises, more sets. The plan already has 154 weekly sets spread over four sessions, so the first decision is to respect the split before expanding it.

## Two days, repeated twice

Monday and Thursday train the push: chest, shoulders, triceps and abs. Tuesday and Friday, the pull: back, legs and biceps. Every muscle group gets two stimuli a week, which is the definition of high frequency.

Five minutes of easy cardio before the first set to raise temperature and pulse. That is not a full warm-up: give one or two light sets to the first exercise of each group.

This approach belongs to the [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) block. If you prefer a four-day plan instead of two, the [high-volume four-day routine](/en/high-volume-four-day-routine/) is the closest alternative.

## Day 1: chest, shoulders, triceps and abs

### Chest

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 8-10 |
| Incline press (compound set) | 4 | 10 |
| Incline fly (compound set) | 4 | 12-15 |

The incline press and the incline fly run back to back, with no rest between them: they are a compound set. Rest comes when the pair is done. If the chest is the area that responds least, the [chest routines](/en/chest-routines/) page has plans with more frequency and more press variations.

### Shoulders

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell military press | 4 | 6-10 |
| Lateral raise (compound set) | 4 | 8-12 |
| Bent-over reverse fly (compound set) | 4 | 8-12 |

### Triceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| French press | 4 | 10 |
| Parallel bar dips | 4 | 8-12 |

Dips use body weight: when you pass twelve clean reps in all four sets, add load.

### Abs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reverse crunches | 3 | 30-50 |
| Hanging leg raises | 3 | 15 |
| High-cable crunch | 3 | 15-20 |

## Day 2: back, legs and biceps

### Back

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell row | 4 | 8 |
| Front lat pulldown | 4 | 8-12 |
| Seated cable row | 3 | 10 |
| Deadlift | 3 | 10-12 |

### Legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 4 | 8-15 |
| Leg press | 4 | 10-15 |
| Leg extension (superset) | 3 | 10-15 |

| Standing calf raise (compound set) | 3 | 10-20 |

Extensions and leg curl run back to back, and so do the two calf raises. If legs are your priority, the [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) page has plans with more lower-body volume.

### Biceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell curl | 4 | 8 |

## The rest ladder

Rests are not a detail: they are part of the stimulus. This routine uses three steps:

- **90 seconds between sets** of the same exercise.
- **2 minutes after a compound set or superset**, because the pair accumulates more fatigue than a single set.
- **2 or 3 minutes between exercises**, above all before the squat and the deadlift.

[Rest between sets](/en/rest-between-sets/) has its own logic: the heavier and longer the set, the longer the nervous system takes to produce again.

## Weekly sets per muscle

Count the sets per session and multiply by two. Chest: 12 per session, 24 a week. Shoulders: 12 and 24. Back: 14 and 28. Legs: 14 and 28. Triceps and biceps: 8 and 16 each. Abs: 9 and 18. That is 154 weekly sets in total.

Those are high numbers. The reduction rule is simple: if the second session of the week arrives with the area still sore, or reps drop on the first set, remove one set from each exercise of that area in that session before touching the load. If the problem repeats for two weeks, cut the first session too.

[Training volume](/en/training-volume/) is the variable you are moving, so measure it instead of guessing by feel.

## Cardio: five minutes before, twenty or thirty after

After the weights, 20 to 30 minutes of steady cardio if the goal is to lose fat while gaining volume as lean as possible. If that is not your goal, the weight work already fills the session.

## How to progress over two months

Use the weight that lets you complete the prescribed reps with clean technique. When you reach the top of the range on every set, add the smallest jump your equipment allows and start again from the bottom of the range: from 8-10 back to 8 with more weight, from 12-15 to 12, from 30-50 to 30.

At eight weeks, change plans. Two months on the same split is a reasonable limit before adaptation runs out of new stimulus.

## What must not change

Technique beats weight on both days. On the barbell row and the deadlift, keep the back straight through the whole range. On the squat, full depth and knees in line with the feet. On the military press, do not arch the lower back to drive the bar up.

If a set breaks down technically, it is over. That is the one failure the plan does not allow.
