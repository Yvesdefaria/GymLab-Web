The structure is simple: three sessions a week, each one pairing muscle groups that do not compete. The muscle that just worked rests while the other pushes, and the session fits inside the time a class lasts.

Monday, Wednesday and Friday is the most comfortable split. Any sequence that leaves a day between sessions works. If you have been training for less than a year, start with the beginner version and move to the advanced one after 8 to 12 weeks.

The days do not touch. The weight session lasts 30 to 45 minutes and cardio goes elsewhere, at another point of the day or on the off days.

## Day 1: chest and back

In the beginner version each block pairs one chest exercise with one back exercise. That is 2 or 3 sets of 15 reps per exercise and 30 seconds or less between the two movements of the pair.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Bench press | 2-3 | 15 |
| A2. Seated row | 2-3 | 15 |
| B1. Lat pulldowns | 2-3 | 15 |
| B2. Incline press | 2-3 | 15 |
| C1. Cable crossovers | 2-3 | 15 |
| C2. Dumbbell pullover | 2-3 | 15 |

The advanced version turns the pair into a tri-set. The first exercise is an isolation move and works as [pre-fatigue](/en/pre-fatigue/): it tires the main muscle before the compound movement arrives, and that is the one that takes the hardest reps.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Cable crossovers | 3-4 | 20 |
| A2. Bench press | 3-4 | 15 |
| A3. Incline press | 3-4 | 15 |
| B1. Dumbbell pullover | 3-4 | 20 |
| B2. Cable pulldowns | 3-4 | 15 |
| B3. Seated row | 3-4 | 15 |

## Day 2: legs

The leg pairs alternate a quad exercise with a hamstring one, and the session closes with calves. The numbers match day 1: 2 or 3 sets of 15 for beginners.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Leg press | 2-3 | 15 |
| A2. Lying leg curl | 2-3 | 15 |
| B1. Seated leg curl | 2-3 | 15 |
| B2. Lunge | 2-3 | 15 |
| C1. Leg extensions | 2-3 | 15 |
| C2. Seated calf raise | 2-3 | 15 |

For advanced lifters the last block is a calf pair, not a tri-set: two exercises cover the soleus and the gastrocnemius.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Leg extensions | 3-4 | 20 |
| A2. Leg press | 3-4 | 15 |
| A3. Lunge | 3-4 | 15 |
| B1. Lying leg curl | 3-4 | 20 |
| B2. Stiff-leg deadlift on the Smith machine | 3-4 | 15 |
| B3. Seated leg curl | 3-4 | 15 |
| C1. Seated calf raise | 3-4 | 15 |
| C2. Standing calf raise | 3-4 | 15 |

Shoulder and arm day uses the same scheme. In the advanced version the first tri-set mixes a lateral raise, a press and a row, and the other two blocks are biceps and triceps pairs.

## The drop sets

On the last set of the exercises marked with an asterisk you drop the load. Pick a weight that leaves you 6 to 8 reps, reach [muscle failure](/en/muscle-failure-training/), strip 40 % to 50 % of the load and take another 6 to 8 reps straight away, with no rest.

That double failure is what stretches the set and raises intensity without adding minutes to the session. It only goes on the last set and only on the marked exercises: put it on every set and the quality of the first ones falls apart.

## Density progression

The load stays put and the rest comes down. Week one, 30 seconds between the exercises of the tri-set and 2 minutes between blocks. Week two, 25 seconds and 1 minute 45 seconds. Week three, 20 seconds and 1 minute 30 seconds, the floor of the plan.

When you complete all three or four sets with clean technique at those rests, the next week holds the load and deloads: two sets per block, the week-one rests and the same exercises. Then you start again with a slightly heavier load. Pairs and tri-sets are explained in the [supersets](/en/supersets/) and [tri-sets](/en/tri-sets/) guides.

## The abs

At the end of every session, pick two or three of these exercises and do one or two sets of 15 to 30 slow reps: basic crunch with the feet up, reverse crunch on a bench, knees to chest on a Roman chair, knees to chest, oblique crunch and cable crunch.

Change the combination every session. Abs respond to variety and control. Two well-run exercises at the end are enough; more sets just make the session longer.

## Where it fits in a cut

The plan covers the strength work of the week and keeps cardio apart, at another point of the day or on the off days. If you prefer to keep weights and cardio completely separate, with three sessions of each, the [weights and cardio cutting routine](/en/weights-and-cardio-cutting-routine/) lays out that other structure.

To pick a plan based on the days you have and the cardio you can handle, the [cutting routines](/en/cutting-routines/) index gathers the options in the block. The weekly volume of this scheme is high: if the joints start complaining, drop a block before touching the rests.
