A structured, customisable template can mark the difference between success and stagnation. You do not need a complicated format: it just has to record what you are going to do and let you write down what you did.

## What the template should capture

Each row is an exercise and each column records the weight, the reps and the number of sets. With that data you know what is due today and what you did last time.

The template also sets the exercise order. That order matters as much as the content, because the most demanding movements should come while you still have strength.

## Why it matters

An organised template gives every gym day a specific purpose. Knowing which exercises, sets, reps and weights you will use removes improvisation and makes sure you train each muscle group in a balanced way.

It also makes tracking easier. A detailed log shows progress in strength, endurance or body composition, and tells you when to add weight or change an exercise.

Structure also keeps monotony away. With a clear view of your training it is easier to alternate exercises and periodise loads, so your muscles keep facing a challenge.

Finally, the template supports motivation and discipline. Seeing the plan and reviewing what you achieved gives every session a tangible, measurable purpose.

## Advantages of taking it to the gym

A portable version, on your phone or on a printed sheet, improves the experience in three ways.

- **Practical and fast**: you check and tick off each set in real time, without distractions.
- **Flexible**: you adjust on the spot if a machine is busy or you need to change the session.
- **Less wasted time**: you avoid deciding as you go or stretching an exercise longer than needed.

## How to use it session by session

Before you start, review the whole session and have last time's weight within reach. During the workout, log each finished set, not from memory at the end.

If an exercise does not go as planned, write it down. That information is worth more than a perfect plan that is never checked against reality.

## When to change it

The template is not a fixed contract. When progress stalls, adjust the loads or change the order and the exercises. Periodisation exists precisely to stop the same stimulus from becoming invisible.
