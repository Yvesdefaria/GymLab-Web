If you have been training for a while, the title may sound like a joke. Plenty of sedentary people want to get fit, and for them running half an hour without stopping is an odyssey. What they need is a planned progression: each week adds a little work and each block repeats the dose before moving up.

## The week-by-week routine

The table is the full progression. Do it three times a week, preferably Monday, Wednesday and Friday. If you are not completely sedentary and have no obesity issues, you can start at week 7.

By the end of the progression you have one of the capacities that help you stay fit, healthier and able to change your physique.

## How to progress

The progression moves in two directions at once: total session time rises from 20 to 30 minutes and the walking stretch gets shorter. The first two weeks are walking. From week 3 you alternate 30 to 60 seconds of jogging with five minutes of walking, and every two weeks that walking break drops to four, three and two minutes.

In week 11 the pattern changes: two minutes of jogging and one of walking. In week 12 you start with 20 minutes of continuous jogging and add two minutes each week until you reach 30.

Do not skip the repeat weeks. The plan includes them so you reach the next step with the previous work settled, so treat them as part of the progression rather than a gap to fill with more.

## Mistakes to avoid

**Starting at week 7 without meeting the conditions.** The jump only applies if you are not sedentary and have no obesity issues. If you start from zero, begin at week 1 and let the progression raise the time little by little.

**Adding sessions outside the plan.** The progression asks for three days with a rest day in between. The free days are part of the plan, so do not fill them with another run: your body needs that margin to absorb the work.

**Changing the order of the weeks.** Each two-week block raises the duration or shortens the walking, and the order is set so the change arrives once you have done the previous dose. Altering it turns a gradual progression into jumps with no base.

When you finish, the next step is to hold the 30 minutes and raise the pace.
