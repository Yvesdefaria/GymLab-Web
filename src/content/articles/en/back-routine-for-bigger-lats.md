Many people neglect their back because they cannot see it in the mirror, so all their attention goes to the chest and arms. The result is an unbalanced physique. This session corrects that with four exercises and a finish.

## How this lat routine works

You start with pull-ups. Then come the heavy movements: the Smith machine row, the rope cable row for a longer range, and straight-arm pulldowns to work the lats fully.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pull-ups | As many as needed | 25 total |
| Smith machine row | 4 | 10, 8, 6, 6 |
| Rope cable row | 3 | 10, 8, 8 |
| Straight-arm cable pulldowns | 2 | 10 |

## Pull-ups: sets until you reach 25 reps

Do as many sets as you need to complete 25 reps in total. Jump to the bar and grip it with your hands slightly wider than shoulder width.

Start with your arms straight and your body dead. Keep your elbows pointing out as you pull up until your chin grazes the bar. Lower slowly to the start and do not let your feet touch the floor between reps.

If you have not mastered the movement yet, the [pull-ups](/en/pull-ups/) guide has the full progression.

## Smith machine row and cable row

On the Smith machine row, lower the safety supports as far as they go. Grip the bar at shoulder width, hinge at the hips to almost 90 degrees, look forward and arch your lower back.

Keep the lower back arch and drive your elbows toward the ceiling to bring the bar to your lower chest. Return to the start and add weight on each set. The base technique is the [barbell row](/en/barbell-row/), with the Smith machine saving you the balance work.

On the rope cable row, attach a rope handle to the low pulley. Sit with your torso upright and your arms forward, gripping one end of the rope in each hand. Without momentum, pull the rope toward your midsection and separate it as it comes closer. Rotate your shoulders back on each rep before returning to the start.

## Straight-arm pulldowns to isolate the lats

Use a pronated grip on a short straight bar attached to a high pulley. Extend your arms and keep them straight throughout the exercise.

Pull the bar toward your thighs by flexing the lats, then return to the start. Do not let the plates touch between reps: keep tension on the lats.

## The finishing touch: isometrics and negatives

Finish back on the pull-up bar with two closers. The first is an isometric hold in the top position for 10 to 20 seconds, with a brief rest before repeating.

The second is negative pull-ups: push with your feet to reach the top and descend slowly from there to the start. Repeat the sequence 3 to 5 times so your back gets the same growth opportunities as the rest of your muscles.
