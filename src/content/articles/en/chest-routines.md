The chest responds to load and volume, and it tolerates more frequency than other muscle groups because it shares work with the shoulder and the triceps on every press. What decides the result is the session: how much weight you move, with what control, and in what order.

## How to organise a chest session

You can pair the chest with almost any muscle. The usual choice is biceps or legs, and lifters on a push-pull split train it with shoulders and triceps. No pairing is mandatory: pick the one that lets you reach the first press fresh.

### Intensity comes first

If you want larger, fuller pectorals, the session has to be hard. Use the heaviest weight that still allows good execution and focus on firing fibres on every rep. Squeeze at the contraction as if the chest were a sponge and you wanted the water out of it.

### Strict style is not optional

The chest needs powerful, controlled movement. The eccentric phase, the lowering, is where most fibres get recruited when you control it; the lift can be explosive with the heaviest weight you can handle. Lowering fast and pushing with momentum turns the press into a shoulder exercise.

### Warm-up, with numbers

Spend five or six minutes raising body temperature and start light on the first press. If your working set is a 45-kilo dumbbell press for 10 reps, the warm-up looks like this:

- 20 kilos x 12 reps
- 30 kilos x 6 reps
- 40 kilos x 3 reps

The reps go down because the warm-up prepares muscles and nervous system without emptying them. If the light set leaves you tired, you warmed up too much.

> **Note:** the routines on this page do not include warm-up sets. Unless a routine says otherwise, rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

## The most complete chest routine in the block

It mixes multi-joint and isolation work and breaks the usual order. The rep range is wide to reach as many fibres as possible.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline press in the Smith machine or bench press | 6\* | 15, 15, 8, 8, 6, 20 |
| Machine chest fly | 4 | 12, 12, 10, 8 |
| Flat or incline dumbbell press | 3 | 10 |
| High-cable crossover | 3 | 15, 12, 12 |

> **\*** The first two sets are warm-up sets.

If the incline press comes first, the dumbbell press goes on a flat bench. If you start with the bench press, the dumbbells go on an incline bench.

## Chest routine for general volume

Three variables hold size together: compound exercises, high volume and 8 to 12 reps. That is 17 sets in total, with five for each of the first two exercises. Parallel-bar dips carry added weight because many people pass twelve reps with bodyweight alone.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline press | 5 | 12, 10, 8, 6, 6 |
| Flat dumbbell bench press | 5 | 12, 10, 10, 8, 8 |
| Incline bench fly | 4 | 12, 10, 8, 8 |
| Weighted parallel-bar dips | 3 | 12, 10, 8 |

## 15-minute chest routine

Cut rest to the minimum: three Smith machine presses and one machine fly to close.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline press in the Smith machine | 3 | 15, 10, 6 |
| Bench press in the Smith machine | 3 | 15, 10, 6 |
| Decline press in the Smith machine | 3 | 15, 10, 6 |
| Machine fly | 1 | 50 |

> **\*** Do not rest more than 30 seconds between sets or more than two minutes between exercises.

## Giant-set chest routine

Four exercises back to back with no rest. A [giant set](/en/giant-sets/) raises intensity and shortens the session.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline bench fly | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| Bench press | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| Decline dumbbell press | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |
| Machine fly | 4 | 10, 10, 10, 10 |

> **\*** A giant set is the four exercises back to back, without resting between them. Rest three minutes before the next round.

## Muscular-endurance chest routine

More reps and less rest. It fits a definition phase, although fat loss is decided by diet.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline dumbbell press | 5 | 10, 12, 12, 15, 20 |
| Dumbbell fly | 4 | 12, 12, 15, 15 |
| Dumbbell pullover | 4 | 15, 15, 20, 20 |
| Weighted parallel-bar dips | 4 | 10, 12, 15, 20 |

> **\*** Do not rest more than 45 seconds between sets.

## Chest routine for strength

Fewer reps and a lot of weight. Volume drops, but the load you move goes up.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 8, 6, 4, 4 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 4 | 8, 6, 4, 4 |
| Decline bench press | 3 | 6, 6, 4 |
| Dumbbell fly | 3 | 6, 6, 4 |

> **\*** Rest two to three minutes between sets. The pump arrives either way; what you are after here is strength.

## Maximum-pump routine

Cables keep tension through the whole range, and that fills the muscle with blood. Two heavy sets and two sets of thirty reps per exercise.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press in the Smith machine | 4 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |
| Cable fly on a flat bench | 4 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |
| Cable fly on an incline bench | 4 | 10, 10, 30, 30 |

## Chest routine for the lower pectoral

Emphasising one area is hard because the pectoral works as a whole. Even so, this routine concentrates the work on the lower part with a decline press and low crossovers.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decline press in the Smith machine | 4 | 12, 12, 10, 10 |
| Low-cable crossover | 4 | 10, 10, 8, 8 |
| Flat dumbbell bench press | 3 | 10, 12, 15 |
| Chest dip machine | 3 | To failure |

## Chest routine for the upper pectoral

Incline work also loads the shoulder. After several weeks of incline pressing you will feel the deltoids tired, so alternate with a flat press and give them a break.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incline press in the Smith machine | 5 | 15, 12, 10, 6 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 5 | 10, 10, 10 |
| Incline bench fly | 4 | 12, 10, 8 |
| Push-ups with feet elevated | 3 | To failure |

## Which routine to pick and how long to keep it

Pick one routine for the next six to eight weeks. A new plan every week makes it impossible to know what worked and multiplies volume without control. For size, start with general volume or the complete routine in this block. For strength, the strength routine and the guide to [building bench press strength](/en/bench-press-strength/) work together: one gives you the sets, the other the method to break the plateau.

The chest tolerates two sessions a week if total volume is planned. When you split the work across two days, leave at least 48 hours between them and cut the sets in each session so the total does not explode. [Training volume](/en/training-volume/) explains how to count it.

## Mistakes that slow chest progress

Lowering the bar without control, bouncing it off the chest and flaring the elbows are the three most expensive faults. All three turn a chest exercise into a shoulder exercise with less range. The right rhythm is a two-second lowering, a soft touch and a powerful lift.

The fourth mistake is order. If you reach the main press after three isolation exercises, you move less weight than you can and the stimulus falls short.
