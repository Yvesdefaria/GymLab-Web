This page is not a full plan. It gathers six blocks, one per muscle, so you can use them as a template for one session or as part of the one you already follow. Do not train all six in a row or chain one per day.

## How to read the tables

Each row is an exercise, with its sets and its rep ladder. Reps separated by commas are done in that order: for example, 6, 6, 15, 20 means four sets with those reps. The asterisk marks the reps that take a partner's help.

Rest is a reference, not a law: 45 to 60 seconds after sets of 6 reps and 30 seconds after sets of 15 or 20. If you need more to complete the set, take it; the clock does not rule over form.

## Chest: forced reps

The chest block uses forced reps: after failure, a partner helps you complete 2 or 3 more. The set gets longer and the muscle takes more tension than it could generate alone.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decline press | 5 | 6, 6, 10, 15 assisted, 15 assisted |
| Dumbbell flyes | 4 | 6, 10, 12 assisted, 15 assisted |
| Hammer incline press | 4 | 6, 6, 15 assisted, 15 assisted |
| Dumbbell pullover | 5 | 6, 8, 12, 15 assisted, 20 assisted |
| Decline bench push-ups | 3 | To failure |

> The reps marked as assisted are completed with a partner's help: 2 or 3 more after failure.

## Shoulders: supersets

Shoulders run as supersets: a press and a raise back to back, with no rest between them. The table shows the pair in consecutive rows.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Seated barbell shoulder press | 5 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| A2. Barbell front raise | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| B1. Dumbbell press | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| B2. Rear raises | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |

Rear raises need a weight that lets you reach 20 without swinging. The press, one that leaves you 6 clean reps.

## Back: high and low reps

Back uses the same ladder and ends with sets of 20 reps. Pull-ups go to failure because bodyweight already sets the difficulty.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable row | 4 | 6, 6, 12, 20 |
| Reverse-grip barbell row | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| Dumbbell pullover | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| Straight-arm pulldowns | 5 | 6, 6, 20, 20 |
| Pull-ups | 3 | To failure |

## Biceps: high and low reps

Biceps combines heavy sets of 6 with light sets of 20. The concentration curl reaches 20 on the last two sets; the Scott curl stays at 15.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seated dumbbell curl | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| Concentration curl | 4 | 6, 6, 20, 20 |
| Reverse barbell curl | 3 | 6, 12, 20 |
| Scott bench curl | 3 | 6, 6, 15 |

## Triceps: less rest

Triceps works with shorter rests and a ladder that ends at 12 or 15. Dips are completed with help, not with a percentage.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-arm reverse-grip pushdown | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 15 |
| Dumbbell kickback | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 15 |
| Overhead triceps extension | 3 | 6, 8, 10, 12 |
| Parallel bar dips | 3 | 12, 10, 8-10 assisted |

The 100 % was a way of saying the set was completed with help, not a rep count. The fix leaves a target of 8 to 10 assisted reps.

## Legs: supersets

Legs are the only block with three pairs. The order runs from press to leg curl: the big movement first, isolation after.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Leg press | 4 | 6, 6, 15, 20 |
| A2. Hack squat | 4 | 6, 6, 20, 20 |
| B1. Smith machine squat | 3 | 6, 6, 15 |
| B2. Smith machine split squat | 3 | 6, 6, 15 |
| C1. Leg extension | 3 | 6, 10, 20 |
| C2. Leg curl | 3 | 6, 10, 20 |

## The techniques, one by one

- **Forced reps.** The partner helps only in the concentric phase and only after failure. If the help starts earlier, the set does not count.
- **Supersets.** Two exercises without rest. Pick muscles that do not compete or one muscle from two angles. The guide to [supersets](/en/supersets/) has the details.
- **High and low reps.** The same session mixes heavy sets of 6 and light sets of 15 to 20. The load changes, the rest does not.
- **Partial reps.** When full range is gone, partials run in the strong part of the movement. It is another way to lengthen the set; the guide to [partial reps](/en/partial-reps/) explains when to use them.

## When not to use them

If you are starting out, these ladders have too many sets and too many techniques. Start with 3 sets of 8 to 12 near failure and add techniques once the base is built. One block per session is enough; two at most if the muscle is small and already in your base plan.

## Where to fit them

Pick one or two blocks per session and add them to your current plan. Do not use them as your only training for weeks: progression and the deficit are still what decide the result. The [cutting routines](/en/cutting-routines/) index has full plans; the [chest](/en/chest-routines/), [triceps](/en/triceps-routines/) and [leg](/en/leg-routines/) blocks expand each group.

## What it does not do

Training a muscle does not burn the fat covering it. The body uses fat as a general reserve, not a local deposit. If a block leaves the muscle pumped, that is blood and fatigue, not fat removed. Definition arrives with the deficit and with time.
