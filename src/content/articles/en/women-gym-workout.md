The original proposed the same split, with six supersets per session and thirty-six sets for the upper body. This version keeps upper and lower, the exercise order and the intensity techniques, and cuts the volume down to what can be executed well in an hour.

## Who it is for

For someone with at least six months of training who knows the basic movements: dumbbell press, pulldown, row, squat and lunges. If you are starting, the [beginner gym guide](/en/beginner-gym-guide/) orders the first block and this routine comes after it.

## The week

Four sessions built from the same pair of workouts. Upper and lower alternate, so each muscle group trains twice a week with at least 48 hours between sessions.

## The warm-up

Five minutes of easy cardio and shoulder and hip mobility. Then one light set of the first exercise in each pair at 50 per cent of the working weight. That is enough to be ready without spending strength before the first set.

## Day 1: upper body in supersets

Four pairs. The first two carry three sets; the last two carry two, because the accumulated work is already high.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Incline dumbbell press | 3 | 10 |
| A2. Cable pulldown | 3 | 10 |
| B1. Incline dumbbell fly | 3 | 12 |
| B2. One-arm dumbbell row | 3 | 12 |
| C1. Lateral raise | 2 | 15 |
| C2. EZ-bar curl | 2 | 12 |
| D1. Dumbbell French press | 2 | 12 |
| D2. Cable crunch | 2 | 15 |

## Day 2: lower body with a triset

One triset for the lower body and two pairs for calves and abs. The Smith machine squat opens the second pair after the leg press, and the stiff-leg deadlift stays in the triset.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Leg press | 3 | 12 |
| A2. Seated leg curl | 3 | 12 |
| A3. Stiff-leg deadlift | 3 | 12 |
| B1. Smith machine squat | 3 | 10 |
| B2. Dumbbell lunges | 3 | 10 |
| C1. Standing calf raise | 3 | 15 |
| C2. Knee raise on parallel bars | 3 | 15 |

## How it runs

In a superset you do one set of the first exercise, then one of the second without rest, then the rest. In a triset the three run back to back. Rest between blocks is 60 to 90 seconds, and two minutes if the goal is to move more weight on the main lift.

Technique does not change because you are moving fast. Reps are slow on the way down, and the weight is chosen so you complete all of them with one or two reps in reserve. Write down the weight and reps of each set, because the progression depends on knowing what you did last week. The [supersets](/en/supersets/) and [tri-sets](/en/tri-sets/) guides cover the variations.

If a machine is taken, swap the exercise for its equivalent, not for one that trains something else. The pulldown becomes assisted pull-ups, the dumbbell row becomes a cable row and the leg press becomes a Smith machine squat. The session holds even if the order changes.

## Load and progression

Six weeks with the same scheme. If you complete every rep with one rep in reserve, add 2.5 per cent to the weight next session. If you fall short, repeat the weight. At the end of week six, take a deload week at half the sets.

Abs appear twice a week, one exercise in each session. They respond to load like everything else.

The [torso legs split](/en/torso-legs-split/) has three, four and six-day variants if you need another frequency, and the [training volume](/en/training-volume/) guide explains why twenty sets per session is already a sensible ceiling.

## Cardio: two or three sessions

The original asked for thirty to forty-five minutes of aerobic work three to six days a week. Six days are not necessary and compete with leg recovery. Two or three sessions of twenty to thirty minutes after weights, or on free days, cover the goal.

The combination has its logic: weights keep muscle mass and cardio adds expenditure. The [cardio and weights for women](/en/cardio-and-weights-for-women/) guide details the order and the intensities.
