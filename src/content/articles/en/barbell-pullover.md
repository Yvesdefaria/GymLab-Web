Each version has its advantages. Dumbbells seem to allow a better stretch and contraction in the chest, while the bar allows it in the lats. That is why this version focuses on activating the back muscles in a more isolated way.

## How to do the barbell pullover step by step

1. Place the bar at the end of the bench where your head will be, unless someone can hand it to you. Otherwise, grab the bar, sit on the bench and get into position.
2. Lie perpendicular or parallel to the bench. If you lie perpendicular, make sure your upper back is supported on the bench.
3. Take the bar with a grip slightly wider than your shoulders and hold it directly over your chest with your arms almost extended.
4. Bend your elbows and take the bar back behind your head until you feel a good stretch in your lats.
5. Drive your elbows forward and pull with your lats until your upper arms are slightly above the position parallel to the floor. Your elbows stay slightly bent throughout the movement, never straight.
6. Repeat as needed.

## Muscles worked by the barbell pullover

The latissimus dorsi is the main mover, although the chest muscles are involved too. The pullover stimulates the upper chest, the clavicular area, and the lower chest, the sternal area.

If you want to review the anatomy before programming it, the [back anatomy](/en/back-anatomy/) guide has the detail.

## How to choose the weight and reps

Weight is not the most important thing here. A weight that lets you do 12 to 15 reps is the right choice for this version.

Keep your elbows bent, because that bend is what allows a really good stretch. Do not raise your elbows past the parallel position in the concentric part of the exercise: if you do, tension in the lats drops.

## Barbell or dumbbell pullover

The barbell version isolates the lats more, and the dumbbell version stays closer to the chest. If your goal is the back, the bar has the edge. If you want the middle and lower chest, use the dumbbell.

You can alternate the two depending on the day. The [dumbbell pullover](/en/dumbbell-pullover/) article has the technique for that version and its intensity adjustments.

## Mistakes and safety precautions

**Straightening your elbows.** If your arms lock out, the movement becomes rigid and the lats lose the stretch. Keep a slight, constant bend.

**Going past parallel on the way up.** When your elbows rise beyond the line parallel to the floor, tension drops. That is where the useful range ends.

**Training heavy without a spotter.** If you use relatively heavy weights, find someone to assist you, especially if you are not an advanced lifter. Performed badly, this can be an injurious exercise.

**Losing control on the way down.** The bar travels behind your head. If you let it drop, the shoulder takes a load it cannot brake.
