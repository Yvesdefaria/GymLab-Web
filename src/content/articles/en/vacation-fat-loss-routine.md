The plan works in a hotel, a neighbourhood gym or a home garage. Loads are light and reps are high, between 15 and 20, because the goal is work and congestion, not maximal strength. The deficit does the rest: without eating less, the scale does not move. The [cutting routines guide](/en/cutting-routines/) explains how to set that up without losing strength.

The split covers the whole body in three days: chest and back first, legs and shoulders second, shoulders and arms third. All three use the same rule: opposing-muscle pairs or triseries for the same group, sets of 15 reps and short rests.

## How the three sessions work

Each day has three blocks. Exercises in a block run back to back, with no rest between them, and you rest after the block before repeating it. The first two blocks are opposing-muscle supersets; the third can be a triset when the day calls for it.

Do 3 to 4 sets per exercise. If you have been training for less than a year, start with 2 or 3 sets of 15 and no drop sets. After eight to twelve weeks, move to the full scheme. Spread the three sessions on alternate days; the exact sequence does not matter.

## Day 1: chest, back and legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell bench press | 3-4 | 15 |
| Cable pulldowns | 3-4 | 15 |
| Dumbbell squat | 3-4 | 15-20 |
| Lunges | 3-4 | 15 per leg |
| Incline bench flyes | 3-4 | 15 |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3-4 | 15 |

The chest and back pair opens the session; legs go in the middle, while you still have energy; flyes and rows close with the muscle warm. Row with your torso supported on the bench so the lower back does not take the load.

## Day 2: legs, shoulders and arms

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | 3-4 | 15 |
| Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 3-4 | 15 |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3-4 | 15 per leg |
| Dumbbell lateral raise | 3-4 | 15 |
| Standing barbell curl | 3-4 | 15 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 3-4 | 15 |

The Romanian deadlift and the Bulgarian split squat cover the posterior chain and the quads. The shoulder press and lateral raises work the shoulder from two angles, and the curl and pushdown close the arm.

## Day 3: shoulders and arms

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell lateral raise | 3-4 | 15 |
| Seated dumbbell press | 3-4 | 15 |
| Barbell upright row | 3-4 | 15 + drop |
| Standing barbell curl | 3-4 | 15 |
| Cable triceps pushdown | 3-4 | 15 + drop |
| Parallel bar dips | 3-4 | 15 |
| Seated dumbbell curl | 3-4 | 15 + drop |

> The last set of those exercises is a drop set: at failure, remove 40 % to 50 % of the weight and do 6 to 8 more reps.

The triset in the first block is the advanced part of the plan: lateral raises, press and upright row back to back, with no rest between exercises. The three exercises marked with an asterisk carry the drop on the last set.

## Rest

One ladder: 15 seconds between exercises in a block, 1 to 2 minutes after the block before repeating it, and 30 seconds between sets of a standalone exercise. If the clock forces you to lower the weight, lower the weight; form is not negotiable. The [rest between sets guide](/en/rest-between-sets/) explains why the clock matters more than the load in this kind of session.

## Progression

Start with a weight that leaves 15 clean reps. Each week, add one rep per set until you reach 20. When all sets hit 20, move to the next dumbbell or cable increment and go back to 15. That is the only load change in the plan.

## Abs

At the end of each session, pick two or three of these exercises and do one or two sets of 15 to 30 slow reps. Change the combination each time so the stimulus does not stall.

- Reverse crunch on a bench.
- Floor crunch.
- Knees to chest on a Roman chair.
- Knee raises.
- Oblique crunch.
- Cable crunch.

Ab work does not burn the fat on top of it: fat comes off with the deficit. It is here to support the midsection during squats and deadlifts.

## Cardio and travel

If you already do cardio, keep what you were doing. If not, start with three days of 20 easy minutes on a treadmill or on foot. The [aerobic exercise guide](/en/aerobic-exercise/) covers the intensities.

Before training, walk or cycle for 5 to 10 minutes and do one light set of the first exercise in each block. Lower the weight slowly, without bouncing or momentum. If form breaks, the load is too high.

The plan lasts as long as the holiday. When you get back, the [leg routines guide](/en/leg-routines/) and the [supersets guide](/en/supersets/) help you reorganise volume. The drop notation is explained in the [drop sets guide](/en/drop-sets/).
