Basketball is decided in short bursts: the first step of a fast break, the jump for a rebound, the escape from a screen. Leg strength drives those moves, and arm strength keeps the shot steady when fatigue arrives.

## Why basketball needs weights

Technique is learned on the court. Once the movement runs without thinking, strength makes the difference: a player who jumps higher and starts faster wins an edge on every play. Weight work also protects the joints that suffer most when braking and turning, the knees and the ankles.

The plan combines hypertrophy and muscular endurance for a body that lasts forty minutes and keeps its explosiveness in the fourth quarter, when the game breaks open.

## The week in four sessions

Spread the four sessions across Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday. Each pattern gets two stimuli separated by 48 hours, and the weekend belongs to the game or to rest. If the calendar gets tight, keep both leg sessions and cut upper body to one.

The first session of the week comes after Sunday's rest. On Friday, with the game close, chase control rather than records.

## The eight-week progression

The plan adds sets and cuts reps until week five, then walks the same path back. The first two weeks are for learning the movement with a weight that leaves two reps in reserve; from week three rest reaches 60 seconds and in week five the work gets serious.

All twelve exercises share the week's prescription. The last set of each should cost, without reaching failure on the squat or the bench press.

## Upper body

The order runs from the biggest movement to the smallest: the lifts that drain the most energy come first and the abs close the session.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat chest press | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Seated row | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| One-arm triceps extension | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Standing barbell curl | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Front shoulder press | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Horizontal crunches | 3-5 | 10-15 |

Use a bar or a machine for the chest press and the row, whichever the gym has. The triceps extension and the curl close the session when fatigue has already arrived, so they take a moderate weight.

## Lower body

The squat opens the session and the calf raise closes it. Do the lunges with dumbbells or a bar, with a step long enough to descend without losing balance.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Lunges | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Leg extensions | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Leg curl | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Seated calf raise | 3-5 | 10-15 |
| Leg raises | 3-5 | 10-15 |

The seated calf raise works with a bent knee and the leg curl with an extended hip; those are the two points young players neglect most. The leg raise closes the abs without loading the spine.

## The five-meal diet

The menu is built for a player between 75 and 80 kg who trains about two and a half hours a day. It has five meals, with protein spread across all of them and carbohydrates through the day.

**Breakfast.** A glass of milk, two scrambled eggs with a slice of turkey ham, three slices of bread and a cup of chopped fruit.

**Snack one.** A high-carb protein shake in 250 ml of milk and a piece of fruit.

**Lunch.** One and a half cups of cooked rice or pasta, 150 grams of chicken breast, fish or grilled beef with a green salad, one medium boiled or mashed potato, a tortilla or a slice of bread and one medium fruit. Optional: a cup of beans.

**Snack two.** A turkey ham and panela cheese sandwich and a drinking yoghurt.

**Dinner.** A low-carb protein shake in 250 ml of light milk and two quesadillas with Oaxaca cheese.

Protein is the number that matters most: between 1.6 and 2.2 grams per kilo of body weight, which is 120 to 176 grams a day for that player. The five meals cover that range and the whole day lands near 3,100 calories, this page's calculation with standard portions for the stated weight.

If one day you miss one of the five meals, do not make up for it with a bigger portion at the next one. Keep the spread and adjust dinner, the most flexible meal of the day.

## How it progresses

Add 5 kilos on the squat and 2.5 on the upper-body lifts when the last set comes up clean, with every rep and no loss of technique. If you miss two sessions in a row on the same exercise, drop 10 % and climb again. After the eight weeks, take a deload week at half the weight and start another block with 2.5 kilos more on the main lifts.
