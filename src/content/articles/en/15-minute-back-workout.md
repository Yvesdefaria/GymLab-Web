Each arm rests while the other works, but your body does not. The pace stays high and the lats accumulate work without long pauses. Pick a weight that lets you do 12 reps on the first set.

The short rests force reps down on later sets. Depending on your condition, you will finish with 6 to 8 reps on the last set.

## How the 15-minute back workout works

The order makes sense. You start with one-arm dumbbell rows to build back mass and density. Then you move to one-arm pulldowns for width.

The third exercise is the one-arm seated cable row, which adds density and size to the lower lats. At the end you isolate the lats with one-arm straight-arm pulldowns using a rope on the pulley.

## The workout: four one-arm exercises

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 | 12 |
| One-arm cable pulldown | 3 | 12 |
| One-arm machine row | 3 | 12 |
| One-arm straight-arm cable pulldown | 3 | 12 |

> **\*** The number is the reps you can complete on the first set. On every following set, do as many reps as possible until you reach muscular failure.

Rest only as long as it takes to move between stations, always under a minute.

## How to sit and set up each station

For the one-arm pulldowns, sit sideways on the pulldown bench: your right side facing the pulley when you work that arm, and your left side facing the pulley when you work the left arm. You do not need to put your legs under the rollers, because you use much less than your body weight.

For the rows with your right arm, sit with only your right leg and glute on the bench, and your right foot on the left side of the platform. Your left leg stays bent with the foot flat on the floor. Do the same with your left arm.

## Grip: why straps help

Moving from one arm to the other without rest fatigues your grip fast. If the forearm muscles give out before your back, the lats stop getting the stimulus you came for.

Straps let you keep the set going when the forearm starts to fail. They do not replace technique, but they stop the grip from setting the limit of the session.

## Who it is for and what to expect

This is an advanced routine. Only someone with gym experience will hold the pace and reach failure under the intended conditions.

If you are just starting, the unilateral format without rest will drain your grip and your back before you finish the four stations. In that case, start with a [beginner back routine](/en/back-routines/) and come back to this one when you have a base.

The one-arm dumbbell row and the one-arm cable row appear in the [dumbbell back exercises](/en/dumbbell-back-exercises/) guide with more execution detail.
