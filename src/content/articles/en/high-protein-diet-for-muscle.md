The menu below uses familiar foods: egg whites, lean meat, salmon, whey and casein. What changes against a normal plan is the split: six moments with a different source each time, built to cover the day's total. Timing matters less than that total, and the total is what this menu puts in plain sight.

The total is 2,918 calories and 286 g of protein. The original text rounds to 2,900 and 280, and the gap sits in the pre-workout meal, which allows two portion sizes.

Four of the six moments are whole food, and that is the part worth copying. The two powders cover the pre-workout and the last meal, when cooking is the last thing anyone wants. Replacing them with food costs nothing, and a yoghurt with fruit can stand in for the post-workout shake on a busy day.

## 286 grams is 3.5 g/kg

At 80 kilos, 286 g of protein is 3.5 g per kilo. The range the evidence supports sits between 1.6 and 2.2 g/kg; above 2.4 no extra muscle appears. The gap between 176 and 286 g is 110 g of protein, about 440 calories that could come from pasta, fruit and oil, and a few euros a week.

That is the cost of the high figure. A healthy adult's kidney copes; what gets lost is room on the plate for fibre and variety.

## Fat comes up short

The day leaves fat at 38 g, about 340 calories, 12 percent of the total. That is low even for a cutting menu. Fat should sit between 0.8 and 1 g/kg, which is 64 to 80 g at 80 kilos. Olive oil, avocado or nuts close the gap without touching protein.

## Order matters less than the total

Protein near training helps spread the day, and the total decides. The post-workout window is wider than it was once made out to be: a shake within the next hour works, and a full meal two hours later works too. The guides to the [pre-workout meal](/en/pre-workout-meal/) and the [post-workout meal](/en/post-workout-meal/) spell out what to put in each.

## The meal before bed

65 g of casein in one sitting is more than most people need; 40 g covers the night. Casein digests slowly, which suits the last meal when the day's total runs short. The guide to the [best night-time protein](/en/casein-protein/) compares the options.

## How to bring it down to your weight

For 80 kilos and 2 g/kg: 160 g of protein and 2,900 calories, with 60 to 80 g of fat and the rest from carbohydrate, about 400 g. Drop one spoonful of casein, swap the beef for chicken and add rice or fruit up to the total.

The [Nick Olsen menu](/en/nick-olsen-routine-diet/) is another bulking day written as a sample, with no body weight or calorie total. The [clean bulk diet](/en/clean-bulk-diet/) reaches 3,136 calories at 2.8 g/kg of protein with more carbohydrate.
