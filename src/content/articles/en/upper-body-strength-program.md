Specialising in the upper body is a strategy for breaking plateaus, not a replacement for a base. It reduces the fatigue that comes from the legs and allows more work on chest, back, shoulders and arms. In exchange, it leaves the lower body almost without stimulus for three months.

## Goal and requirements

The programme picks one main lift per muscle group: bench press for the chest, barbell row for the back, overhead press for the shoulders, barbell curl for the biceps and close-grip bench press for the triceps. These are the exercises that let you move the most weight in each pattern.

It is not a plan for beginners: you need settled technique and a 1RM reference. If you come from a full-body routine, finish a linear base first; the [strength routines](/en/strength-routines/) directory has options. And twelve weeks of upper body only cost leg strength if there is no stimulus: add one short leg day a week or accept the loss. [Central nervous system fatigue](/en/central-nervous-system-fatigue/) explains why heavy leg work shapes the rest of the week.

## The three phases

Sessions 1, 2 and 3 run on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Session 4 is the Friday speed day.

| Weeks | Load | Sets x reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-4 | 80 % | 4 x 6-8 |
| 5-8 | 85-90 % | 4 x 4-5 |
| 9-12 | 95 % | 4 x 2-3 |

The speed day stays at 50-60 percent for the whole programme: four sets of three reps and 2 minutes of rest. Before that, one warm-up set with the empty bar for 10 reps. At the end of the twelve weeks you can do one extra set at 60 percent to failure on each exercise; it is a test, not a habit.

## How to estimate 1RM without a true max

The test looks for a safe estimate, not a true one-rep max. It uses two or three warm-up sets, a load of 8-10RM and a single set to failure. Rest 5 minutes between exercises.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 1 | 8-10 |
| Barbell row | 1 | 8-10 |
| Overhead press | 1 | 8-10 |
| Barbell curl | 1 | 8-10 |
| Close-grip bench press | 1 | 8-10 |

Example: you estimate 84 kilos for 10 reps on the bench press, reach failure on rep nine and the formula returns about 108 kilos. The guide to [one-rep max and volume routines](/en/one-rep-max-volume-training/) explains the formulas and their limits. Test at the start, at the end of week 8 (it replaces the speed session) and in week 12. The first and last tests call for two or three rest days beforehand.

## Session 1: chest

On the five main lifts, the warm-up is 50 % x 10, 60 % x 6 and 70 % x 4. The final set at 60 percent to failure comes after the work sets.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 4 | 6-8 / 4-5 / 2-3 |
| Bench press, 60 % final | 1 | To failure |
| Dumbbell press, flat or incline | 2 | 5-6 + 3x2-3 |
| Dumbbell fly, incline or flat | 2 | 8-10 |

The dumbbell press uses a flat or incline bench, alternating the position. After each set of 5-6 reps, three clusters of 2-3 with a short pause. The fly follows the same bench alternation.

## Session 2: shoulders, back and traps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell overhead press | 4 | 6-8 / 4-5 / 2-3 |
| Overhead press, 60 % final | 1 | To failure |
| Dumbbell overhead press | 2 | 5-6 + 3x2-3 |
| Lateral raise | 2 | 8-10 |
| Bent-over barbell row | 4 | 6-8 / 4-5 / 2-3 |
| Barbell row, 60 % final | 1 | To failure |
| Seated cable row or one-arm dumbbell row | 2 | 5-6 + 3x2-3 |
| Lat pulldown | 2 | 8-10 |
| Barbell shrug | 3 | 6-8 |

The one-arm dumbbell row replaces the cable row when no cable is available. The shrug uses a barbell.

## Session 3: triceps, biceps and forearms

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Close-grip bench press | 4 | 6-8 / 4-5 / 2-3 |
| Close-grip bench press, 60 % final | 1 | To failure |
| Lying barbell extension or overhead dumbbell extension | 2 | 5-6 + 3x2-3 |
| Cable pushdown or reverse-grip pushdown | 2 | 8-10 |
| Barbell curl | 4 | 6-8 / 4-5 / 2-3 |
| Barbell curl, 60 % final | 1 | To failure |
| Alternating or incline dumbbell curl | 2 | 5-6 + 3x2-3 |
| Hammer curl | 2 | 8-10 |
| Wrist curl | 2 | 8-10 |
| Reverse wrist curl | 2 | 8-10 |

The lying extension and the overhead dumbbell extension are interchangeable. The cable pushdown alternates a wide grip in supination and pronation every two weeks.

## Session 4: speed reps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbell bench press | 4 | 3 |
| Bent-over barbell row | 4 | 3 |
| Barbell overhead press | 4 | 3 |
| Barbell curl | 4 | 3 |
| Close-grip bench press | 4 | 3 |

The whole day moves at 50-60 percent of 1RM. The concentric phase is driven as fast as possible; if the bar slows down, the set ends. Two minutes of rest.

## Assistance work and the week

Assistance work comes after the main lifts. On the dumbbell presses, two sets of 5-6 and, inside each one, three clusters of 2-3 with a pause: the [rest-pause](/en/rest-pause/) technique. The session ends with a single-joint exercise for the target muscle, except on back day, where the lat pulldown is already a compound movement.

Four sessions leave little room to add. If you need to cut, remove accessories before main sets. For the bench press, the guide to [building bench press strength](/en/bench-press-strength/) covers the points that limit the lift; for the shoulders, the comparison between the [overhead press and the behind-neck press](/en/military-press-vs-behind-neck-press/) helps choose the variant. Leave 48 hours between heavy sessions for the same pattern.
