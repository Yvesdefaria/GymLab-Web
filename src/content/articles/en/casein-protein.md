Milk has two protein families: casein, around 80 %, and whey, the rest. In the stomach, casein forms a clot and releases amino acids for hours; whey passes sooner and its peak is shorter. That speed difference is real and can be measured in blood.

## What casein is and why it is slow

Casein from a tub is processed milk protein. It can come as caseinate, precipitated with acid, or as micellar casein, filtered cold. Both are slow; micellar keeps the original structure better and costs more. Neither stops being milk.

## Protein before sleep: what the evidence shows

Thirty or forty grams of protein before bed raises protein synthesis overnight in adults who train. The effect on muscle mass is small and adds to the daily total; it does not turn a short diet into a sufficient one. If you already eat 40 grams of protein at dinner, an extra shake adds nothing new.

Cottage cheese, Greek yogurt and quark do the same job. About 300 grams of cottage cheese supplies around 30 grams of protein, with the advantage of whole food and the drawback of lactose. Casein powder is a concentrated, low-lactose version of that same protein.

## Lactose, allergy and the error of mixing them up

Lactose intolerance is a problem digesting the sugar in milk. Milk protein allergy is an immune reaction to casein or whey. They are different things, and casein does not solve the second: a casein supplement is milk protein and is not suitable for an allergic person.

Casein powder usually carries less than 1 gram of lactose per serving, so many people with intolerance tolerate it. That does not make it a milk-free product. Anyone with an allergy needs a plant or egg protein, and the page on [vegan protein](/en/vegan-protein/) covers that option.

## The insulin myth

For years the advice was that casein absorbs better with carbohydrates because insulin pushes amino acids into muscle. Insulin is a permissive hormone: it allows synthesis to happen, but it does not raise it on its own in healthy adults. Adding sugar to casein does not improve muscle building.

Carbohydrates are still useful for refilling glycogen and for performance in the next session. That is a different job. Casein works with water, with milk or with a meal, and the daily protein total matters more than the exact combination.

## Casein, whey and timing

Whey is fast and casein is slow, but timing matters less than it is made to sound. The thirty-minute window is not an emergency; the daily total and its split into 25 to 40 gram servings decide. Casein fits a meal far from training or before bed for satiety, not for a secret advantage.

If you train at night and eat dinner afterwards, dinner covers the nighttime serving. If you train early and eat breakfast soon, the night shake only makes sense if the day fell short. The guide to [whey protein](/en/whey-protein-production/) explains the other end of the scale.

## When casein is unnecessary

It is unnecessary if you reach 1.6 to 2.2 grams per kilo with food. It is unnecessary if the budget is tight and cottage cheese costs less. It is unnecessary if you have a milk allergy. And it does not fix sleep: no shake makes up for five hours, and the guide on [how much sleep you need](/en/sleep-for-muscle-growth/) explains why.

Casein is a slow, convenient tool, not an obligation.
