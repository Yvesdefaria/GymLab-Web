Work and family do not negotiate. The alternative to skipping the session is a short, well-ordered one: less rest, paired exercises and a clock that runs the show. These sessions train the upper body, so leg days stay free for the long work.

## The week

An order with three lifting slots is enough. Monday carries the full template, Wednesday the chest and back pair, and Friday can be any of the other four sessions depending on the time you have.

## The 30-minute session

Three exercises per muscle group and a single set of each. The order is built so the group that just worked rests while the next one goes, so rest between exercises is only the walk to the next machine.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chest: incline barbell press | 1 | 8 |
| Back: bent-over barbell row | 1 | 8 |
| Shoulders: overhead dumbbell press | 1 | 8 |
| Triceps: lying barbell extension | 1 | 8 |
| Biceps: alternating dumbbell curl | 1 | 8 |
| Chest: flat-bench dumbbell press | 1 | 10 |
| Back: pull-ups | 1 | 10 |
| Shoulders: cable front raise | 1 | 10 |
| Triceps: overhead cable extension | 1 | 10 |
| Biceps: cable curl | 1 | 10 |
| Chest: dumbbell flye | 1 | 15 |
| Back: lat pulldown | 1 | 15 |
| Shoulders: dumbbell lateral raise | 1 | 15 |
| Triceps: reverse-grip triceps pushdown | 1 | 15 |
| Biceps: high-cable curl | 1 | 15 |

That is fifteen sets: three blocks of five exercises at 8, 10 and 15 reps. If the load lets you finish the block with two reps in reserve, add weight next week.

## The 20-minute session

Three chest and back [supersets](/en/supersets/), each repeated three times, with one minute of rest between rounds. Here the rest goes between complete supersets, not between the two exercises.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Close-grip pull-ups | 3 | to failure |
| A2. Parallel bar dips | 3 | 8-10 |
| B1. Incline dumbbell press | 3 | 10-12 |
| B2. Seated cable row | 3 | 10-12 |
| C1. Lat pulldown | 3 | 12 |
| C2. Machine press | 3 | 12 |

## Five-minute blocks

Here the clock really runs the show. Pick a load that brings you to failure on the tenth rep, do 10 reps and rest 30 to 60 seconds. Repeat as many rounds as you can until the five minutes are up, then move to the next exercise with 1 or 2 minutes of rest between blocks.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smith machine bench press | 1 block | 5 min |
| Pull-ups or seated cable row | 1 block | 5 min |
| Smith machine overhead press | 1 block | 5 min |

## Chest and triceps, back and biceps

The two pair sessions are not true supersets: you do a set of one exercise, rest a minute and do a set of the other, until three of each are done. The time trick is to keep both exercises in the same spot so there is no walking between them.

**Chest and triceps.** Smith machine bench press 3 x 15-20 and Smith machine close-grip bench press 3 x 8-10, with the same load on both. Second pair: incline dumbbell press 3 x 10-12 and overhead barbell extension 3 x 10-12, with both arms at once.

**Back and biceps.** Dumbbell row with a pronated grip 3 x 10-12 and dumbbell curl 3 x 10-12. Second pair: dumbbell row with a supinated grip 3 x 12-15 and incline dumbbell curl 3 x 12-15. Both arms at once, not alternating.

The rest is one minute between sets, not between reps.

## Arms in 15 minutes

Three biceps and triceps supersets, 2 to 3 rounds each, with one minute between rounds. Every pair uses the same equipment, so the whole thing runs without moving.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1. Barbell curl | 2-3 | 6-8 |
| A2. Lying barbell extension | 2-3 | 6-8 |
| B1. Overhead dumbbell extension | 2-3 | 8-10 |
| B2. Dumbbell curl | 2-3 | 8-10 |
| C1. Cable curl | 2-3 | 12-15 |
| C2. Cable triceps pushdown | 2-3 | 12-15 |

The three pairs go from heavy to light: 6 to 8 reps first, then 8 to 10, then 12 to 15. That order lets you load the first superset without paying for it in the last one, which is what a 15-minute session needs.

## Density progression

The load stays put and the clock comes down. Week 1: one minute between supersets in the 20-minute session and between rounds in the five-minute one. Week 2: 45 seconds. Week 3: 30 seconds, the floor. In the 30-minute template the rest between exercises is already just the walk, so there progress means adding a little weight when all fifteen sets come out clean.

When you hold 30 seconds with clean technique, add a round to the superset or go back to week 1 with more load. The [rest between sets](/en/rest-between-sets/) guide explains why that margin changes the session so much.

That is why the short session works: total work stays and the waiting is what gets cut. Respect the exercise order and the muscle arrives fresh for the next set even when the clock has barely moved.
