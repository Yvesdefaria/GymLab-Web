Almost every biceps exercise is a variant of this one. The barbell curl fixes the shoulder, leaves the elbow as the only moving joint and turns the set into a measure of strength. Once the technique makes sense here, the rest of the curls fall into place.

## Why it opens the session

The barbell curl is a single-joint exercise: the elbow flexes and the shoulder does not follow the movement. That simplicity is what allows load. With both hands on the same bar, it is the heaviest bilateral elbow flexion, and the easiest to measure: the bar and plates progress in 2.5 kilo steps.

That is the real argument for putting it first, while the body is fresh. The shoulder stays still, the biceps takes the load and the rest of the arm session arrives later with less weight and more detail.

## The position

Feet at hip width, knees slightly bent, torso upright. The bar is gripped at shoulder width with the palms up and the wrists in line with the forearm. The elbows stay at the sides, not in front.

The [wrist position in the biceps curl](/en/wrist-position-biceps-curl/) deserves its own review: a wrist bent backwards lengthens the lever and moves the bar away from the elbow, and a flexed wrist ends the set when the forearm fails. Neutral through the whole range is the option that moves more weight without punishing the joint.

## The range

At the bottom the arms extend fully. The biceps reaches its long position and the elbow straightens without locking backwards. At the top the bar rises to chest height and the elbow flexes fully without the shoulder drifting forward.

The descent is half the exercise. If the bar falls without resistance, the work stays on the way up. Two or three seconds of controlled lowering turn the same set into a different stimulus.

## Grip width

Grip width does not choose which biceps head works. What decides which head arrives stretched is the shoulder angle: with the arm behind the body the long head stretches, with the arm in front the short head works. The bar does not move the shoulder.

What width does change is comfort and leverage. A grip slightly wider than the shoulders keeps the elbow comfortable and lets the palm turn without the wrist opening. A very narrow grip crowds the hands, presses the elbow into the torso and adds no stimulus. The guide to [biceps exercises](/en/biceps-exercises/) develops the shoulder rule and orders the variations by position.

## How much weight and how many sets

The dose this page proposes is three sets of 8 to 12 reps with 90 seconds of rest. It is a recommendation from this article, not a number that comes with the bar.

Add weight when all three sets reach 12 reps without swinging and with a controlled descent. Plates of 1.25 kilos allow small steps and keep the progression going for months. If the torso starts helping the bar up, the load has passed the biceps limit.

The straight bar is the reference; the other grips change the share and the weak point.

The other two members of the family have their own pages: the [spider curl](/en/spider-curl/) works with the shoulder flexed and the arm hanging, and the [EZ bar preacher curl](/en/preacher-curl-ez-bar/) rests the elbow on a pad. Both remove momentum from the torso, which is exactly what the straight bar allows when the load gets heavy.

## Mistakes that steal tension

- **Swinging the torso.** Momentum moves the rep and spreads the work into the lower back.
- **Letting the elbows drift forward.** Once the elbow leaves the side, the front delt starts pushing.
- **Cutting the extension.** Without lowering to a straight arm, the long head never reaches its long position.
- **Bending the wrist at the end.** The set ends when the forearm fails, not the muscle you meant to train.

## Where it fits

The barbell curl opens biceps work and the rest of the session lines up behind it. The [biceps routines](/en/biceps-routines/) place this exercise in ten plans with their volume, and the [map of the biceps and forearm](/en/biceps-forearm-anatomy/) explains which muscle answers each grip.

If the goal is the long head, the shoulder has to sit behind the body, and that position is covered by the incline curl in the [biceps exercises](/en/biceps-exercises/) guide. The [rope on a cable](/en/rope-hammer-curl/) and the [EZ bar curl](/en/ez-bar-curl/) complete the week without repeating the same stimulus.
