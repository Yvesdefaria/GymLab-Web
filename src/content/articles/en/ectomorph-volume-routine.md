Ectomorph describes a starting point: a narrow bone structure and a tendency to stay thin. It explains why gaining weight feels hard. Anyone who gains mass with difficulty needs the same sets and the same progression as everyone else, with two adjustments: more food and more sleep.

## The label describes; it does not prescribe

The three somatotypes describe a constitution. The [somatotypes](/en/somatotypes/) guide explains where they come from and what their limits are. Nobody is one hundred per cent ectomorph, and the label does not change muscle physiology.

What does change the result is the energy balance. If you train three days, eat below what you spend and sleep six hours, no routine turns that into muscle mass. The routine orders the stimulus; food and rest supply the material.

Each session mixes one strength exercise with several hypertrophy ones. The strength lifts open the session with a fresh nervous system; the hypertrophy work closes it with more reps and less load.

## Monday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Squat | 5 | 5 |
| Incline dumbbell or barbell press | 2 | 10 |
| Lat pulldown | 2 | 12 |
| Military press | 2 | 12 |

The squat allows three formats: 5 x 5, 6 x 4 or 4 x 4. Pick one and keep it for six weeks. The incline press can go at 3 x 8 if the load allows, without passing 25 total reps. The pulldown and the military press accept 2 x 10 instead of 2 x 12. Abs carry no number in the original: pick one exercise and one figure, and repeat them for six weeks.

## Wednesday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press | 5 | 5 |
| Leg press | 2 | 15 |
| Leg curl | 2 | 12 |
| Biceps curl + triceps extension | 2 + 2 | 10 + 10 |

The bench press is the strength lift of the day and allows 6 x 4 instead of 5 x 5. The leg press goes at 2 x 12 if you prefer fewer reps, without exceeding 30 in total. Biceps and triceps run as a superset: two sets each, no rest between them. Back extensions carry no number in the original either: two or three sets and a figure you can repeat.

## Friday

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deadlift | 5 | 5 |
| Parallel bar dip | 2 | 12 |
| Upright row | 2 | 12 |
| Standing calf raise + seated calf raise | 2 + 2 | 12-15 + 10 |

The deadlift allows 4 x 6 if your back asks for fewer sets. The upright row can go at 3 x 8. Calves split between the standing and the seated machine, in the rep range the table shows. Dips close the chest week with a bodyweight exercise. Friday abs follow the same rule as Monday: your own figure, repeated.

## The four-week ramp

There is no need to test a five-rep max. Pick a load you can lift for five clean reps leaving one or two in reserve; that load is your reference and equals 100 %. The percentages come from it.

The reference is measured on the squat, the bench press and the deadlift. Hypertrophy exercises do not use the percentage: the load stays in the rep range and only moves when technique allows.

## Weeks 5 and 6

Week five changes the format: three sets of three reps with a 2.5 to 5 kg increase over the reference. It is the only big jump in the cycle and checks that the ramp worked.

Week six is rest. If you cannot stop, train the basics at 60 % of the reference and keep the load on the hypertrophy exercises. It is an assimilation week.

## The two-rep rule

On hypertrophy exercises the set ends when you could still do two more reps. That margin lets you repeat the load next session and accumulate volume without breaking technique. If the movement slows or loses range, the set no longer counts.

Load only goes up when that margin holds with the same fluidity, week after week. It is the [progressive overload](/en/progressive-overload-principle/) logic applied to a percentage plan: first you earn the right to add weight, then you add it.

## What moves the needle

Three training days sustain the stimulus; bodyweight comes from eating more than you spend and sleeping enough. A small surplus, enough protein and a weekly weigh-in are the part of the plan that no rep scheme replaces. The [build muscle](/en/build-muscle/) guide develops that side.

For leg exercise choices, the [leg routines](/en/leg-routines/) guide expands the Monday and Wednesday blocks. If the cycle feels short, the [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) index has plans with more days and more sets.
