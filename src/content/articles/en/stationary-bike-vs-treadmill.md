Both options have advantages, and combining them prevents injuries from repeating the same movement pattern. Your current physical situation also counts: if you cannot do impact sport, the bike and other no impact options move to the front.

## Stationary bike vs treadmill: the benefits of pedalling

If you want training that works and is kind to your joints, the stationary bike is an excellent option. When you buy one, pick a model with a good flywheel: in the end you are investing in your health.

### Advantages of the stationary bike

- **Less impact on the joints.** With no direct contact with the floor, knees and ankles suffer less than on the treadmill.
- **More focus on muscular endurance.** Pedalling mainly strengthens quads, hamstrings and glutes, and also works the core and lower back.
- **Ideal for long workouts.** You can hold a steady pace for longer without the fatigue of the treadmill.
- **Safer for beginners.** It needs no balance or advanced technique.

### Disadvantages of the stationary bike

- Lower calorie burn per minute than the treadmill.
- Less positive impact on bone density, because the body does not carry its own weight as it does when running.

In short: it is the natural choice with joint problems, when you want leg muscular endurance, or when you prefer a less demanding cardiovascular effort.

## Advantages of the treadmill

If your goal is to burn fat fast or improve your aerobic capacity, the treadmill can be your best ally. Years ago having one at home was prohibitive; the pandemic pushed demand up and today there are options for every budget.

### Benefits of running on a treadmill

- **Higher calorie burn.** Running spends more energy than pedalling, especially at moderate or high speeds.
- **Cardiovascular improvement.** It raises endurance and improves lung capacity faster than the bike.
- **Stronger bones and muscles.** When you run, healthy bones receive impacts that help strengthen them and prevent osteoporosis.
- **Versatility.** It works for a light walk and for high intensity sprints.

### Disadvantages of the treadmill

- Greater impact on knees, ankles and hips, so it is less advisable if you have problems in those areas.
- Higher injury risk with prolonged use: repetitive impact ends up as overload.

In short: excellent for fast calorie burn and cardiovascular endurance, as long as you look after the impact.

## Calorie comparison: which one burns more

Approximate calories in 30 minutes of exercise:

- Stationary bike at a moderate pace: 210 to 300 kcal.
- Stationary bike at an intense pace: 400 to 600 kcal.
- Running on a treadmill at a moderate pace, around 8 km/h: 280 to 400 kcal.
- Running on a treadmill fast, around 12 km/h: 500 to 700 kcal.

At high intensity, the treadmill burns more calories. The bike remains an excellent option if you raise resistance and intensity.

## Joint impact and injury prevention

Impact on knees, ankles and hips is the factor that weighs most when choosing between the two machines.

- **Stationary bike:** zero impact, perfect with joint injuries or when you want to avoid overload.
- **Treadmill:** more impact, which you can reduce with proper footwear and good running technique.

With a history of injuries or joint problems, the bike is the safer option.

Excess weight also counts. Walking on a treadmill is still excellent exercise, but running with excess weight means double impact on the joints. If impact takes its toll, the [swimming workout routine](/en/swimming-workout-routine/) offers an alternative with no load on the joints.

## Combining bike and treadmill: the best strategy

Using both machines in the same plan is how you keep the best of each one:

- **You avoid injuries.** Alternating reduces overload on muscles and joints.
- **You maximise calorie burn.** Combined workouts boost fat loss.
- **You balance strength and endurance.** The bike strengthens the legs and the treadmill improves aerobic capacity.

## A combined routine example

Two simple ways to split the session:

- 20 minutes on the bike at medium resistance and 20 minutes of light running on the treadmill.
- 15 minutes of intense bike and 10 minutes of treadmill sprints.

Alternating the two machines improves performance without falling into overload or injury. If intervals click with you, the [HIIT training guide](/en/hiit-training-guide/) explains how to program them with sense.
