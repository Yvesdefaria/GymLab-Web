Choosing an exercise because it is "for women" tends to lead to light loads and a stimulus that does not land. The same movements serve strength, muscle mass and learning, with different sets and reps.

## Exercises for women: why there is no separate list

A squat works the same muscles in any body. Differences in starting strength, training history and tolerance to volume explain almost everything else. Hip biomechanics change the width of your stance and the depth that feels comfortable, not the muscle that contracts.

## If you want strength

Prioritise the basics: squat, deadlift, bench press or overhead press, row and pull-ups or pulldown. Three to six reps, two or three minutes of rest and a load that leaves two reps in reserve. Technique leads; the weight goes up when the rep comes out just as clean.

The basics allow more load and progress for longer. [Women and weights](/en/women-and-weights/) reviews the concepts behind that choice.

## If you want muscle mass

Eight to twelve reps, one or two in reserve, two minutes of rest and 10 to 20 sets per muscle per week, spread over two sessions. The basics stay at the centre; machines and isolation fill the gaps.

The guide to [women's fitness training](/en/womens-fitness-training/) develops the weekly split, and the [routines for women](/en/women-weight-routines/) work as a template once you have a base.

## If you are starting: the six-movement sequence

An order that covers the main patterns and lets you repeat the session without overthinking. Two or three times a week, with a rest day between sessions. Before the first set, five minutes on a bike or rower and one light set of the first exercise at half the weight.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dumbbell squat | 3 | 10-12 |
| Leg press | 3 | 12 |
| Incline dumbbell press | 3 | 8-12 |
| One-arm dumbbell row | 3 | 10 per side |
| Lat pulldown | 3 | 10 |
| Crunches | 3 | 15 |

The squat and the leg press cover the leg push. The incline press and the row balance upper-body push and pull. The pulldown adds vertical work and the crunches close the session with abs.

If an exercise does not run through a clean range, change the variation before the weight: lighter dumbbells, a shorter range or an assisted machine. Repeat the sequence until the movement feels boring, then add load.

## The load is chosen by reps in reserve

Three or four reps in reserve to learn, one or two once the movement is solid. If you cannot reach the minimum with good technique, lower the weight; if you pass the top on every set, add 5 percent. The load is not chosen by what you think you should lift.

## Mistakes that matter

Duplicating squats also costs. Squat, leg press and leg extension are three movements of the same pattern; two in one session is enough. Leg raises do not train the glutes: they work the abs and the hip flexors. And the chest does not "separate" with an exercise: the line you see is anatomy and fat.

The triceps has three heads and all of them cross the elbow; the long head also crosses the shoulder. There is no exercise that excludes one head, only movements that lengthen it more.

## What does not change with the goal

Warm-up, full range, progression and rest, every session. A six-exercise plan with those four elements beats a long list with no order and no way to measure progress from week to week.

For the chest, the guide to [chest anatomy](/en/chest-anatomy/) explains what sits under the breast and the guide to [chest exercises](/en/chest-exercises/) orders the work. The [chest exercise guide](/en/firm-breasts-exercise/) sets out the limits of that area.
