Cutting diets rarely fail for lack of willpower. They fail because the deficit gets too big too soon, protein falls short or the plan does not distinguish week one from week ten. This template separates the phases and writes the numbers down so that adjusting is a decision, not a guess.

## What makes a cutting diet work

Three conditions hold any cutting plan together: a deficit of 300 to 500 calories a day, protein between 1.8 and 2.5 grams per kilo and fat above 0.6 grams per kilo. Carbohydrate is what moves to make the total fit.

The guide on the [calorie deficit](/en/calorie-deficit-for-weight-loss/) covers the maths, and the one on [how to build a diet](/en/how-to-build-a-diet/) orders the whole process before you touch the menus. Eating five times a day does not burn extra calories on its own; spreading food out helps you reach the total, and that does matter.

## How the 12 weeks are split

The template is written for a reader around 80 kg. Heavier readers add 10 percent to the calories; lighter readers take the same proportion off.

The phase 1 arithmetic: 160 grams of protein at 4 calories per gram is 640, 320 grams of carbohydrate at 4 is 1,280 and 60 grams of fat at 9 is 540. The total is 2,460. Phase 2 lifts protein to 200 and drops carbohydrate to 160: 800 plus 640 plus 540, or 1,980. Phase 3 is the [maximum definition diet](/en/maximum-definition-diet/), with 240 grams of protein and 100 of carbohydrate for 1,900 calories.

Foods swap for equivalents: 100 grams of dry rice is about 350 calories and 78 grams of carbohydrate; 150 grams of raw chicken, about 250 calories and 33 grams of protein. Vegetables are not weighed precisely, but half the plate at lunch and dinner is a good target.

## Cutting carbs without breaking the plan

The cut happens in two steps, one every four weeks, and stops there. Going faster leaves training without fuel and protein without room to hold the muscle.

Carbohydrate comes off the meals furthest from training first: the afternoon and the dinner. The meals before and after training keep their share because they carry the session. Fibre from vegetables and wholemeal bread stays, and water stays steady: there is nothing to "flush out" by drinking more than usual.

## Adjusting weeks 9 to 12

The third phase only starts if the first two have genuinely stalled, with average weight and waist flat for two or three weeks. It is a 1,900-calorie day, aggressive and temporary, not a starting point.

Below 1,400 calories a day for a man or 1,200 for a woman, professional supervision is needed. The guide on [muscle definition](/en/muscle-definition/) explains why a small deficit preserves strength better, and the one on [fat burning](/en/fat-burning/) covers cardio and rest.

## When to stop and how to come out

Twelve weeks is the sensible ceiling for this template. After that, go back to maintenance for two to four weeks at phase 1 calories or a little more, then reassess. Returning straight to old habits recovers part of what was lost; moving into maintenance keeps it.

[Weight rebound](/en/weight-rebound-after-dieting/) has more to do with returning to old habits than with a broken metabolism.
