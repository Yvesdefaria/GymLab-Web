## What is FiveStars Fitness and what courses does it offer?

On its personal trainer course, students can validate their training under the applicable regulatory framework and apply for the competencies accreditation process to obtain the Spanish certificate of profesionalidad. That allows them to work legally in the Spanish autonomous communities that require it and to earn certificates such as **EQF3** (Fitness Instructor) and **EQF4** (Personal Trainer), with European recognition.

FiveStars Fitness's affiliation with EHFA in 2014, after an audit process, makes that accreditation possible. The school presents it as significant recognition.

## Live online training and teachers

FiveStars Fitness describes its programmes as unconventional. According to the company, its instructor team includes Olympic medallists, world champions and researchers recognised in the sector.

It also presents itself as a pioneer in sports training through live online classes, a method that lets students interact and take part with their teachers.

The programmes align with current labour market demands and include digital marketing coaching and interpersonal skills for comprehensive training.

## Interviews with sports legends

The website has an interview section called "legacy of legends", with figures such as **Vicente del Bosque**, **Rafael Vera** and **Diego Forlán**, among many others linked to some of the most prominent congresses in the field.

The aim of these interviews is to inspire, educate and motivate fitness professionals and fans, with a view of the experiences and perspectives of influential figures. These are their features:

- Include elite athletes, successful coaches, well-known nutritionists, outstanding physiotherapists and other experts in the field.
- Give access to the experience of people who have reached the top of their fields.
- Offer perspectives that inspire anyone who wants to advance their career or improve their fitness.
- Share practical advice, training strategies, nutrition approaches and motivation techniques the guests have used throughout their careers.
- Personal overcoming stories and the achievements of the interviewees serve as a source of motivation.
- Cover a wide range of topics, from training and nutrition strategies to career advice and personal development.

Together, the section opens a window onto the stories and knowledge of influential figures in fitness and sport, and offers inspiration and guidance to anyone looking to improve their performance or broaden their knowledge.

## Fitness congresses: what they offer

FiveStars Fitness presents its congresses as events valued and respected in the sector. They offer the chance to learn from experts, interact with colleagues and stay up to date with the latest sports trends. Its distinguishing features are:

- Cover a wide range of fitness and health topics, such as new training trends, nutrition and much more.
- Often feature renowned coaches, elite athletes, expert nutritionists, sports psychologists and other influential professionals.
- May include discussion panels where several experts debate important topics and answer audience questions.
- Are a good opportunity to build contacts in the fitness industry and network among attendees.
- Taking part keeps professionals up to date on the latest research and advances in the sector.
- Are a source of inspiration and motivation to reach personal goals and objectives.

The school presents congresses as one of the strengths of its training site, because they bring learning, collaboration and professional growth.

## How to certify EQF3 and EQF4

To obtain the EQF3 Fitness Instructor certificate and the EQF4 Personal Trainer certificate, you must validate the results at EHFA once training at FiveStars Fitness is complete. The school's team then issues both certificates in the student's name.

Both recognitions are well established in Europe and also give access to the European Register of Exercise Professionals (EREPS).

EQF3 Fitness Instructor and EQF4 Personal Trainer training exceeds **300 hours**.
