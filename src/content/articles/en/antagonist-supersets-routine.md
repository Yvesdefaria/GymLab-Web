A normal superset chains two exercises for the same muscle. The antagonist version alternates two opposite patterns: press with row, curl with extension, leg extension with leg curl. The general theory is in [supersets](/en/supersets/); this page keeps what changes when the pair is opposite.

The advantage is not only the clock. When one muscle works, its antagonist relaxes; by alternating, the second exercise finds the opposite side fresh and usually moves more weight or more reps than in a normal set. That is the reason to pair press with row and not two presses in a row.

The real rest is short: 0 to 15 seconds between the two exercises in the pair and 60 to 90 seconds after closing it. The original version prescribed 30 to 60 seconds between exercises; that is a modified superset, not a superset.

The goal is time. By alternating, the resting muscle rests while the opposite one works, and the session fits in 30-45 minutes. The [volume routines](/en/volume-routines/) page places this plan among the others in the block.

Train 3 to 5 days a week. A Monday, Wednesday and Friday split leaves a day between sessions; Monday, Tuesday, rest and Thursday repeats the cycle. Five days only with good recovery.

## Day 1: chest, back and shoulders

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bench press / cable row | 3 | 5-6 / 8-10 |
| Incline press / barbell row | 3 | 5-6 / 8-10 |
| Machine flyes / machine rear delt flyes | 3 | 6-8 / 10-12 |
| Military press / lat pulldowns | 3 | 5-6 / 8-10 |
| Cable front raise / straight-arm pulldowns | 3 | 6-8 / 10-12 |

The first exercise in each pair carries fewer reps than the second: 5-6 against 8-10 on the main lifts, and 6-8 against 10-12 on accessories. If you want to prioritise chest, start with the row and leave the press for second place: the muscle you care about most goes second, when the antagonist is already activated.

## Day 2: biceps and triceps

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parallel bar dips / incline dumbbell curl | 3 | 6-8 / 10-12 |
| Cable pushdown / standing barbell curl | 3 | 5-6 / 8-10 |
| French press / Scott curl | 3 | 5-6 / 8-10 |

Abs and lower back are added at the end, but not as an antagonist pair: back extension and 90-degree leg crunches form a compound set of 3 sets of 8-10 and 12-15. Good mornings are left out: they are not the opposite of a crunch.

## Day 3: legs

| Exercise | Sets | Reps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leg extension / lying leg curl | 3 | 6-8 / 8-10 |
| Squat / Romanian deadlift | 3 | 6-8 / 8-10 |
| Standing calf raise / seated calf raise | 3 | 8-10 / 12-15 |

Squat and Romanian deadlift are not antagonists: both extend the hip and the knee. They stay as a compound set because the pair works, but the page says what it is. The true antagonist pair is leg extension with leg curl. The two calf raises are also a compound set: the same muscle in two positions.

Lunges go separately, in strict sets of 3 to 4 of 10 to 12 reps. They are the exercise with no clear opposite, and they do not need a partner.

## Rest and order

0 to 15 seconds inside the pair. 60 to 90 seconds after it. If you need more than 90 seconds to recover, the weight is too high or the pair is not the right one. If a pair still leaves you out of breath, swap it for one that uses less muscle. If time is short, the pair also warms up the second exercise without an extra set.

Fewer reps on the first exercise, more on the second. Do not reach failure on the first: it would lower the strength of the second. On the second you can stop one rep short.

Alternate the order of the pair every week. Run a block for 6 to 8 weeks, then return to normal sets for another six or eight.

If the gym is full, build the pair from two nearby stations or switch the exercises to dumbbells. What matters is alternating opposite patterns, not the specific machines.

The [rest between sets](/en/rest-between-sets/) page has the ranges by goal; [high-intensity training](/en/high-intensity-training/) has the other techniques; [training volume](/en/training-volume/) explains how to count sets. For a back pair at the end of the session, the [back superset](/en/back-superset-finisher/) is a good finisher.
