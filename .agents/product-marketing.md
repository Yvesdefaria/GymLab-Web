# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v2

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** A strength-training app that logs every set and shows your progress in graphs.

**What it does:** GymLab helps lifters plan a routine, log each set while training, and see progress by exercise, muscle group, and period. It includes a catalog of 873 photographed exercises, 9 calculators, progress photos, step tracking, and achievements. Data stays on the device.

**Product category:** Fitness / workout tracking app.

**Product type:** Consumer mobile app (Android first; iOS planned).

**Business model:** Pre-launch; pricing and monetization undecided. Do not make price claims.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** N/A (consumer).

**Primary use case:** Know whether training is working, set by set and week by week.

**Jobs to be done:**

## Personas

N/A (B2C). Core user: Spanish-speaking lifter, beginner to intermediate, trains 3–5 times a week, currently tracks in a notes app, a paper notebook, or memory.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Training without a record means guessing whether the plan works.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Notes apps and paper: no structure, no charts, no exercise catalog.
- Generic trackers: account walls, subscriptions, and ads before the first set.
- Personal coaches: effective but expensive and not always available.

**What it costs them:** Repeated guesswork on weights, stalled lifts noticed late, motivation drops when progress is invisible.

**Emotional tension:** Doubt ("am I even progressing?"), frustration with apps that demand a sign-up, fear of wasting training months.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Strong, Hevy, Fitbod — solid trackers, but account-first, subscription-gated features, and generic Spanish exercise content.

**Secondary:** Notes apps, spreadsheets, paper notebooks — free and familiar, but no structure, charts, or catalog.

**Indirect:** Personal trainers and printed routines — high quality, high cost, not always available.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- 873 exercises with photos, filterable by muscle, equipment, and category.
- 9 calculators in the same app (BMI, calories/macros, 1RM, body fat, water, measurements).
- 68 routine templates plus a routine builder.
- Progress graphs by exercise, muscle group, and period.
- Works offline; no account required to train.

**How we do it differently:** Everything lives on the device; the app stays useful in a basement gym with no signal.

**Why that's better:** Faster logging, no sign-up friction, training history that stays yours.

**Why customers choose us:** Spanish-first, complete toolkit, no account wall.

**Anti-persona:** Users looking for social feeds, coaching programs, or cloud collaboration.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Notes apps and memory lose detail; generic apps demand accounts.

**Pull:** One place for routine, log, catalog, calculators, and charts.

**Habit:** The notebook is familiar and fast.

**Anxiety:** Migrating history; another abandoned app; privacy.
